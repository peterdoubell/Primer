Complete published source views are independent teaching examples, not the current patient, native acquisition arrays, calibrated measurements, histologically segmented wall layers or registered 3D geometry. Source arrows, scales, ADC/SUV values, tumour measurements and stage labels remain author annotations. MRI does not establish microscopic LVSI, molecular class, histologic grade, nodal micro/macrometastasis size or a complete FIGO 2023 stage. No cross-case fitting or every-structure clinical approval is supplied.
