# Source MRI comparison

Bazin, Forstmann, Alkemade, Miletic and Isherwood. [AHEAD qMRI templates v3](https://doi.org/10.21942/uva.19646364.v3); [MASSP 2.0 lifespan atlas v2](https://doi.org/10.21942/uva.27291579.v2). Both sources CC BY 4.0.

Changes: native sections and regional crops, display-only grayscale windows, and direct joint-model triangle sections. Group templates from 105 adults and model atlas from 97 adults; not individual clinical MRI. Source voxels and labels unchanged. Anatomy and derived subvoxel interfaces remain unapproved. Full acquisition/geometry review is in docs/brain-ahead-qmri-source-review.md and docs/brain-massp-source-review.md.
