This reader includes every original PNG in the eight methods-defined categories. Three additional archive categories remain excluded because their separate anatomical definitions and panel roles are unverified. A model prediction is not ground truth, and the study documents both prediction and annotation errors. Pixel comparisons do not establish clinical completeness, current-patient findings, normality or quantitative wall thickness.

Source: Baskaran and colleagues, PLOS One study and colour definitions. Official Zenodo/Dryad data mirror, DOI 10.5061/dryad.9s4mw6mc9, CC0 1.0. The archive matches the original publisher's MD5 and Dryad SHA-256. Original PNG bytes and source category names are retained.
