Equations and assumptions

The first ideal polariser transmits half the intensity of an unpolarised beam. Each later filter multiplies the incoming intensity by cos² Δθ, where Δθ is the angle between its axis and the incoming polarisation. The transmitted field is polarised along the filter axis whenever its intensity is positive.

Each filter is ideal and the light travels along the same axis. The model ignores reflection, finite extinction ratio, wavelength dependence and material losses apart from the rejected polarisation component.
