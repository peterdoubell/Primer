Equations and assumptions

A 1 kg mass is attached to a linear spring with stiffness 4π² N/m. The undamped natural frequency is 1 Hz. A sinusoidal force with amplitude 1 N drives the mass against linear viscous damping.

The equation is mẍ + bẋ + kx = F₀ cos(ωt). The damping ratio is ζ = b / (2√(mk)).

Steady-state displacement is A cos(ωt − δ), where A = F₀ / √((k − mω²)² + (bω)²) and δ = atan2(bω, k − mω²).
