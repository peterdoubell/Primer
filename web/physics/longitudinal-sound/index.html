Sound and moving air

Move the time control to follow a sound wave. Each dot tracks a small parcel of air. Watch the gold parcel move back and forth while the coloured pressure pattern travels through the medium.

Model and units

This ideal plane wave uses dimensionless length units, sound speed 1 and bulk modulus 1. Its period equals its wavelength. Displacement stays within 1% of a wavelength, using the small-amplitude approximation. Dots represent parcels of the medium; molecular thermal motion and damping are omitted.

Displacement ξ = A cos(kx − kt), velocity = kA sin(kx − kt), and pressure perturbation = kA sin(kx − kt), with k = 2π/λ. Pressure follows −∂ξ/∂x in these units.
