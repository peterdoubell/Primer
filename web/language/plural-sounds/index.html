Choose the English plural sound

Start with the last sound of a regular noun. Follow the first applicable rule to predict the sound of its plural suffix.

  1. After a sibilant such as [s], [z], [ʃ], [ʒ], [tʃ] or [dʒ], add [ɪz]. Some speakers use [əz].
  2. After another voiceless sound, add [s].
  3. After another voiced sound, including a vowel, add [z].
