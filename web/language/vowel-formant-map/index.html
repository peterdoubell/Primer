How to read these measurements

Each row is one vowel token lasting at least 30 ms. F1 and F2 are the source’s estimated median formant frequencies for that token. The source vowel codes are case-sensitive, so I and i remain distinct.

Formants are vocal-tract resonances. Lower F1 often accompanies a higher tongue position, and higher F2 often accompanies a more fronted vowel. Speaker anatomy, rounding and speech context also affect these frequencies. The plot uses unnormalised Hz. Formant and articulation background.

Each L1 group contains ten speakers. English and Spanish speakers reuse anonymous identifiers such as s1; the L1 and speaker identifier together identify a person here. These summaries describe the selected sample.
