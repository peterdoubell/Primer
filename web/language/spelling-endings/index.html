Explore a word

  1. Ends in silent e? Drop the e.
  2. Ends in a consonant and y? Change y to i before -ed. Keep it before -ing.
  3. Has one syllable and ends in a short vowel written with one vowel letter, followed by one consonant letter? Double the consonant, except w, x and y.
  4. Otherwise, join the two parts for the regular examples below.
