    Model source and interpretation

    These are atlas objects from the Z-Anatomy Blender scene. The duct outlines show the exterior of the modelled tubes. The contours follow the triangles cut by the selected plane. They carry no grayscale, nodal, flow, stone or tumour findings. Left and right use the atlas authors’ paired objects and share underlying geometry. In the source scene, the left gland bodies occupy positive X and the right gland bodies negative X. Its coordinates are separate from those of ultrasound participants and clinical patients.
