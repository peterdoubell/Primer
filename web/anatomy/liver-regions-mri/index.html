Explore the eight labelled regions in one released MRI from the TotalSegmentator liver-segment dataset. The regions are functional annotations within a continuous liver. The 3D surfaces follow the source label grid.

Source definitions and limitations

The catalogue describes nine segments, while its dataset configuration defines background and eight foreground labels. The viewer follows the actual label definitions.
