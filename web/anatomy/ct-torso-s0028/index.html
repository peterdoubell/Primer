Case, sampling and source limits

The source identifies s0028 as a 59-year-old female neck–chest–abdomen–pelvis CT in the training set. Its stored grid is 299 × 299 × 394 with 1.5 mm spacing. I, J and K are source indices. The NIfTI aligned sform gives increasing I, J and K toward right, anterior and superior. Radiological view places source right on the left in axial and coronal planes and source anterior on the left in sagittal planes. The header omits a physical-unit flag; measurements use the documented 1.5 mm sampling. Original DICOM orientation and HU calibration have not been independently verified.

The 117 supplied masks include 109 with labelled cells and eight empty masks. Twenty masks reach an edge of the acquired grid. Surfaces follow the 0.5 mask interface without capping, smoothing or removing components. A mask describes the source’s labelled extent. It does not establish lesion absence, reportable injury, acquisition phase, or tumour response.
