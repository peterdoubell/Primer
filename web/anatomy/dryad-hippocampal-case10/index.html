Hippocampal MRI and two manual annotations

Clark and Maguire, Dryad case 10: a participant from a healthy-adult research cohort. The annotations belong to the prepared, co-registered mean T2 image. Raw repetitions are available without overlays because their anatomical registration to that mean has not been verified. Their differences are not a diagnosis or an estimate of anatomical truth. This partial-volume study does not cover every epilepsy reporting structure.

How to interpret this source

The prepared mean has its original nonunit intensity scale. The source files named 0009 and 0010 are byte-identical. Combined CA2/3 and pre/parasubiculum regions remain combined; the publication describes the dentate-gyrus protocol as DG/CA4. The source “Cyst” category occurs only in the Reliability annotation and is not independently diagnosed here. Source grid spacing is approximately 0.52 × 0.52 × 0.5 mm; this is not a measurement of effective imaging resolution. One participant does not establish a normal range, a disease threshold, or complete clinical coverage.
