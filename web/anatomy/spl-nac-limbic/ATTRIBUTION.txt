Modified transport and derived visualization version by Primer, 2026-10-16. Exact categorical label-boundary meshes are newly derived from every exposed original label voxel-cell face; they are not original publisher VTK files. No components, cavities, or faces are deleted. The original publisher VTK models are separately retained for comparison.
Conversion: native MRI/label sample bytes are unchanged; original NRRD gzip bodies and headers permit exact source-file reconstruction. VTK coordinate/normal/strip bits are retained; triangle strips are expanded without deleting any implied triangle. Display colors follow the original lookup table. No MRI resampling, coordinate fitting, smoothing, capping, merging or anatomical repair is applied.
This single source case and its processed anatomical labels/surfaces do not establish normal populations, pathology, current-patient registration, complete fine anatomy or clinical approval.
