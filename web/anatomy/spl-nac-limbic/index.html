Limbic anatomy in the SPL brain atlas

Compare the original T1 and T2 MRI with twelve selected source labels and their 3D boundaries. You can also inspect the atlas producer’s original processed surfaces. These data describe one atlas subject, not a patient-specific reconstruction or a diagnostic test.

The label map has a different grid from the MRI. Overlays use the source physical coordinates, with nearest-label sampling at each native MRI sample. The default 3D surfaces follow every exposed label-voxel face. The producer’s processed surfaces are a separate comparison and can differ substantially, especially at the fornices. Separate hippocampal CA fields and subiculum are not supplied here.

Source, coordinate conventions and reuse

This is the SPL-PNL/NAC atlas, published through the Open Anatomy Project. Its native MRI and label headers specify LPS coordinates; this viewer displays them in RAS using the explicit sign conversion. The original Slicer 4.4 model vertices already use RAS and receive no additional sign conversion.

The original data are research material. They do not establish normal ranges, complete reporting anatomy, clinical accuracy or suitability for a particular patient. The original licensing terms and notices accompany this atlas reference.
