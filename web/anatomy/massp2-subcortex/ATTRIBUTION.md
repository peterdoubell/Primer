# MASSP 2.0 source reference

Bazin, Forstmann, Alkemade and Miletic. [MASSP 2.0 lifespan atlas v2](https://doi.org/10.21942/uva.27291579.v2), [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).

Changes: joint tetrahedral interfaces from unchanged label voxels, float32 position storage, generated lighting normals and gzip transport. Models remain anatomically unapproved; no independent registration to BodyParts3D. Full acquisition, geometric validation and interpolation limits are in docs/brain-massp-source-review.md.
