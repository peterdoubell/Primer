Original case, provenance and limits

This is MedSeg’s version 2 image 1 paired with mask 1, not the original Decathlon case number. The publisher’s collection does not provide that original case ID, demographics, diagnosis or acquisition phase. These labels do not establish a lesion observation, LI-RADS category, current-patient registration or clinical approval. Segments 4A and 4B are inspected separately; neither alone completes the whole of segment IV.

The encoded CT and labels restore the original float32 sample bytes exactly. No resampling, intensity clipping, registration or interpolation changes the source arrays. Source volumes use label-cell count × native voxel volume; they describe this annotation, not independently measured functional liver territories. Meshes approximate the sampled mask boundary, particularly across the original 5 mm spacing.
