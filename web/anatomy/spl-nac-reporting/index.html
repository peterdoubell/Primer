Source, coordinate conventions and reuse

This is the SPL-PNL/NAC atlas, published through the Open Anatomy Project. Its native MRI and label headers specify LPS coordinates; this viewer displays them in RAS using the explicit sign conversion. These cell boundaries derive directly from the original categorical grid in RAS. The earlier twelve-structure viewer retains a separate publisher-surface comparison.

The original data are research material. They do not establish normal ranges, complete reporting anatomy, clinical accuracy or suitability for a particular patient. The original licensing terms and notices accompany this atlas reference.
