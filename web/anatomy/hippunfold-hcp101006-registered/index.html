Source methods, class boundaries and limitations

The published methods describe bias correction, affine atlas registration, coronal-oblique cropping and sagittal left mirroring. The dataset description says left labels were mirrored. This reference preserves the provided arrays and affines without an additional mirror, resampling or anatomical fitting. The exact training exporter convention remains unresolved.

The authors produced heavily supervised, semi-automatic research segmentations. The medial temporal neocortex class is combined entorhinal/parahippocampal boundary context. The pial class is a method boundary, not whole meninges. HATA is a transition area, not the whole amygdala. Indusium griseum supplies posterior boundary context, not complete fornices or mammillary bodies. The dentate gyrus class represents a granule-layer/endpoint label, not whole DG, hilus or CA4. A cyst label alone establishes neither a diagnosis nor benignity.

CA1–CA4 and subiculum are not separately labelled. Surfaces follow exact categorical voxel-cell boundaries, including disconnected components and contacts; they are not smoothed biological tissue surfaces. Every exposed voxel face is retained.

This selected source does not establish normality, hippocampal sclerosis, FLAIR abnormalities, native volume or asymmetry, clinical MTA scores, complete fornices/mammillary bodies, whole-brain cortex, lesions or dual pathology. It is a partial anatomy reference; complete epilepsy and dementia reporting requires additional images and clinical interpretation.

In the 2D view, left-to-right pixels follow increasing first displayed index; bottom-to-top follows increasing second index. View file identities, original affines and derivation metadata.
