# Source-native lentiform MRI inspection

Bazin, Forstmann, Alkemade and Miletic. [MASSP 2.0 lifespan atlas v2](https://doi.org/10.21942/uva.27291579.v2). Bazin, Forstmann, Alkemade, Miletić and Isherwood. [AHEAD quantitative MRI templates v3](https://doi.org/10.21942/uva.19646364.v3). Both pinned datasets: [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).

Adaptations: an unresampled native regional crop; reversible bit-preserving compression; display-only grayscale windows, crosshairs and explicit labels; plane intersections of existing complete joint source meshes. No stored source intensities, probabilities, labels, coordinates or meshes are modified. Full limits and original source hashes are in manifest.json. These are adult population references, not independently validated patient boundaries.
