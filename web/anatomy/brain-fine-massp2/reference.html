    Scope, source context and reuse rights

    The regional crop contains every original best-label voxel of bilateral putamen, external pallidal segment (GPe) and internal pallidal segment (GPi), plus a 2 mm context margin. Adjacent structures and nonzero probability tails can continue outside this region. Source label readout preserves every original neighbour ID in the crop.

    Source population probabilities describe region membership across 97 atlas subjects. MRI IQR describes intersubject intensity spread across 105 template subjects. These are distinct quantities; neither measures an individual patient's diagnostic certainty. Quantitative source units and clinical calibration remain unverified.

    The complete joint meshes preserve every original face and their original MNI2009b RAS coordinates. Their tetrahedral subvoxel interpolation adds no measured resolution. Fine medullary laminae, capsule limbs, caudate segments, thalamic nuclei, pathology and developmental coverage remain unresolved. This reference grants no clinical anatomical approval or complete reporting coverage.
