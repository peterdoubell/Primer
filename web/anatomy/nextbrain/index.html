Source and interpretation

The 98-region manual MRI reference is separate from the 333-region probabilistic histology atlas. Manual reference construction included sparse tracing, interpolation and correction; cortical subdivision used FreeSurfer. Broad source labels may exclude separately labelled subregions. Fibre regions do not represent tractography.

Outside the annotation crop, zero means no annotation supplied. Codes 99–101 are absent. The supplied file is named T2w, while its cited acquisition used FLASH; the exact derivative contrast lineage is unresolved. The reader therefore uses “Supplied ex vivo MRI”. No quantitative T2 or 100 µm derivative claim is made.

Models retain all labelled source cells, including disconnected islands and nonmanifold contacts. Boundaries are not smoothed or anatomically repaired.
