Primer derives lossless plane packs and exact categorical-cell meshes, preserving all supplied source sample bits and source components. No smoothing, anatomical fitting, patient registration or clinical approval is implied. Source WMn-like contrast is synthesized.

Additional Primer derivations: exact meshes of all 53 supplied structure masks, 9 supplied tissue masks and the supplied intracranial mask; 16 explicitly listed overlapping fine-label compositions; multiscale MRI overlays, source-cell outlines and source-frame size comparison. All source components and cavities are retained. Coarse header units remain unspecified; millimetres follow exact voxel correspondence to the fine source grid. Source masks and compositions do not establish completeness of reporting anatomy.
