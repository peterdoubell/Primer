Source objects

Objects preserve their source coordinates and remain independently selectable. Repeated labels are separate source representations. The source labels include gallbladder, common hepatic duct, cystic duct, right and left hepatic ducts and selected tributaries. A published comparison of anatomical resources flagged a BodyParts3D common hepatic duct segmentation error. The label here identifies the source object. Its anatomical boundary remains under review. A separately labelled common bile duct, continuous lumina, wall layers and clinical variants remain outside this collection.
