Source objects

Objects preserve their source coordinates and remain independently selectable. Repeated labels are separate source representations. The source labels include gallbladder, common hepatic duct, cystic duct, right and left hepatic ducts and selected tributaries. The source labels all of object FJ3079 common hepatic duct and has no common bile duct object. The object runs from the hepatic duct confluence to about 60 mm below the cystic duct, so most of it is common bile duct. A published comparison of anatomical resources flagged this segmentation. Here the object is shown in two parts, divided where the source cystic duct meets it, to within about 3 mm. Every source triangle is in one part, unchanged. The division was placed by measurement and no radiologist has reviewed it. Continuous lumina, wall layers, the intrapancreatic and intramural segments and clinical variants remain outside this collection.
