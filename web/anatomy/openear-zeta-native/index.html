Source and limits

The original meshes and masks are separate author-derived representations in supplied source coordinates. Their numerical correspondence does not establish clinical surface accuracy, every small wall or joint, complete neural branches, tissue thickness or patient registration. “Sinus Dura” does not separately resolve venous lumen, vessel wall and dura.

Fixation, staining, dehydration and epoxy embedding change tissue appearance and morphology. A preparation opening was drilled into the superior semicircular canal; this is not a clinical dehiscence case. The reconstructed photographic colour has unresolved tissue support and is not applied to the meshes. The images provide no hearing, neural-function or vascular-flow measurement.

Outside the cropped 13-channel segmentation grid, no annotation was supplied for those channels. The separate full Bone mask has its own complete source-volume extent. A zero or a missing annotation is not proof of absent tissue. The registered image grid is not a claim of effective acquisition resolution.
