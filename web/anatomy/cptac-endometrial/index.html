MRI display

The sequences share a source study and coordinate frame. Their acquisitions can differ because of motion and tissue deformation. T2 navigation is independent, and the lesion outlines remain attached to their original post-contrast sections.

The post-contrast images have 4.6 mm slice thickness and approximately 2.3 mm between slice centres. The T2 images have 4 mm thickness and approximately 4.5 mm centre spacing. The 3D display retains individual contour loops at their original positions.

This source labels the lesion. Separate endometrium, myometrium and junctional-zone boundaries require additional evidence. Source contours alone do not establish histological invasion depth or stage.

Images from the National Cancer Institute Clinical Proteomic Tumor Analysis Consortium. Lesion annotations by Rozenfeld and Jordan (2023).
