Finer triangles capture more surface relief. For each closed model, surface area is the sum of the triangle areas. Volume is the sum of the signed tetrahedral volumes formed by the origin and each oriented face.

Source geometry by the OSIRIS-REx Altimetry Working Group, archived in NASA’s Planetary Data System. The Canadian Space Agency contributed the laser altimeter. The three v21a meshes use the same body-fixed frame. Their differences measure how these representations differ; the spacecraft photograph provides a separate view.
