Conditioning keeps only outcomes consistent with the information. If that information cannot occur, the conditional probability is undefined. Drawing twice without replacement also requires at least two marbles.

For the quiz’s three red and two blue marbles without replacement, both red has probability (3/5) × (2/4) = 3/10. With replacement it is (3/5) × (3/5) = 9/25.
