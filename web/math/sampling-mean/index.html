Spread of observations and means

Each trial returns 1 for success and 0 for failure. Assume independent trials with the same known probability p. Compare one observation with the average of n observations.

Two different measures of spread
QuantityStandard deviation

The individual population spread stays fixed when n changes. The mean’s standard deviation is its standard error, √(p(1−p)/n), in this specified model. Exact fixed-band probabilities can change in steps as the possible means move.
