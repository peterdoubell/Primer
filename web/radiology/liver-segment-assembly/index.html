Source and interpretation

MedSeg image 1 and mask 1 share a 512 × 512 × 47 grid with 0.977 × 0.977 × 5 mm sampling. The volume and area measurements use the original mask cells. Segment IVa and IVb have separate publisher labels. The surfaces follow the mask boundary between sampled cells. Section spacing limits fine detail along the superior–inferior axis.

Section views use a display window of −125 to 225 stored CT units. The original acquisition phase and independent Hounsfield-unit calibration are unknown. These teaching measurements describe a single source case.
