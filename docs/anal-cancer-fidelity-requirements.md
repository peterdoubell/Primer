# Anal cancer: explicit structure and source requirements

The effective `ra.mri-anal-cancer` reporting contract now has a draft inventory of 55 groups and 375 separately reportable leaves. Each leaf requires image, schematic and model evidence: 1,125 obligations, with no new verified coverage. Multiple actual lesions, nodes, interfaces or tracts must be instantiated separately. This is a known floor pending specialist review, not a clinical completeness assertion.

The inventory separates the primary boundary and measurement limits; canal, margin and rectal continuity; sphincter layers; paired pelvic-floor and ischioanal regions; patient-specific adjacent organs; individual nodal stations and sides; conditional fistula, treated-bed and supplementary distant-staging findings. Acquisition and measurement correspondence are explicit. Existing three linked MRI examples and a generic pelvic landmark model cannot supply all of these leaves.

The [effective reference](https://radiologyassistant.nl/abdomen/anal-cancer-2/anal) motivates source-specific layer, extent and nodal localisation. The inventory retains dentate-line uncertainty rather than asserting that MRI directly resolves it. Source examples are not automatically licensed, independently anatomically reviewed, or interchangeable with complete patient series.

[NCI PDQ](https://www.cancer.gov/types/anal/hp/anal-treatment-pdq) supplies an explicit AJCC8 reference and names adjacent-organ and nodal categories. Its anal-margin wording needs reconciliation with the primary source and actual histology/epicentre. No universal staging rule is inherited across anal and rectal disease, and no edition is silently assumed current. Sphincter geometry, organ invasion and staging categories remain separate evidence questions.

The [SAR expert survey](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10929685/) supports including fistula and, when relevant, treated-bed response evidence. Its accessible primary search extract was reviewed; the complete PMC fetch encountered a challenge. The [ACR 2025 abstract](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jacr.2025.02.018) supports separating local from systemic staging. The ACR narrative fetch was denied. Neither access limitation is represented as a full guideline review or a clinical approval.

Negative findings require adequate actual source coverage. Normal anatomy cannot satisfy positive tumour/invasion examples, one side cannot satisfy the other, and pelvic MRI cannot establish uncovered systemic status. Size, response, viability and suspected nodal malignancy require appropriate acquisition, comparison and clinical confirmation. No MRI node-size threshold or reconstructed histological boundary is introduced.

Tests bind every group to the current reporting fields, keep all five report/walkthrough stages, reject generic parent coverage, and enforce modality, side and pathology context. The full audit still reports 137 investigations and 106 unreconciled curriculum surfaces, with 85 investigations unexpanded and an unknown total requirement count. Runtime assets, deployment, rights counts and held-model status are unchanged by this draft.

The [full-series source review](anal-series-source-review.md) adds paired nodal subregions named by the public ExACT form, while holding its historical staging choices and controlled MRI data outside runtime reuse. Twenty new groups add 160 leaves. Compartment overlap, inguinal field grouping and presacral vessel terminology remain explicit anatomical review issues. Adjacent-organ and nodal presets retain actual assessment/coverage prompts rather than unverified negative findings.
