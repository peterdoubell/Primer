# Hip FAI reporting correction

Reviewed 26 September 2026. This corrects diagnostic wording and acquisition
conditions; it does not approve the guide or its anatomical references.

The previous pitfall required corresponding labral/cartilage findings before
attributing pain to FAI morphology. That improperly made tissue damage a
diagnostic prerequisite. The [Warwick Agreement abstract in the authors'
institutional repository](https://wrap.warwick.ac.uk/id/eprint/85354/) defines
the diagnostic combination as compatible symptoms, clinical examination signs
and imaging findings. The revised text uses that combination and continues to
distinguish asymptomatic cam/pincer morphology from clinical FAI syndrome.

The alternative-pain checklist also previously instructed assessment of
periarticular soft tissues without its worksheet's MRI condition. It now
distinguishes additional radiographic bone/joint findings from tendon, bursal,
muscle and covered pelvic findings on an acquired MRI. Existing conditional
labrum/cartilage prompts, report sections and imaging scope remain unchanged.
