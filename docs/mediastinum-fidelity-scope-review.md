# Mediastinal CT fidelity scope

The effective `ra.ct-mediastinum` contract now has a draft inventory of 326 localisation/anatomical/finding targets across 48 groups. Each needs an image, schematic and model: 978 representation obligations. All five checklist fields, report-template sections and walkthrough steps are retained, and the complete reporting/templates/walkthrough/reading contract is fingerprinted. No clinical or anatomical approval is granted.

| Report field | Required expansion |
|---|---|
| Compartment | Actual prevascular, visceral and paravertebral region boundaries; acquired thymic, cervical, vascular and host relationships |
| Composition | Positive mass extent, solid/fluid/fat/calcific regions, lesion walls/septa/nodules and acquired enhancement context |
| Local relationships | Specific vessel lumens/walls, airways, esophagus, heart/pericardium and conditional neural/spinal interfaces |
| Nodal map | Individual source-defined stations and boundary landmarks, actual consequential nodes, morphology and measurement planes |
| Other thoracic findings | Acquired lung, pleura, chest wall, spine, neck/upper-abdominal continuity and conditional consequences/treatment alterations |

The [ITMIG CT classification](https://pubs.rsna.org/doi/10.1148/rg.2017160095) was consulted for the modern compartment definitions. These are localisation constructs bounded by actual anatomy, not invented tissue septa. The prevascular region follows the anterior/peripheral pericardial relationship; the visceral/paravertebral boundary uses a reference relative to the anterior vertebral body. Actual acquired levels and displacement still require case-specific interpretation. Extrapericardial pulmonary vessels remain relevant host relationships while being pulmonary structures in that definition.

The effective report uses the modern names, while the linked [older mass article](https://radiologyassistant.nl/chest/mediastinum/masses-differential-diagnosis) describes radiographic anterior/middle/posterior divisions. Its original diagrams and labels must be reconciled before assignment; the inventory does not silently convert these into a modern segmented atlas. This source consultation does not grant figure reproduction rights or prove any source-case diagnosis.

Station identities and boundary-landmark requirements are retained from the existing draft lung-cancer work, using the current [IASLC nodal definitions](https://www.iaslc.org/research-education/publications-resources-guidelines/staging-cards-thoracic-oncology-9th-edition). Their representation evidence is not copied or credited. Acquired lower-cervical, hilar and intrapulmonary stations remain conditional scope, including optional right station 11 subdivisions and side-specific station 3A borders. This descriptive mediastinal investigation does not automatically assign lung-cancer N categories. The [older node-map source](https://radiologyassistant.nl/chest/mediastinum/mediastinum-lymph-node-map) retains its original source-label reconciliation issue.

The draft also retains actual thymic/developmental context, cervical/thyroid continuity, lymphatic/esophageal pathways, specific vessels and cardiac/pericardial recesses, neural roots/foramina/canal interfaces, adjacent lung/pleura/bone, positive mass composition and positive compression/invasion/consequences. Every actual affected site, variant, age condition, intervention and source/case finding still requires instantiation and review. CT visibility limits for neural and soft-tissue boundaries retain explicit complementary MRI scope; no nerve, duct or histological layer is guessed from a generic landmark.

Six separate acquisition/functional evidence obligations address measurement, attenuation/enhancement, vascular continuity/patency, invasion/neural extension, imaging composition versus histology, and developmental/treatment comparisons. Normal geometry cannot satisfy positive mass, necrosis, thrombus or invasion requirements. A converted source image with uncertain phase/intensity lineage cannot supply a calibrated before/after enhancement claim.

Existing acquired node sources remain review candidates. Their numeric labels are not station IDs, and their native sampling, single-plane annotation, metadata identity, topology and diagnostic-context issues remain explicit. No source case is merged or presumed to supply the new 326 targets. All 978 obligations in this new inventory remain missing, and no reader-media or clinical evidence binding is added by this scope change.
