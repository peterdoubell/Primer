# Posteromedial-knee source acquisition

Reviewed 30 September 2026. New source candidates supply semimembranosus expansions, the oblique popliteal ligament and three posterior oblique ligament arms. No current geometry, requirement, evidence approval or app entry was changed by this review.

[Picasso et al.](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s00330-025-11868-8), published 29 July 2025 in the January 2026 *European Radiology* issue, explicitly includes images under CC BY 4.0 unless a contrary credit applies. The complete article and eight-page supplement were inspected; selected captions/pixels carry no restrictive or third-party permission credit. Rights and source fingerprints are preserved in [rights-and-scope.json](msk-picasso-knee-source-review/rights-and-scope.json).

[Exact proposals](msk-picasso-knee-source-review/proposed-entries.json) preserve panel types, narrow candidate IDs, unresolved population/side and pending fidelity review. The next implementable action is to append the unchanged figures with panel-specific scope and attribution, retaining pending anatomical approval. No 3D tissue volume is supplied.

## Local reader integration

The three original composites are now available in the local knee reader, with exact native JPEG bytes and full-resolution enlargement. S1 A and S4 A supply four partial schematic candidates: semimembranosus distal course and tibial relationship, oblique popliteal course, and posterior oblique course. Their dissection and ultrasound panels are retained with separate types and observations. S4 ultrasound and main Figure 4 MRI have no automatic full-structure bindings; the latter keeps its ACL-injured patient context and contralateral-US normality distinct.

Catalog validation accepts the actual uppercase source panel letters and explicitly typed ancillary ultrasound panels. The source/role tests prohibit dissection or schematic credit as MRI and retain partial extent. Desktop/mobile source-image, full-resolution, caption and layout checks are in `reader-browser-checks.json`. This is local work following the last deployment, not evidence of clinical approval or complete model coverage.
