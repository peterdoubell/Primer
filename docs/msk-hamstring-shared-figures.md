# Explicit hip-to-hamstring figure selection

27 September 2026. Two already preserved figures are relevant to the hamstring
investigation despite being curated originally under Hip/FAI:

- `open-hip-proximal-hamstring-schematic-balius-fig2`: the complete Balius
  expanded hamstring drawing labels BFlh, BFsh, ST and SM. These provide partial
  schematic muscle-belly candidates; no fascicles, junctions or footprint
  dimensions are inferred from the schematic separation.
- `open-hip-tendon-boutin-fig6-5`: the complete Boutin MRI comparison retains
  normal local tendon labels in a and pathological injury in b. It is shared as
  relevant image context only; no new hamstring tendon-substructure binding is
  asserted from this selection.

This explicit selection crosses the catalogue's Hip and Muscle topic groups,
not an anatomical mismatch. It does not share the remaining FAI figures,
rename source IDs, re-encode pixels, or waive clinical fidelity checks. Existing
source captions, licences, limitations and provenance are retained. It is a
curation decision, not clinical approval. Original rights and preservation
reviews are in `msk-hip-image-progress.md` and `msk-boutin-hip-source-review.md`.
