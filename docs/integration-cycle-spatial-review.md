# Integration and Carnot 3D fidelity review

## Bounded representation

The two new families replace generic contextual geometry for `math.5.measure` and `phys.3.thermo` with coordinates derived from the actual lesson examples. `shrinking-support-subgraph` displays the dimensionless unit-depth product-measure subgraph of the shrinking-support sequence; `carnot-state-path` displays the ideal working gas in calibrated pressure–volume–temperature coordinates. These are quantitative mathematical models, not photorealistic objects, physical apparatus, experimental data or proof that either entire lesson is complete.

The existing 2D measure and cycle activities supply separate limit/DCT arguments, norm comparisons, first-law budgets, reservoir entropy and the refrigerator room boundary. Their broader requirements must not be attributed to these 3D shapes alone.

## Independent geometry and mathematics

For `h_n=A n^α 1_(0,1/n)`, the controls expose `n=2^q`, q=0…16, A=0…8 in quarters, and α=0,1/2,1. Every box coordinate uses the real dimensionless width `1/n`, height `A n^α` and depth one. Its product-measure volume is `A n^(α−1)`. This is distinct from `∫h_n² dx=A² n^(2α−1)`: at n=4,A=2,α=1 the volume is two and the squared L² norm is sixteen. Six correctly oriented faces, eight unique vertices, all six boundary planes, bounding extents and signed triangulated volume are checked independently of the readout metadata. No minimum thickness is inserted for a thin interval. At A=0 there are no filled solid faces; the interval reference line has zero volume.

The faces draw the closure for inspection. Function values exclude x=0 and x=1/n; drawing those endcaps does not include them in the function's nonzero set. Their zero product measure leaves the volume unchanged. Marker radius is a display aid and is excluded from that volume. Rational membership is tested using integer cross-products for zero, quarter, right-boundary and interior probes. Zero and quarter are fixed points; the right-boundary and interior choices move with n and cannot establish a pointwise-limit claim. Very thin or tall solids may become visually unresolved while their world coordinates and analytic readout remain meaningful. The [MIT measure/integration notes](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/18-125-measure-and-integration-fall-2003/1473498db368e7a3194855b6935cc616_18125_lec5.pdf) and [Lᵖ definitions](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/18-125-measure-and-integration-fall-2003/0c85400465928cf42a19e5b87bdc349c_18125_lec15.pdf) supply the measure and norm conventions.

For the Carnot model, x=V/0.005 m³, y=p/200000 Pa and z=T/1000 K. The working substance is 0.1 mol of a hypothetical monatomic ideal gas, with `R=8.31446261815324`, `C_v=3R/2`, `γ=5/3`, and volume at A fixed to 0.001 m³. The controls keep `T_h>T_c>0`, with hot temperatures 400…1200 K, cold temperatures 100…350 K and hot-isotherm gas heat magnitude 200…500 J. These selections all fit the underlying 2D model without clipping; their isothermal expansion ratio stays below eight.

The oracle derives corners from `V_B/V_A=exp(Q_h/(nRT_h))` and `V_C/V_B=V_D/V_A=(T_h/T_c)^(1/(γ−1))`. It independently evaluates p(V)=nRT/V on isotherms and `p(V)=p_start(V_start/V)^γ` on reversible adiabats. Work is separately checked against quadrature of p(V), and all states satisfy `ΔU=Q−W_by`, the ideal-gas equation and ideal-gas entropy differences. The forward topology is A→B→C→D→A; reversing it gives A→D→C→B→A, with signed work and heat reversed. Net gas energy/entropy changes vanish for a completed cycle.

Each of four curved legs contains 121 points spaced uniformly in log volume, joined by straight display segments. Those joins are a finite visualization, not an exact polygonal physical path. The selected state is analytically evaluated at its chosen log-volume position rather than interpolated from neighboring display points. The 3D model has no bypass leak; leak and whole-room accounting remain in the 2D activity. These assumptions and signs follow the [Carnot-cycle construction](https://openstax.org/books/university-physics-volume-2/pages/4-5-the-carnot-cycle), [quasistatic ideal-gas adiabats](https://openstax.org/books/university-physics-volume-2/pages/3-6-adiabatic-processes-for-an-ideal-gas) and [entropy definition](https://openstax.org/books/university-physics-volume-2/pages/4-6-entropy).

## Persistent verification

`tools/check_integration_cycle_spatial.js` uses fixed shipped CommonJS requires for the geometry engine, module families and cycle/numerical helpers. No VM, source-file evaluation or generated executable code is used. It checks 6,745 actual subgraph builds, 26,136 independently derived Carnot path points, 1,300 cycle states and 35 native mounted control states. The measure builds cover every exposed q, amplitude, exponent and probe combination; the Carnot checks cover mixed extremes, every discrete control value, all forward/reverse legs, endpoints and selected interior states. Mounted select/input values deliberately behave like native strings, so direct numeric builds cannot hide a dropdown normalization failure. Both new models also restore initial state and camera on reset.

`tests/test_integration_cycle_spatial.py` passes three tests. Fraction arithmetic independently verifies power-of-two widths, rational probe membership and squared height/volume/L² quantities. SciPy separately integrates p(V) over every exported leg. Actual world points are checked against SI calibration and independent ideal-gas equations. A separate read-only reviewer also checked 26,136 path points, 1,296 selected states, 1,512 pressure–volume integrals and 384 subgraph states, with no remaining numerical or thermodynamic mismatch.

The review found a real new-family UI bug: numeric leg options were rejected after a native select supplied strings. The family now uses string values `0`…`3`; each selected leg is verified through the rendered control path. The latent shared-handler issue also affected the existing numerical-conditioning sign control. The shared handler now restores the authored option's number/string type. The regression selects native `-1`, verifies negative δ and signed solution change, and checks reset to +1. Copy was also clarified so A=0 uses a defined input interval rather than a nonempty support, and moving probes are explicitly distinguished from fixed points.

## Coverage limits

Correct geometry and a finite set of checks establish consistency of these particular models. Measure Theory still needs broader work on atomic measures, measurable sets, exceptional sets and general probability foundations. The thermodynamics lesson still needs broader material/process coverage; this ideal reversible cycle is not a rapid irreversible compression transient, microscopic entropy simulation, real refrigerator or solved evolving room-temperature model. Whole-subject and whole-Primer fidelity remain open.
