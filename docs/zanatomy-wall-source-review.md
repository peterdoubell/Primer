# Original Z-Anatomy wall-layer source review

The pinned [Z-Anatomy source](https://github.com/LluisV/Z-Anatomy/tree/6c7f9016bd5899ac8edafd31b9900c151df42ed6) supplies separately named rectus abdominis, internal oblique, transversus and fascia geometry that was not available in the preceding BodyParts3D 4.3 selection. These are recovered original source candidates, not independently approved clinical anatomy, calibrated patient segmentation or complete hernia models.

The original `MuscularSystem100.fbx` is 37,343,180 bytes, SHA-256 `4c19df534d5d84aabbce08604306aa0485b43e8a2483c72a95b569e1dfea2279`. All 16 selected native model instances and nine geometries are retained. There are 65,926 native position records across the nine geometries, and 244,931 native faces across the instances. The 257,939 possible triangulation count is a count only: no source polygon is triangulated, capped or replaced in the preserved geometry evidence.

Seven left/right pairs share the same native geometry ID, with distinct source model IDs and source transforms. Seven instances have negative transform determinants. They are original reflected source instances, not locally fabricated opposite-side anatomy or independently observed patient sides. Every source geometry/model/material connection is checked against the original FBX. Native positions, polygon index records and original edge, normal and material arrays are conserved losslessly with typed-array hashes. Native normals use the original direct per-polygon-vertex domain, whose count and finite values are checked. Material slots such as `Tendon`, `Flexion` and functional rotation labels remain artist/source labels; they do not independently delineate histological muscle/aponeurosis boundaries.

World transforms come from the pinned official ufbx reader and the existing exporter, whose source hash is recorded. NumPy independently reproduces all selected world bounds from original positions and those source matrices, retaining float64 world-coordinate hashes. Source world units are centimetres; review display uses a factor of ten for millimetres. These numerical checks do not approve patient-axis registration, source-acquisition resolution, physiological thickness or registration to the different BodyParts3D/MRI sources. No fitting between datasets is performed.

The investing fascia contains three exact duplicate position records. Analysis-only exact-position indexing reduces its boundary-edge count from 390 to 386; source positions and polygon indices remain unchanged. Internal-oblique findings persist under exact-position analysis. Every original edge finding retains its source face IDs, oriented vertex IDs and positions; the unused fascia position is preserved. An open index or closed artist surface is not automatically missing tissue, a pathological defect, a measured tissue volume or a valid biological interface.

Fascia polygons are not all planar: maximum least-squares-plane corner departure is approximately 2.188062 mm in the original transformed coordinates. Original quads and pentagons remain intact, and no automatic planarization, thickness, extrusion, capping or anatomical repair is introduced. Nine review sheets show 32 source-instance views, every native face, boundary/winding markers and unused positions. Source normals provide review lighting; colours and projected polygon fill are display choices. Nonplanar face fill and view occlusion cannot replace the numerical evidence. All final sheets were inspected.

The bounded original-model queries find no separate rectus-sheath, semilunar, Scarpa or Camper model names. The six aponeurosis-name matches are epicranial, palmar and plantar structures, not abdominal wall replacements. Investing abdominal fascia must not be relabelled as separately validated anterior/posterior rectus sheath, arcuate-line regions, Scarpa/Camper layers or every tendon/aponeurotic interface. Inter-layer contacts, continuity, self-intersections, fine layer identity and independent anatomical review remain pending; named model availability is not completed reporting coverage. Pathological defects, sac/content, mesh/fixation, atrophy and dynamic mechanics are not supplied by these artist reference objects.
