# Original cystic lung CT figures

All eleven original CT figures from Peng et al., *Diffuse cystic lung diseases—a primer for radiologists* (2026), PMC13200743, are preserved as complete unchanged publisher JPEGs. Original article metadata/XML/media MD5 and encoded/decoded pixel SHA checks bind the figures to the source. The full grant is CC BY4.0; complete captions and in-pixel material were individually inspected without a separate figure credit. Author attribution excludes editors. The older PMC5474378 XML has an explicit CC BY-NC4.0 link despite its short generic Attribution statement, and its figures are not reused commercially.

Figure1 combines three different CPAM patients A/B/C with a separate source sequestration case D-F. Its caption describes E as3D, but the actual E pixels are axial CT and F is a flat vascular projection. Neither is an interactive native mesh. Figures2/4 actually have A-I, although their captions omit sagittal panelI. Figure5 actually labels A-F,H,I,K, with no G/J panel; original lettering/caption are retained rather than repaired. Figure3 source baseline A/B and eight-month C, and figure8 December2016 A/B versus January2018 C/D, remain dated source cases without independently calculated registration or burden change. Figure9's different patients are not merged into one case. Figure11 is six separate cyst/mimic examples, not a longitudinal sequence or an acquired PET examination. Figure12 is a diagnostic flowchart, not tissue anatomy; its size categories overlap at20mm and no validated reader classifier is inferred.

Source disease labels, age/sex, smoking, Sjogren/lupus and FDS/AMP terminology describe their specific source cases. They do not establish the current patient's histology, genotype, organism, whole-lung burden or functional severity. Source geometry and original native series are unavailable, and full wall/cyst/airway/vessel relationships remain unresolved. No leaf coverage or clinical approval is granted from the figures.

The investigation now starts with original CT examples and uses appropriate source figures in its reporting steps. Four normal presets are removed. Reports require actually observed cyst/mimic boundaries, site-specific distribution, mural/nodular components, intervening lung and pleural/systemic context. The age/count heuristic is qualified; thin walls, wall thinning and low PET uptake do not independently establish benignity. The thorax guide is explicitly partial orientation, not cyst anatomy or disease-specific3D geometry. Shared lesson media remain unchanged.
