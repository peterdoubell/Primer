# Source motion and tongue reader integration

The swallowing Images and motion tab now offers the original published MRI movie through a lossless VP9 browser transport. All 64 original decoded RGB frames were independently compared and remain identical. The original MPEG4 file is retained separately. Browser container timestamps differ by at most 0.000133 seconds; the original presentation timestamps remain the displayed source chronology. There is no cropping, interpolation, added/removed frame or enhancement. This does not turn the montage into raw MRI data or independent temporal sampling.

The reader keeps MRI modality, seven-panel montage, unknown individual subject and research-cohort context separate from the patient's VFSS. The source sliding-window reconstruction, temporal regularisation, 2×2×6 mm grid and source figure-number discrepancy remain explicit. No physiological phase labels, aspiration exclusion, native 3D tissue geometry, patient registration or image/schematic/model coverage is assigned.

Source and browser container readers derive the actual dimensions, sample/block count and timestamps from bounded MP4/WebM structures. They reject unsupported edits/tracks/lacing/encryption and malformed sizes. Metadata cannot silently change either timestamp list, dimensions or frame count while files remain unchanged. The timestamp-error bound must be finite and nonnegative. Playback fallbacks use the active presentation interval rather than prematurely selecting the nearest next frame. Seeking shows a loading state until the selected frame is available.

The four tongue source surfaces and matching MRI preview are a separate registered, mouth-cropped, publisher-smoothed WT9FC case; they are not registered to this movie. All 67,508 triangles and both inferior-longitudinal components remain intact. Dataset CC0 and the separately restricted article licence are distinguished. The shared anatomy renderer changes only to register that atlas/range; its geometry, transforms and existing controls remain unchanged. Seven pending native-CT shared-reader hashes and 24 pending MSK viewer hashes were refreshed after checking the relevant changes, without anatomical approval.

Tinnitus reporting now has a complete draft source scope, with 185 groups/1,110 parts and actual temporal/functional evidence requirements. Four unsupported normal presets and categorical protocol/angiography conclusions are removed. Existing OpenEar original prepared-specimen geometry is offered as a partial local anatomy reference with preparation, drilled opening, source-coordinate and unresolved structure/functional limits preserved. It supplies no current tinnitus lesion or cause.

Local browser review confirmed movie metadata/playback, end-frame seeking, matched tongue label selection and inferior-longitudinal isolation, MRI preview and licence label, and the tinnitus specimen model. A 390×844 viewport has no horizontal overflow (page width390; movie slider bounds14.47–315.02). No browser errors were reported. Source movie, geometry and images are locally byte-bound. Authenticated production reader UI remains unverified until independently tested.
