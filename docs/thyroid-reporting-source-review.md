# Thyroid reporting and representation requirements

The thyroid ultrasound investigation now enumerates 72 source-qualified groups and 445 components: both lobes and actual variants, midline isthmus, every relevant nodule and internal component, source-resolved margins/capsular interfaces, actual acquired node cortex/hilum/content, and adjacent muscle, airway, esophageal, vascular and encountered neural/parathyroid relations. Every component requires image, schematic and 3D evidence. Listed groups are a known floor; every actual site must be instantiated. Unacquired deep, retrosternal, microscopic or tiny unresolved tissue remains missing, not absent disease.

The [ACR thyroid/parathyroid practice-parameter preview](https://gravitas.acr.org/PPTS/DownloadPreviewDocument?DocId=157&ReleaseId=2) was acquired and its examination sections reviewed. Its preview status is retained; no new certification or final guideline status is inferred. Its graphics and full text are not repackaged. The [ACR FAQ](https://radssupport.acr.org/support/solutions/articles/11000071474-acr-ti-rads-faq), modified January 2026, informs the distinction between nodule classification and clinical management after biopsy. The [existing RA TI-RADS source](https://radiologyassistant.nl/head-neck/ti-rads/ti-rads) supplies descriptor context; no image reuse grant is inferred from that text.

Three preset normal statements are removed. The comet-tail tip no longer classifies all such artefacts as zero-point findings. The prior-biopsy tip no longer makes growth an automatic exception that enables routine ACR management. Actual feature evaluability, applicable population/risk, cytology and previous treatment are required. Nodule-to-thyroid-boundary and adjacent tissue relationships must distinguish source-supported gross extension from abutment/bulging; pathology and microscopic invasion remain separately supplied evidence.

The neck model is explicitly partial orientation. It does not supply native nodule internal tissues, every gland variant/node, or vascular/neural/airway interfaces. Static neutral textures cannot reproduce acquired ultrasound echogenicity, reverberation or posterior artefacts. Doppler, motion, flow, physical scale, thyroid function, cytology and assays require corresponding sources rather than invented geometry. No anatomical approval or representation coverage is granted. All-radiology fidelity remains incomplete.
