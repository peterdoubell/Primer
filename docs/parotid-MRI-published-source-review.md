# Original source parotid MRI cases

Three complete clinical figure masters from [Gaudino et al., High Field MRI in Parotid Gland Tumors: A Diagnostic Algorithm](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11719534/) were acquired under the original CC BY 4.0 grant. XML, PDF and all six published media match publisher MD5 values. The three clinical masters preserve every decoded RGB sample without resampling, enhancement, cropping or anatomical edits. PDF page/caption/image-object bindings were visually inspected: Figure 1/object 107/page 7; Figures 2/3/objects 140/175/page 8. The PDF images have different original encoded contrast from the smaller HTML images (thumbnail RMS approximately 26.59–27.48). No brightness alteration is performed to force pixel equality across the publication versions.

The original source cases remain distinct: Figure 1 is a 54-year-old man with a left-parotid source-labelled pleomorphic adenoma; Figure 2 a 65-year-old woman with a right-parotid source-labelled Warthin tumour; Figure 3 a 15-year-old boy with a right-parotid source-labelled low-grade mucoepidermoid carcinoma. The adolescent example is not relabelled adult or borrowed as a universal population reference. Original tissue labels are publication context, not newly inferred current-patient histology.

Panels a/b/c are source coronal T1, axial T2 and late postcontrast fat-suppressed T1; d/e are DWI and ADC; f is a derived DCE signal-intensity/time plot. These roles remain distinct. A displayed plot is not the native acquisition, independently calibrated signal values or patient-specific 3D. Paired DWI/ADC availability does not supply full raw b-values, native registration, every tissue boundary or a new quantitative ADC measurement. Original normalized ratios and thresholds are source study results, not independently recalculated patient measurements.

The study statistics/distributions (Figures 4/5) and diagnostic algorithm (Figure 6) remain nonclinical intake. They are not rendered as anatomical structures or adopted as a universal histological classifier or management pathway. A source ghosting/signature appearance, enhancement or threshold does not remove the need for actual applicable clinical/tissue evidence.

All three masters and original source pages were visually inspected. Four source-case/role/provenance checks passed. Source anatomical approval, full gland/duct/nerve/vessel/node/lesion-interface extent and matched native 3D remain unproven. No runtime publication or representation coverage is granted by this intake.

## Reader integration, 7 October 2026

The head/neck malignancy reader now includes the three complete original PDF-master figures. Each retains its distinct age, side and source tissue label, including the 15-year-old case. Panels a–e are acquired MRI views; panel f is explicitly an MRI-derived DCE signal/time plot. Source curve labels and reported measurements are not a new patient classifier or independently measured acquisition.

Local API delivery returned all three exact original file hashes. The targeted integration/source/reader/safety checks passed (92 tests); the raster inventory verified 495 originals and the source mesh inventory verified 47 contracts. The full fidelity gate remains incomplete: 62 investigations have unexpanded requirements. These assets grant no new structure coverage or anatomical approval, and this release does not claim complete clinical/commercial readiness.
