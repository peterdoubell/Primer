# Non-traumatic intracranial haemorrhage source candidates

The effective `ra.intracranial-haemorrhage` report assesses compartment, parenchymal site and oedema, subarachnoid and ventricular extension, mass effect, and demonstrated underlying causes. `ra.ct-traumatic-haemorrhage` has a different clinical context. The reviewed CENTER-TBI figures retain their trauma identities.

The PHE-SICH-CT-IDS Figshare API record 23957937 version 4 identifies a 1,300,209,108-byte ZIP and displays CC BY 4.0. The authors' article, PMID 38522249, describes the dataset as available for noncommercial use. The archive remains a candidate pending clarification. This review acquired the metadata only.

INSTANCE Zenodo 6362221 lists one challenge-description PDF under CC BY-ND 4.0. That repository record supplies no CT or masks. PhysioNet CT-ICH 1.3.1 requires a data use agreement and describes a traumatic cohort. The RSNA 2019 original data terms specify noncommercial use, including for reuse of CT underlying later segmentation projects. None establishes a reusable non-traumatic case and annotation pair for Primer.

The AHA/ASA spontaneous ICH guideline, doi:10.1161/STR.0000000000000407, supplies acquisition and finding context. Separate evidence is required for actual source CT, lesion and oedema boundaries, ventricular and cisternal extension, and cause-specific vascular or structural acquisitions.

## Released study evidence

The three source cases in Primer preserve their separate study identities. Their delivered samples, surfaces and overlays were checked against the original data.

| Study | What the source depicts | Reporting evidence still needed |
| --- | --- | --- |

| [CMHA AHMU1218005](https://doi.org/10.6084/m9.figshare.26965450) | A sac surface and a combined model with the parent artery and branches, paired with the case CTA | Independent review of the surface alignment and individual branch identities |
| [RHUH case 001](https://zenodo.org/records/8228847) | Preprocessed non-contrast CT with one expert haemorrhage label | Compartment-specific masks, acquisition-native attenuation, and an independently confirmed cause |

These examples cover aneurysm geometry and binary haemorrhage distribution. The investigation still needs separate evidence for perihaematomal oedema, ventricular extension, hydrocephalus, midline shift, vascular malformation, venous thrombosis and other causes. This source review grants no clinical approval or representation completion to the inventory rows.
