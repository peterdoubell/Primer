# Chest CT cardiovascular reporting requirements

`ra.ct-cardiovascular-pearls` now requires **108 groups / 1,053 leaves / 3,159 image, schematic and model obligations**. They cover actual chamber/appendage/septal/valve interfaces, all source-assigned myocardial regions, covered aortic/coronary/systemic/pulmonary/venous branches and actual variants, pericardial layers/recesses/fat, each disease component/repair and source-quality/calibration/functional/comparison controls. Labels are a known floor; source CT may not resolve every thin layer or assign every region. Unassessed parts remain explicit, not normal or absent.

All five prior normal presets were removed. Unknown nongated phase/obliquity can affect wall and root measurements. A single filling defect may be mixing rather than thrombus; chamber shape, reflux, diameter/ratio, fluid/thickening or calcium do not supply measured function, pressure, shunt, tamponade, constriction or exclusive aetiology. Whole-patient visual CAC burden can be reported with method/coverage on suitable nongated CT; this is distinct from stenosis/CAD-RADS and unvalidated quantitative scoring. Valve/root/wall/annular calcium sites and unavailable valve function remain separate.

Relevant source passages from PMC6365314 (DOI10.1186/s13244-019-0693-y) and PMC7774698 (DOI10.1259/bjr.20200894), both actual CC BY4 grants, were inspected with metadata/XML hashes and scope recorded in `chest-cardiovascular-source-review.json`. This does not claim full current guideline or independent clinical approval. Source-specific fixed dimension/ratio suggestions are not adopted as universal diagnostic thresholds. Further clinical assessment is contextual, not an automatic procedure instruction.

The scoped heart companion explicitly lacks native complete patient-specific chambers/walls, coronary tree, venous/arch variants, valve apparatus, pericardial/disease/repair geometry and measured physiology. No parent figure/model fills leaf coverage. Original CT calcium examples are separately preserved in the accompanying published-source packet, with pending anatomical review. Other lesson/investigation media sharing the cardiac-masses/devices module are unchanged and still require explicit reconciliation.
