# Solid renal-mass fidelity scope and reporting correction

The solid renal-mass reporting surface now has 214 draft anatomical/feature targets and 642 image/schematic/model obligations. Its effective reporting checklist, templates, walkthrough and reading contract are fingerprinted. Both kidneys, renal cortex/medulla, collecting systems, actual arterial/venous branches, observation margins and composition, renal sinus/fascial compartments, local extension, venous upper extent, acquired adjacent organs, nodes and covered spread are separate targets. Each actual observation, branch, variant and acquired site still requires instantiation; this is not a closed patient anatomy list.

MRI-only T2, chemical-shift, suppression, subtraction and diffusion/ADC regions cannot borrow CT imagery. Static geometry cannot validate enhancement, tissue composition or invasion. Conditional tumour/mimic examples require source diagnosis and context; a normal kidney atlas cannot replace a mass or thrombus. The renal cyst/Bosniak module remains separate and unexpanded; Bosniak is not assigned to solid masses. No new renal image, schematic, model or clinical approval is created by this scope.

The old Composition prompt called macroscopic fat diagnostic of angiomyolipoma without qualification. It now favours classic AML in appropriate renal-origin/context, asks for calcification, suspicious soft tissue and engulfed fat, and distinguishes opposed-phase signal loss from macroscopic fat. The [Society of Abdominal Radiology consensus](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6980339/) describes rare fat-containing RCC exceptions and MRI fat terminology; the [EAU diagnostic guideline](https://uroweb.org/guidelines/renal-cell-carcinoma/chapter/diagnostic-evaluation) retains histological uncertainty among overlapping solid renal tumour patterns. Neither is treated as a source-image redistribution licence.

The existing [Radiology Assistant reading](https://radiologyassistant.nl/abdomen/kidney/solid-masses) contains tissue, differential, venous and spread examples. Its summary shortcuts are not imported as deterministic diagnosis rules. Enhancement calibration, acquisition adequacy, source uncertainty and pathological identity remain explicit evidence requirements. The inventory does not claim that every MR sequence or every whole-body staging site is acquired in every case.

Five new renal checks and 46 combined renal/liver/scope/walkthrough checks passed. They verify current reporting scope, reject parent-organ coverage of fine structures, keep MRI-only features separate, reject normal anatomy for positive pathology, and preserve the qualified fat prompt. The local browser renders the corrected composition tip; desktop and mobile DOM checks pass without horizontal document overflow, and browser runtime errors are absent. Full radiology readiness remains unproven: the known floor is 11,151 representation obligations, 106 investigations remain unexpanded, and all 106 curriculum surfaces still need reconciliation. These changes are local after PR #63.
