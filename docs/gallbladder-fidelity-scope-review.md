# Gallbladder ultrasound fidelity scope and dynamic evidence

The gallbladder ultrasound investigation now has a draft inventory of 220 reportable targets and 660 image/schematic/model obligations. All new representations remain missing/unverified. Whole gallbladder models, image counts and a complete walkthrough cannot stand for separately reportable wall, neck/outlet, material or complication anatomy.

Targets include fundus/body/infundibulum/neck/cystic-duct regions and inner/outer interfaces; actual sonographic wall layers and conditional resolved tissue compartments; intramural cystic/echogenic foci and wall disruption; every luminal stone/material/polypoid component; cystic-junction anatomy; distension/compression source geometry; vascular sampling sites; pericholecystic collections and hepatic bed; suspected necrotic/gas/defect/fistula/ectopic-stone regions; acquired ducts/host anatomy, variants and postoperative/device anatomy. Conditional CT/MRI adjuncts retain their actual modalities instead of lending ultrasound-specific features.

The effective checklist, templates, walkthrough and reading sources are fingerprinted. Actual site/variant/lesion instances, adequate original source resolution and independently reviewed anatomical extent remain required. Apparent sonographic layers are not automatically individually resolved histological layers. The original biliary packet's gallbladder envelope does not supply fine wall/lumen anatomy, disease states, flow or clinical symptoms; no source model is promoted by this inventory.

Mobility requires real position-change/temporal observation, compressibility requires actual response assessment, tenderness requires directly documented probe-site response, and wall vascularity requires actual adequate Doppler. None is established by a still frame or model colour. Sluggish material, incomplete neck visibility, contraction/fasting, clinical timing and assessment limitations remain explicit.

Source boundaries were checked against [Gallbladder obstruction](https://radiologyassistant.nl/abdomen/biliary-system/lk-jg), [Gallbladder wall thickening](https://radiologyassistant.nl/abdomen/biliary-system/gallbladder-wall-thickening) and the original [TG18 cholecystitis diagnostic/severity guideline](https://doi.org/10.1002/jhbp.515). Local/systemic inflammatory evidence and characteristic imaging are combined for clinical diagnosis; morphology alone is not a clinical severity grade. A large/round gallbladder or isolated thick wall does not establish hydrops or the full cholecystitis diagnosis.

The walkthrough now preserves incomplete visualisation, avoids size/shape-only hydrops claims and no longer prefills a negative sonographic Murphy sign. Its normal action retains explicit positive/negative/not-assessed-or-unreliable choices plus actual fluid and clinical-context fields. The positive finding phrase is framed as support for a diagnosis integrated with clinical/laboratory context, rather than complete proof from two observations alone.
