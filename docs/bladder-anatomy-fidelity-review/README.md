# Original bladder anatomy and tissue evidence

This delivery preserves all six original HRA v1.1 bladder primitives from each female and male reference: 41,290 and 10,073 triangles respectively. Position and normal Float32 values and triangle index order are unchanged. Index storage becomes uint32 in the BP3D transport; its values do not change. The original complete GLBs, metadata, crosswalks and transport/contact reviews are retained here. Raw model rights are CC BY 4.0, separate from graph-data permission and from rights to acquired Visible Human images. Kristen Browne and Heidi Schlehlein’s source citations and dataset DOIs are preserved.

These are curated Visible Human reference geometries. They are separate from TCGA-DK-AA6P and any current patient. No MRI fitting, registration, segmentation or pathological extension is invented. Camera directions refer to original glTF coordinates; acquisition calibration and patient axes are not independently verified. Original region names—including the duplicate fundus labels and dome/base node names—remain visible rather than being silently reinterpreted as histological layers.

Every original nondegenerate triangle enters the existing conservative contact audit. The female model retains 82 nonshared/overlapping contact pairs; the male model has none in that numerical audit. Their biological meaning is unverified. Exact-coordinate edge comparison retains 768 female and 149 male unmatched boundary occurrences. Region cuts, intentional openings, tissue thickness and missing acquisition-derived extent remain separate questions. No cap, bridge, smoothing, welding or repair hides them. Two orifice nodes in each model retain original FMA IDs with embedded `representation_of` strings that differ from the canonical FMA URI form. This discrepancy is documented and does not confer independent label/extent approval.

The complete [Niazi et al. Figure 1](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1186/s13000-020-01002-1/figures/1) is preserved as its original publisher PNG under the article’s CC BY 4.0 grant, with full attribution. Panel a is H&E tissue. Panels b/c are annotation/training masks; panel d is edited tissue with unlabelled regions removed. The source pathologist’s selected urothelium and lamina-propria observations do not establish full-thickness tissue, muscularis propria, whole-slide resolution or 3D anatomy. These panels are not registered to the MRI or HRA references. Direct inspection confirmed all four original panels and their processing differences; image bytes were not edited.

The reporting inventory adds 56 explicit groups and 323 separate structures/interfaces, each required in images, schematics and models. This is a minimum expansion of the actual report, not a closed anatomical universe. It retains wall sites, unresolved microscopic layers, lesion interfaces, UVJs, outlet/diverticular anatomy, sex/anatomy-dependent adjacent organs, nodes and only actually acquired upper tracts. Histology is a distinct evidence type and cannot fulfil an MRI-specific requirement. Source regions and a whole-bladder label do not fulfil their children automatically. All 969 new representation requirements remain missing/unapproved.

All 16 registered source-anatomy references now reach their 11 matching lessons with their original case notes, source images and volume links. They are selected independently rather than fused across cases. The old teaching and source-figure review fingerprints remain bound to their historical surface; a new complete current model-props/module proof covers the additive delivery. No clinical/commercial completeness, every-layer fidelity or full-radiology approval is granted.
