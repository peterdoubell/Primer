## Full draft request with appendix

**Subject:** Permission and full-resolution source request — 141 MSK-related figures for Primer

Dear Radiology Assistant editorial/permissions team,

I am seeking written permission or confirmation of the applicable terms for
using the figures identified in the accompanying URL/use appendix in **Primer**,
a multi-subject educational web application with musculoskeletal radiology
learning and reference pages. The proposed distribution includes commercial
use and potentially paid access. The appendix identifies 141 image URLs and
167 placements, including supplementary spine, paediatric and nuclear-medicine
references; it is not limited to your main musculoskeletal section.

Your disclaimer permits educational reproduction with credit. Please confirm
whether that permission covers, or whether you can grant, non-exclusive worldwide
rights to:

- Reproduce and display the listed figures in Primer's lessons, reference
  galleries and image-enlargement views, including commercially distributed or
  paid-access versions.
- Store approved copies on our own servers or hosting/CDN providers and deliver
  them to users' browsers, with normal caching and backups.
- Resize and encode responsive copies, crop or separate source panels, and add
  clearly identified educational arrows/labels or accompanying descriptions
  while preserving clinical meaning and original credits. Please specify any
  adaptations that require separate approval.

Could you also provide the highest available **de-identified original figure
files**, preferably original-resolution TIFF/PNG or minimally compressed JPEG,
and individual panels/animation frames where available? Please include the
original legend, annotation key and available plane, sequence and laterality
information needed to preserve context, and flag files available only at web
resolution. We are requesting approved educational figures, not identifiable
patient records.

For each item or group of items, please confirm which rights you control, the
required author/publisher credit and source link, any fees, term or territorial
limits, and any excluded third-party figures or subpanels requiring separate
author, institution or journal permission. Please identify exclusions by the
appendix's `RA-MSK-###` ID or exact image URL; a referral is welcome where you
cannot grant the relevant rights. Please also identify any consent-related or
other reuse limitations attached to the supplied material.

Any authorised changes would be marked, and the app would retain agreed credits
and article links. Permission would not be presented as your endorsement or as
clinical validation of Primer. Please let me know if you require a particular
agreement or additional project details to assess the request.

Thank you,
[Sender name]

[Sender email]
