The protocol metadata identifies these controls using the existing requires_scope coordinates for section, finding, slot and values. Both report composers reject the combined state. The browser reports the conflicting field and offers recovery by clearing that selection or unconfirming the acquisition. It retains the original state, including other observations and any text the reader entered. The preview and copy action resume once the conflict is cleared. Loading a previously saved conflict follows the same rules.

The check also covers the complete authored sentence pasted into the acquisition field. Other free text remains reader-supplied content. Quality limitations, previous comparison, laterality and modality-specific observation rows do not establish an exclusive overall acquisition. No competing performed-acquisition control was found in the other eleven confirmed worksheets.

The acquisition-conflicts-inverse.json file restores the exact prior composer and profile bytes before the earlier acquisition, spacing, formatting and report histories are evaluated.
