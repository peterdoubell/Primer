# Investigation source image review — 23 September 2026

The investigation-specific MSK galleries contain 76 figures across 16 investigations. A further 74 figures populate 37 investigations that had no images after strict source filtering. All 150 new selections were rendered and visually reviewed in the in-app browser; their source dimensions were read from the loaded image elements. No broken images were reported during that review.

Selections were checked against the exact assigned Radiology Assistant article. Captions identify MRI, CT, ultrasound, radiography or anatomical schematics as appropriate. This review corrected a sonographic biceps image encountered within an MRI elbow chapter, replaced a muscle-disease lecture slide with a true MR image, and relabelled schematic-only and multimodality panels. Normal labral variants are described as variants rather than tears. Perianal, shoulder and ankle anatomical planes were checked visually.

The two bowel-ischaemia images are explicitly labelled static lecture covers because that source provides video lectures. They are not represented as patient scans. Older source images retain their native resolution, including some 370-pixel figures; images were not artificially enlarged or reconstructed. Every figure retains a source link and attribution.

The catalogue validation reports 136 investigations, all with at least one source-gallery figure, and 364 source-gallery figures in total. The 16 MSK reporting overrides include separate examination-specific checklists and editable templates, with ultrasound shoulder separated from MRI shoulder and radiographic hip morphology separated from optional MRI observations.

This is a modality, source-selection and rendering review; it does not constitute independent clinical validation of all source teaching material or the separate generated anatomical artwork.
