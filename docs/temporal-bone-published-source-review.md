# Original temporal-bone CT/MRI source figures

Seven complete unchanged original figures from [ESHNR imaging of middle ear cholesteatoma recommendations](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11913933/) are added to the temporal-bone investigation. Actual original XML expressly grants CC BY 4.0; no separate figure-credit restriction was found for these seven figures. Complete captions, authors, DOI, grant and source pointers are retained. Every publisher JPEG MD5, file SHA256, decoded-pixel hash and image dimension was checked; all seven whole figures and annotations were visually inspected. Figure 8 is a management flowchart and is not promoted as anatomical imagery.

Figure 1 contains four source examples: healthy right A/B/C, pars-flaccida right D/E, pars-tensa right F/G and left chronic-otitis H/I. Healthy panels have explicit normal-reference states; they cannot serve as pathological evidence or be merged with other cases. Figure 2 A/B and C/D show different mastoidectomy patients. Figure 3 A/B, C and D are different patients with different erosion sites, not a continuous single-case lesion.

Figure 4 retains non-EPI DWI A/B, original ADC ROI C and the publisher's colourised DWI/T2-SPACE fusion D. No new fusion or native-series registration is performed. The printed ADC mean/unit scaling is retained without independently converting or calibrating it. Figure 5 A/B and C/D describe distinct source examples; shared patient identity is not established. Lower perilymphatic signal is not a new measurement of protein content or physiological function.

Figure 6 keeps CT A separate from MRI B/C/D, including the original ADC inset. The caption's 590×10⁻⁶ mm²/s and printed overlay units/scaling are attributed source statements, not independently recalibrated measurements. Figure 7 A/B fat graft, C/D cartilage graft and E/F/G cerumen are separate examples. Only G is explicitly subsequent after wax removal; no exact time interval or universal DWI interpretation is inferred. Bright DWI has mimics and does not alone provide tissue identity.

These are selected local source images, not a full native examination, verified whole ossicular/inner-ear/neurovascular model, complete device trajectory or independently calibrated acquisition. Ages, native orientation, anatomy outside each view and clinical function are not guessed. All new assets remain pending anatomical review with empty structure coverage. Replaced investigation media are archived; other investigations and lessons are unchanged. Complete high-fidelity image, schematic and native model coverage and independent clinical review remain outstanding.
