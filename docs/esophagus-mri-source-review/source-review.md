# Original esophageal MRI source review

The original [article](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11227487/) XML, PDF and numbered figure media match their publisher MD5 checksums. The article grants CC BY 4.0; the six original figure records contain no separate rights exception. Complete original PDF decoded image samples and ICC profiles are retained, with no enhancement, crop, resize or relabelling. Original PDF pages 6–9 and all six complete composites were visually reviewed.

MRI panels show source-local wall/lesion interfaces. CT, endoscopy, endoscopic ultrasound, histology and esophagram retain separate roles. Figure 1 contains multiple staging examples and explicitly two different T4 cases; no patient identity or registration is transferred. Figures 2–6 retain source age/sex. Source ADC values, histology, treatment response and stage are published case context, not calibrated new-patient classification.

Caption conflicts are recorded in visual-review.json. Figure 5's caption calls the second histology h, but the original inset is j and h is ultrasound; no correction to the image or independently verified subtype assignment is made. Figure 1's repeated d/capital I and Figure 2/6 arrow discrepancies remain explicit.

These original clinical composites improve available fine wall and extramural examples. They do not establish every reported tissue, native volume/mesh registration, microscopic completeness, current clinical certification or physiological timing.
