Figure 1 supplies no ADC panel, so DWI brightness alone does not independently establish restricted diffusion. Doppler colour belongs to ultrasound. Figure 2 depicts two patients: post-resection ultrasound observations cannot establish MRI tract anatomy in the separate abscess case. Figure 3 preserves selected baseline/follow-up views and source-reported response, without granting complete remission, treatment causality, quantified fibrosis or independent spatial registration.

For Figure 4, CT c was performed one day before MRE and ultrasound f/g one week later; CT e timing is not separately established by the caption. These sources cannot be silently treated as a simultaneous registered volume. Figure 6 references fat-suppressed sequences that are not shown; CT obtained two months later cannot supply missing earlier MRI features or establish earlier perforation. Full tract courses/endpoints, complete bowel coverage, calibrated lengths, cine persistence, acquisition parameters and histological fibrosis remain unverified.

Figure 5 contains ultrasound/CT only and is not registered as an MRI figure. Figures 7/8 are reporting/modality flowcharts and are not registered as clinical anatomical images. No MRI feature or anatomy credit is borrowed from these figures.
