The 2017 source's Figure 4 carries a separate courtesy credit and is excluded. Pathology drawings and other modality examples were not substituted for the requested ultrasound observations. The newer supplementary item is a DOCX rather than an acquired cine clip; it is not claimed as dynamic ultrasound evidence.

Original PDF image resolution is larger than each acquired repository preview. Three output JPEGs retain the original encoded DCT stream byte-for-byte. Four PNGs retain every independently decoded Flate image sample. One PNG retains every original indexed-colour pixel after exact palette decoding. The perforation figure retains both original JPEG colour samples and independently checked JPEG soft-mask samples in a lossless RGBA PNG; no source RGB or alpha value is changed. The alpha range is 225–255. A bare opaque JPEG would discard this part of the published presentation.

The complete nine-figure packet was directly reviewed as a contact sheet on a white source-paper background. The perforation image was also inspected separately. This establishes source presentation evidence, not tissue/diagnostic accuracy. Pixel sampling, original native DICOM/ultrasound data and display/ICC calibration remain unverified. Correct source-background composition and explicit unlettered mixed-modality identifiers must be checked when integrating the masked figure into the reader. The full packet remains outside runtime until those presentation requirements are handled together; this turn does not quietly publish a smaller easy subset.

Colour Doppler twinkling is an artefact and cannot stand for wall hypervascularity. CEUS stills cannot prove every phase, source flow timing or a complete vascular response. Static apparent membranes do not prove movement, probe frequencies are source descriptions rather than calibrated native pixel resolution, and neither a parent gallbladder mesh nor a publication frame can establish Murphy response, compressibility, full wall layers, histology or clinical severity. Source diagnoses, arrows and inferred panel roles remain source/visual-review claims, not independent clinical approval. No publication letters are invented for unlettered views.

Eleven focused source/scope regression checks passed. They bind all nine files, source rights, original pixel hashes, mixed-modality roles and the retained nonopaque alpha channel. Original images are stored in this offline review directory with their CC BY 4.0 source attribution recorded in the evidence; raw XML/PDF/media snapshots remain ignored. No new runtime image, model, structure coverage, clinical approval or production release is claimed. Original dynamic evidence and independent fine-anatomical/clinical review remain required within the complete radiology goal.
