# Tinnitus reporting source review

Reviewed 8 October 2026. This is a draft inventory requiring independent neuroradiology, neurovascular, otological and anatomical review. No source image, cine, segmentation or structure coverage is approved here.

The effective `ra.tinnitus` reader had four automatic negative/normal statements, including absence of vascular abnormality and shunting, and offered an unconditional catheter-angiography exclusion phrase. The draft removes those presets and preserves actual acquisition limits. It requires 185 groups and 1,110 separately instantiated parts (3,330 representation obligations). These are a known floor; each actual branch, lesion, repair and source-specific interface still needs its own evidence.

The [original Radiology Assistant article](https://radiologyassistant.nl/head-neck/tinnitus/pulsatile-and-non-pulsatile-tinnitus) describes neural/temporal-bone, vascular and intracranial-pressure contexts. Its source clinical examples and graphics have not been repackaged. A contact or anatomical variant is not independently proof of a symptomatic cause.

The [original Pegge review](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5263210/) distinguishes morphology from dynamic vascular information and conditions further evaluation on clinical suspicion and acquired examinations. This source does not validate a universal scan combination or prove that routine negative MRI excludes every small shunt.

[NICE NG155](https://www.nice.org.uk/guidance/ng155/chapter/Recommendations) distinguishes pulse-synchronous, non-synchronous and non-pulsatile symptoms and accompanying clinical findings. Isolated symmetrical non-pulsatile symptoms do not automatically require imaging. Its phenotype guidance is not an acquired patient finding or complete radiological exclusion.

The [original compartmental review and patient series](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3719451/) supports reviewing acquired epicranial, cervical, temporal, dural and intracranial compartments. It discusses varied causal pathways and common incidental variants. Older management examples are not automatic present-day recommendations.

The [institutional MR protocol](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8917066/) separates source sequence roles and their technical limitations. Actual time-resolved phases support transit interpretation; static vascular signal or geometric stenosis does not directly supply calibrated pressure, complete reflux classification or a complete angiogram. One institutional protocol is not a universal acquisition mandate.

The [original palatal-tremor case/pathway review](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3639700/) supports conditional assessment of acquired palate/muscle and brainstem/cerebellar interfaces for supplied non-synchronous movement symptoms. It does not make all tinnitus a tremor disorder or turn static MRI into observed muscle function. Tiny pathway boundaries remain unproven rather than fabricated.

The ACR narrative and appendix endpoints failed to return reviewable contents during this review. Search snippets were not substituted for the full guideline. No claim is made that its full current narrative was reviewed. The source lists therefore do not cite it as fully reviewed evidence.

All images and cine remain outside this draft. An article's reuse grant would require figure-specific checks before any later packaging, and does not certify anatomical labels or transfer unrelated dataset rights.

## Optional original OpenEar reference

The existing verified OpenEar ZETA resource is relevant as partial prepared-specimen anatomy for local temporal/IAC relationships. A proposed registry entry is saved in `proposed-openear-source-reference.json` for root review; the registry is not changed by this proposal. It reuses the current immutable manifest and original prepared microscopy picture without new geometry, texture, relabelling or coverage approval.

Its 13 source surfaces retain all 7,349,910 original triangles. They do not independently separate cochlear/superior/inferior vestibular fascicles, every middle-ear membrane or tiny canal wall, all arterial feeders or shunts, venous lumen/wall/dura, or patient-specific lesions. Source RAS coordinates are available; patient anatomical directions and laterality remain unverified. The superior semicircular canal preparation opening must not be presented as pathological dehiscence. Fixation, staining, dehydration and embedding alter specimen appearance and may alter shape. These are separate specimen references, not the reader's patient CT/MRI, normality, transit, neural function, sound or evidence that tinnitus is caused by the displayed structures.

The scope script is import-safe. Only direct execution applies reporting JSON and creates a fresh effective-source digest; that mutating entrypoint has not been executed by this review agent.
