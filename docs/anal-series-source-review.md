# Anal MRI series screening and nodal subregion requirements

The [TCIA ExACT collection](https://www.cancerimagingarchive.net/collection/exact/) is directly relevant to anal tumour and nodal MRI. Its current table marks the images and clinical data unavailable under controlled-access terms. The public project form has a separate CC BY 4.0 entry; that permission is not a grant for the underlying MRI or patient data. The acquired original form is retained byte-identically, with its source hash and dataset attribution. No restricted images or clinical records were downloaded.

The [Pelvic Reference Data collection](https://www.cancerimagingarchive.net/collection/pelvic-reference-data/) supplies CT/CBCT registration references. Its supporting annotations are landmark coordinates and transforms. Those records do not supply anal MRI, tumour masks or complete soft-tissue layer geometry. No source modality or annotation type is inferred from a broad review article or transferred between these collections.

All 13 public form pages were inspected. Pages 4, 6, 8 and 10 identify source-specific nodal subregions and paired sides. Twenty added draft groups separate anterior internal-iliac, lateral-sacral, presacral, lateral/medial external-iliac, lateral/medial/posterolateral common-iliac and superficial/deep inguinal regions. Each has eight leaves for bed/vessel interface, actual nodes, measurement, morphology and unresolved extent: 160 new leaves and 480 image/schematic/model obligations. These remain anatomical review requirements, not validated geometry or a new universal nodal taxonomy.

The form's inguinal field combines superficial/deep entries. Its posteromedial external-iliac field includes obturator nodes, and its presacral vessel label requires terminology review. Existing distinct obturator requirements remain; overlapping compartments must be reconciled without double-counting a node. Locator circles and a normal example cannot establish actual metastatic node boundaries or full vessel course. Diagram-specific attribution and independent anatomical fidelity are not verified, so its figures receive no runtime or coverage promotion.

Pages 11 and 12 contain N2/N3 and an M2 option. These differ from the explicitly AJCC8 tables in [NCI PDQ](https://www.cancer.gov/types/anal/hp/anal-treatment-pdq). PDF modification dates are not staging-edition evidence. The source options are retained as historical review evidence and are not adopted by the runtime report or classification. Histology, primary site, named edition and clinical reconciliation remain necessary.

Adjacent-organ and regional-node presets now retain adequate/limited/unassessed assessment, actual findings and unresolved coverage prompts. They do not insert unsupported negative findings when source adequacy is unknown. Report fields stay bound to the updated effective contract.

The anal-cancer draft now has 55 groups, 375 leaves and 1,125 obligations. The full known radiology floor is 31,359, with 85 investigations unexpanded, 106 unreconciled curriculum surfaces and an unknown total. Runtime figures and image-rights counts remain 674 unique / 231 cleared / 443 unverified. Full-series acquisition, source geometry and independent anatomical validation remain unfinished.

Changed access terms, source identity or form bytes require re-review. Tests bind the form to its specific license, preserve controlled-data exclusions and historical staging holds, reject generic parent/opposite-side coverage, and check the assessment prompts.
