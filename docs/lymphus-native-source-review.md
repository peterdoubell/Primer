# Original LymphUs ultrasound audit

The full radiology fidelity objective remains unachieved. This audit acquires actual individual-node ultrasound images for further review rather than substituting radiation target regions for node tissues.

Both complete original [author-hosted archives](https://qamebi.com/cervical-lymph-node/) were acquired: Center 1 is 21,867,173 bytes with 541 files; Center 2 is 29,108,550 bytes with 475 files. All 1,016 file CRCs and recorded SHA256 values were checked. No publisher checksum was supplied, so locally computed hashes are preservation evidence rather than an independently published checksum claim. Original archives and pixels remain in the ignored research cache.

There are 338 original image/mask/masked-image triples. Centre 1 contains 99 source-benign and 81 source-malignant cases; centre 2 contains 76 and 82. Every case has exactly one corresponding clinical spreadsheet row. Case identity includes centre, source group and number: numeric IDs alone are not unique. Blank spreadsheet rows are retained in the audit; none is turned into an invented patient. All 338 masks match their image dimensions, are nonempty and have the original binary representation. No samples, labels, masks or clinical rows were repaired, resampled or relabelled.

Every original `Lesion.bmp` equals the decoded original `Image.bmp` multiplied by its original mask. The [paper](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC13049566/) describes these as fused/overlay images, but the acquired files are masked images. The binary annotation supplies the selected node envelope, not independent cortex, hilum, capsule or ENE tissue boundaries. Source groups are source clinical outcomes and are not diagnoses newly inferred from pixels. The methods describe ultrasound-guided FNA cytological confirmation; no new histology, HPV/EBV status or microscopic ENE claim is made.

A four-case plate (one source-benign and one source-malignant case from each centre) was visually inspected. Original source arrows, dotted calipers and measurement marks are retained, not added by Primer. Displayed pixels are anonymization-cropped 8-bit exports; they are not the native ultrasound DICOM/cine acquisition, and image-derived physical scale is not independently verified. Individual source age, sex, nodal level, side and plane are not inferred. Two-dimensional node masks do not supply 3D interiors or motion. The plate and its original-member hashes are kept for review, without runtime publication.

The article XML explicitly grants CC BY 4.0 for its published content and has no separate exception in Figure 3's caption. Article XML and PDF match their publisher metadata MD5 values. The author dataset page separately says the complete database is available for research; an explicit commercial native-dataset grant was not established. These grants are not conflated. Full archive acquisition does not itself clear commercial publication. HaN-Seg's actual Zenodo record also specifies CC BY-NC-ND 4.0, so it was not used as a commercial native-data substitute.

The deterministic audit checks every original member and all decoded triples, and matches every clinical case. It does not approve every pictured structure or close an imaging, schematic or model obligation. The next usable published-image route is the original licensed Figure 3, with larger PDF master binding and source-case review before runtime integration. Individual node internals and all adjacent interfaces remain required.
