# Original fixed-lung multiscale anatomical figures

Whole original figures1/3 from Xian et al., *A multiscale X-ray phase-contrast tomography dataset of a whole human left lung*, PMC9163096, are preserved as unchanged publisher JPEGs. Source metadata/XML/media MD5 and encoded/decoded pixelSHA bind the original figures and explicitCCBY4 article grant. Complete captions and pixels were inspected without separate figure credit. This clears the article figures, not the rawN5/TIFF/JPEG2000 dataset licence, clinical accuracy or complete anatomical coverage.

Figure1 a/b are photographs of an excised prepared specimen and mounting jar; c is a synchrotron instrument schematic; d is a flat25.08µmCT-derived whole-left-lung rendering. Only d is selected as CT. The photographs are typed as anatomical specimen photographs, and c has separate non-tissue schematic scope. The source labels indicate left upper/lower lobes, interlobar fissure and mainstem bronchus, not every wall, branch or secondary lobule.

Figure3 a-d are source sampling-volume locations on flat renderings, e is source6.5µmVOI4b/2.5µmVOI5 rendered context, and f shows a source partially calcified spiculated apical nodule with secondary-lobule septa and perilobular vessels at multiple scales. Vessel annotations do not separately establish every artery, vein or lymphatic; the nodule has no independently verified pulmonary histology or benignity diagnosis. Source f uses6.5µmVOI5 and2.5µmVOI2b, not the acquiredVOI03/03breview blocks. Shared donor identity is not identical local-tissue correspondence. Original scales/annotations are retained, not recalibrated or redrawn.

The source donor is a94-year-old woman; the specimen was formalin fixed and imaged in70%ethanol after preparation. It is not an in-vivo healthy/normal HRCT or the current reader's patient. Phase-contrast scalars are not clinicalHU, published pitch is not independently verified effective resolution, and source fixation/registration effects remain. Flat renderings do not supply an interactive native mesh. Neither source stills nor numeric registration metadata approve actual in-vivo anatomy, genotype, physiology or clinical conclusions.

The figures supplement the HRCT reporting guide's lobular/vascular and regional anatomy steps. Existing fibrotic clinical examples remain; no shared lesson or other investigation media changes. The thorax model remains explicitly partial. No structure leaf, model or clinical anatomical review is approved. Figure2 is an acquisition pipeline rather than additional tissue anatomy and is not packaged here.
