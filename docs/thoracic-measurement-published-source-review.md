# Original CT/MRI measurement teaching figures

Reviewed 6 October 2026. Three complete publisher JPEGs from [Rajiah, CT and MRI in the Evaluation of Thoracic Aortic Diseases](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3874367/), DOI 10.1155/2013/797189, are retained with the original **CC BY 3.0** grant and copyright. XML/media publisher MD5s, raw JPEG checksums, decoded samples, dimensions and actual CT/MRI types are verified in [source proof](thoracic-measurement-published-source-review/original-source-review.json).

The PDF contains individually placed CMYK panels and separations. They are not recomposed or colour-converted. Instead, the complete original repository figure JPEGs are reproduced byte-identically: Figure 1 is native grayscale 597×867; Figure 2 RGB 600×1777; Figure 3 RGB 600×2217. All were inspected with original labels, callipers, intensity statistics, scale marks, white spaces and full montage retained. Highest acquired masters and display calibration remain unverified.

Figure 1 is an unlettered sagittal CT locator of root, ascending/arch/isthmus/descending and arch branch origins. Caption normality does not establish every boundary or independently validate a normal study. Figure 2 preserves CT coronal/sagittal plane references and the original short-axis minimum/maximum callipers; those printed values and statistics do not supply the source voxel series or independently verified edge/endpoint calibration. Neither provides patient-specific 3D geometry.

Figure 3 retains four MRI methods: non-ECG-gated 3D contrast-enhanced MRA; noncontrast ECG-gated 3D SSFP; a 2D SSFP cine method represented by one static snapshot; and ECG-gated T2 black blood. Complete continuous cine, exact phase, registered/same-patient comparison and functional valve assessment are not supplied or inferred. MRI is not relabelled as CT.

The references are linked to method, ascending, arch and descending steps; they are not forced into root/interval steps as complete evidence. [Package proof](thoracic-measurement-published-source-review/packaged-source-images.json) and evidence entries retain pending anatomical review and empty structure coverage. The nearby 2024 review's CC BY-NC figures are not commercially reused.

Reproduce with acquire_thoracic_measurement_figures and package_thoracic_measurement_figures, using .research/thoracic-measurement-source-review as source-root. Tests check byte/pixel conservation, source modality/sequence/gating, static-versus-cine limits, original CC BY 3.0 proof and no complete model/anatomical coverage grant.

These local figures improve available source examples; the full radiology goal remains active and their hashes are not clinical certification.
