# Original dynamic abdominal-wall MRI figures

Two complete original figures from Belton et al., *DyABD: the abdominal muscle segmentation in dynamic MRI benchmark*, DOI [10.1186/s12880-026-02204-7](https://doi.org/10.1186/s12880-026-02204-7), are available in the abdominal-wall muscle/mesh reporting step. Published source labels and overlays are retained. These are reference figures, not independently approved complete wall anatomy or spatial 3D models.

Figure 2 is the original annotation workflow, with MRI-bearing artwork A/B/D and workflow-only correction stages C/E. The native artwork explicitly depicts a 2D-plus-time stack: t is time, not spatial depth. It shows right/left rectus and grouped lateral-muscle overlays. The source annotation process uses a manually labelled subset, human corrections, propagation and further human corrections; it is not independently manual segmentation of every frame.

Figure 3 contains 18 axial snapshots: three original patient rows, beginning/middle/end of a cycle, and preoperative a/postoperative b. The artwork assigns patient A to breathing, B to coughing and C to Valsalva. The source caption describes these as paired stages of the same patients. Original colours mark right lateral muscle lime green, right rectus dark green, left rectus grey and left lateral muscle purple; source red arrows locate hernias. Preserve these labels as source descriptions, not an independently validated diagnosis or full defect/repair geometry.

The source acquisition section describes 17 preoperative patients and 10 postoperative patients, selecting an axial position with the largest rupture for each acquisition and collating that position over time. Cohort follow-up timing is not the individual timing of these three figure cases. The displayed snapshots do not establish continuous motion/reducibility, calibrated dimensions or pressure, independent inter-stage registration, full spatial depth, viable bowel, complete mesh/fixation, every muscle/aponeurosis/sheath layer, or surgical success. Lateral-group labels must not be divided into separately approved external/internal oblique or transversus models.

The NLM/PMC snapshot metadata, XML, PDF and selected repository JPEG checksums were verified. Independent `pdfimages` extraction and PDF object readback identify Figure 2 on page 4/object 129/image index 3 (1996×923) and Figure 3 on page 5/object 157/image index 5 (1996×737). Their original RGB JPEG samples and 3,144-byte embedded ICC profiles are retained in lossless PNGs. These ICC streams have N=3 and an RGB signature but no Alternate entry; this source requires explicit opt-in for that verified profile form, while the shared acquirer’s existing defaults remain strict. No crop, annotation, interpolation or colour conversion is introduced. Highest-resolution acquired masters and calibrated display appearance remain unverified.
