# Lung-cancer staging fidelity scope

The effective `ra.ct-lung-cancer` contract now has a draft inventory of 281 reportable/localisation targets across 50 groups. Each needs an image, schematic and model: 843 representation obligations. The inventory preserves all five reporting checklist fields, five walkthrough steps, report template sections and the complete reporting/templates/walkthrough/reading hash. No anatomical or clinical approval is granted.

| Report field | Expanded targets |
|---|---|
| Primary | Five actual lobes and fissural/hilar relationships, case-specific segments/subsegments, whole tumour and solid/ground-glass boundaries, central, cystic, subsolid and pneumonic morphology |
| T descriptors | Airway/pleural/chest-wall anatomy; individual mediastinal, cardiac and vascular relationships; apical neural/spinal targets; positive invasion interfaces and separate pulmonary lesions |
| Nodal map | Individual station regions 1R/L, 2R/L, 3A/P, 4R/L, 5, 6, 7 and bilateral 8–14; optional right 11s/11i; boundary landmarks and separately identified suspicious nodes |
| Metastases | Pleural/pericardial deposits and fluid, contralateral lung lesions, adrenal/liver/bone and other acquired sites, with each lesion and host interface retained |
| Confidence | Actual acquired extent, primary-relative nodal laterality, station counting, complementary staging/confirmation evidence and treatment context |

The current [IASLC ninth-edition staging card](https://www.iaslc.org/research-education/publications-resources-guidelines/staging-cards-thoracic-oncology-9th-edition) was downloaded from the official public link, byte-counted and SHA-256 fingerprinted. Lung-staging text pages 1–11 were inspected; rendered pages 2, 6, 7, 8 and 10 were checked directly for invasion targets, metastatic organ-system distinctions and nodal definitions. Acquisition/review metadata are in `lung-cancer-fidelity-scope-review/iaslc-source-review.json`. Original PDF and internal renderings remain in ignored research storage. No card images are packaged; its figure reproduction notice requires permission before distribution and no permission was requested or granted.

The linked [adenocarcinoma](https://radiologyassistant.nl/chest/lung-cancer/Lung-Adenocarcinoma) and [cystic-cancer](https://radiologyassistant.nl/chest/lung-cancer/cystic-lung-cancer) articles inform conditional morphology rather than prove source-case histology or reuse rights. The effective reporting contract already names TNM ninth edition; this change adds structure requirements rather than changing its staging edition.

Station spaces remain distinct from tumour-positive nodes. Their boundaries need actual adjacent anatomy; a generic mediastinum or lymph-node sphere supplies neither a station map nor node disease. The official card's station 3P terminology and optional right station 11 subdivisions are retained. Older [node-map prose](https://radiologyassistant.nl/chest/mediastinum/mediastinum-lymph-node-map) includes inconsistent 3B/3P wording, so its original image labels require reconciliation before binding. Side-specific borders, actual primary side and the distinction between node counts and station counts remain explicit review work.

Positive tumour, invasion and nodal/metastatic examples cannot borrow coverage from normal anatomy. Contact geometry, a CT solid component, node size, effusion or a diagram cannot supply pathological confirmation. CT visibility limits for small neural or pleural interfaces stay explicit; complementary imaging is required where applicable. Neural/spinal targets and brain lesions permit CT/MRI evidence on their separately acquired examinations, not presumed coverage by chest CT. All actual nodes, lesions, affected vessels, segments, organ systems and treatment alterations still need source/case instantiation and specialist review. The draft lists are a floor, not a closed patient-independent denominator.

Twenty-six relevant tests passed, including the new staging inventory checks and preserved all-radiology, appendix, brain, coronary and airway checks. They verify source/report consistency, retention of named stations and smaller interfaces, rejection of parent-model substitution and normal-to-pathology binding. These software tests do not validate anatomy. The complete fidelity gate still fails as intended.

The updated all-radiology audit contains 8,163 known obligations, zero clinically verified representations, 110 unexpanded investigations and 106 curriculum surfaces requiring reconciliation. All 843 new lung-cancer obligations are missing. Clinical/commercial readiness remains false and the complete count remains unknown. This scope work is local and follows the successful PR #61 deployment; it has not itself been merged or deployed.
