# Original cardiovascular-device radiographs — 6 October 2026

All 22 complete publisher JPEGs from [Mathew et al., Chest radiographs of cardiac devices (Part 1)](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6837806/) (DOI 10.4102/sajr.v23i1.1730) were visually inspected, including text printed within the pixels. Twenty are preserved byte-identically under the actual CC BY 4.0 grant. Figures 13 and 21 explicitly print “Reproduced with permission” from Cressman et al. (DOI 10.1067/j.cpradiol.2018.05.006), although their XML captions omit that notice. Their source licence/permission to these article authors is not a redistribution grant to this project. These two remain in ignored inspection storage only and are not copied to runtime or given rights approval. Figure 21 also contains an occluder illustration; it is not cropped out or reclassified as cleared material.

Metadata/XML/file and decoded-pixel SHA, original dimensions/modes and publisher MD5 are recorded in the proof. All selected annotations, white margins and source notes remain complete; source images are not recoloured, sharpened, cropped, resampled or recomposed. The [published erratum](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8661294/) (DOI 10.4102/sajr.v25i1.2256) corrects article classification to Review Article; it adds no independent image/anatomical/clinical approval. Live publisher pages and original XML confirm article CC BY 4.0, with the separately printed permission exceptions preserved. The noncommercial PMC4286824 images are not reused.

Figures 1–12 cover a generator/connector close-up and MRI marker (1), paired dual-chamber projections (2), source-described abandoned leads (3), IVC atrial-lead malposition (4), ICD coil projections (5), subcutaneous ICD (6), external magnet (7), SVC atrial-lead malposition (8), subtle proximal lead fracture (9), externalised conductors (10), CRT/coronary-sinus projections (11) and loop recorder (12). The MRI marker is source historical device identification, not complete current system clearance. The printed parasternal distance in 6 is case/device context, not a universal rule. The magnet in 7 is not a recommendation to use one or a guarantee of its response. Failure/abandoned status requires actual device/history evidence, beyond the source case caption. The coronary-sinus LV-targeting lead stimulates an epicardial target through a cardiac vein, not an assumed intracavitary LV endpoint.

Figures 14–20 cover mitral annuloplasty (14), mitral/aortic tissue prostheses (15/16), TAVR (17), tricuspid mechanical prosthesis with epicardial leads (18), multiple valve prostheses (19) and pulmonary prosthesis (20). Native/prosthetic thin interfaces, complete device geometry, motion/regurgitation and patient-specific suitability are unverified. Figure 22 preserves a catheter aortogram (a) and subsequent chest radiograph (b) of source-described ductal-occluder migration; its source aortic-lumen description is complication context, not an acceptable procedural route. Both are radiographic projections, with angiography/chest subtypes explicitly distinguished; timing, registration and flow are not inferred.

All paired figures retain a/b together; single unlettered figures use the explicit whole source panel. Original acquired examinations, independent calibration, same-examination/cross-figure correspondence and highest-resolution master remain unavailable. No actual 3D device geometry or electrical/valve/pump function is derived from pixels. The 20 figures replace the investigation's two unverified remote gallery items and are linked to actual identification, course, position and complication steps. Original source generator/CRT figures replace the unrelated shared module overview in the intro. Reading links and the shared curriculum/cardiac-mass images are retained in their own scopes. Scheme/device CAD and many remaining pump, catheter, closure and complication examples still need suitable sources.

No structure receives verified leaf coverage or anatomical/clinical approval. Original copyright/rights proof is distinct from clinical fidelity. New figures are local, not deployed.
