# Vascular anomaly reporting fidelity — 6 October 2026

The actual `ra.vascular-anomalies` contract now requires tracing actual arterial, airway and venous connections instead of relying on a normal arch, four pulmonary venous ostia or presumed left-SVC drainage. Its five normal presets are removed. Static compression is separated from dynamic airway dysfunction, and morphology is separated from functional shunt/gradient assessment. Additional contrast phases are not automatic.

The reproducible inventory contains 66 groups and 672 source-specific leaves (2,016 image/schematic/model obligations). It includes both potential brachiocephalic and double-arch components, patent versus inferred atretic/ductal components, branching airway/cartilage abnormalities, proximal pulmonary interruption versus whole-lung developmental absence, actual lobar/segmental/accessory/common/dual venous channels, atrial or systemic drainage endpoints, coronary-sinus/septal relationships, covered IVC/hepatic and azygos/hemiazygos pathways, and actual repairs/devices. Conditional structures must be instantiated for the case; this remains a known floor rather than an exhaustive congenital classification.

Source review used the current [Radiology Assistant module](https://radiologyassistant.nl/cardiovascular/thoracic-aorta/vascular-anomalies-of-aorta-pulmonary-and-systemic-vessels), the original XML body/tables/captions of [Etesami et al., CT evaluation of vascular rings and slings](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4141344/) (DOI 10.1007/s13244-014-0343-3), and relevant original XML systemic/pulmonary passages of [Thoracic Vascular Variants and Anomalies](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9705143/) (DOI 10.1055/s-0042-1757742). Metadata/XML hashes and exact grants are recorded in `vascular-anomaly-source-review.json`. These are pictorial-review sources, not a complete contemporary congenital management guideline review.

The first article's actual grant is CC BY 4.0; its figure-specific credits, original pixels, panel roles and anatomy still need inspection before packaging. The second is CC BY-NC-ND 4.0, so its figures have not been commercially reused. No new figures are added in this change. The generic aorta spatial guide is explicitly limited: it does not represent the actual anomaly, complete airway, ductal/ligamentous components or pulmonary/systemic venous tree. No structure receives verified representation coverage or clinical approval.

The full gate still fails: 137 investigations, 106 unreconciled curriculum surfaces, 80 unexpanded investigations and at least 39,849 representation obligations. Whole-scope completeness, independent anatomical/clinical review and commercial readiness remain unproven.
