# Arts illustration review

Eight stage 0–1 illustrations inspected at 800px and recorded. Drawing compares
cup widths at constant height; crafts distinguishes adding, removing and
arranging material. Authored colour mixing and visual-elements plates remain
intact. Singing separates equal pulse spacing from changing pitch.

Dance now shows four solid figures with different poses and directional cues,
instead of four instruction-only cards. Beat and Melody now beams its two
eighth notes and holds the half note across the complete two-beat interval,
not one. A drawing regression checks both notation fixes.

The instruments plate now shows a plucked string between anchors, longitudinal
air-particle motion in an open tube, a struck drum membrane and a bell clapper
touching the body. These schematic mechanisms replace text-only columns and
distinguish the vibrating part from its resonator. Later arts lessons still require
review. Three stage-2 plates were checked: colour/composition distinguishes
contrast, visual balance and converging perspective; reading music correctly
totals quarter + two eighths + half to four beats. Photography now depicts an
open door, reacting face and empty chair rather than naming unseen shots, and
the close-up has facial features. These are schematic storyboards, not a claim
that every viewer infers the same emotion or event.

Stage 3 music theory was checked: W-W-H-W-W-W-H orders C major, highlighted
degrees 1/3/5 select C/E/G, and G/B/D to C/E/G is explicitly tonal-context
motion. Film & Media now depicts soup and an empty bed next to identical
neutral faces; interpretation labels say “may suggest,” not guaranteed viewer
responses. Design now compares 22 mm and 36 mm handle openings against the
same 30 mm gauge at an identical drawing scale. The caption explicitly makes
this an invented clearance test, not a hand-size standard or proof of grip
comfort/strength. A drawing regression checks both slot heights and gauges.
Art History remains pending broader review.

Aesthetics now has a visible invented composition: a gold circle over a coral
diagonal on blue. The four surrounding questions refer to that actual form,
possible viewer responses and context requiring research, not an absent artwork.
The other advanced plates were inspected: criticism, creative practice and
frontier still need concrete worked examples; World Arts needs source review.

Frontier has since been upgraded to three actually generated fields: a 25-site
threshold rule with seeds 7/19 at density .35 produces 12/13 disks; seed 7 at
.70 produces 21 and preserves all 12 earlier disks. The code draws the exact
rule, not hand-arranged outputs. A regression checks reproducibility, counts
and strict subset preservation. Caption distinguishes parameter snapshots from
a live sensor or interactive control. Criticism and creative practice remain
pending concrete examples.

Creative Practice has since been replaced with two versions of an invented
river-cleanup poster. Wording is preserved while typography and spacing change;
a specific critique motivates the revision and a future reader test remains
explicit. No successful user study or real event is claimed. Criticism remains
pending a concrete worked example.

Criticism now uses a visible invented circle/diagonal composition. Observation
names the overlap; interpretation proposes movement; a counter-reading cites
the circle/frame as possible stability; context requires research. This avoids
presenting a guessed history or one fixed symbolic meaning as fact.

Final arts review checkpoint: all 25 plates inspected at 800px. Theatre now
compares two overhead blocking plans with a single explicitly scoped audience
sightline. The introductory timeline separates early East Asian woodblocks from
nineteenth-century photography. Art-movement studies are explicitly invented
schematics, not reproductions or comprehensive representations. World Arts is
a parallel comparison of structure, setting and transmission, not a universal
ranking. The semiotic plate anchors possible interpretations to a visible form.
These observations do not certify enlarged browser views or interactive states.
