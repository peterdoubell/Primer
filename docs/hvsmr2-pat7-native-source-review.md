# Native bilateral-SVC MRI and source-label surfaces — 6 October 2026

Source: [HVSMR-2.0 (orig), version 2](https://figshare.com/articles/dataset/HVSMR-2_0_orig_/25226360/2), DOI 10.6084/m9.figshare.25226360.v2, by Danielle Pace and coauthors, exact CC BY 4.0. Associated [primary publication](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11219801/) DOI 10.1038/s41597-024-03469-9 describes the acquisition, annotation and required/optional extents. Original metadata/XML and CSVs are preserved with source proof. Images are manually cropped at the chin; no original DICOM or complete original acquisition linkage is supplied here.

Actual named clinical CSV columns select pat7, age 27, moderate anatomical category, bilateral SVC as the only marked diagnosis. This is a source description of anatomical deviation, not prognosis. Initial manual column counting selected pat17 incorrectly; named-column verification identifies pat17 as severe dilation, so that transfer was stopped. No pat17-derived anatomy is included. Version-2 clinical CSV has 59 records (0–58), while the technical CSV and ZIP contain 60 (0–59). Pat59's diagnosis is missing and is not inferred. Nine actual bilateral-SVC source IDs are recorded.

Original ZIP is 1,777,739,061 bytes. Only exact selected members/directory ranges were acquired, with consistent archive ETag, member CRC32 and compressed-payload decoding checks. Native pat7 image is 64,052,855 compressed bytes; original segmentation 245,276 bytes and optional-zone volume 120,214 bytes. SHA values are pinned in tests/proof. Whole-archive MD5 is explicitly unverified. Download chunks are resumable and checked; a repeat acquisition verified cached complete members against source directory CRC and metadata.

All three grids are 528×507×200 (53,539,200 voxels). Original voxel payloads and affine were compared with an independent nibabel reader: every voxel matched. The source sform/qform and NIfTI-declared millimetres are retained; this does not independently validate physical calibration against original DICOM. Axis orientation is P/I/R, spacing 0.7310609817504883, 0.7310609817504883, 0.75 in source index axes. Original intensity range is 0–2704.544359, not HU. Technical row records TE 2.4, TR 4.7, flip angle 90, bandwidth 545, and source split values. The paper describes static SSFP, often navigator/prospective ECG-gated with mixed contrast usage; case-specific contrast and exact physiological phase are not supplied in this technical row. No cine/flow is acquired.

All eight exact original blood-pool classes are retained: LV, RV, LA (including source PV extents), RA, AO, PA, SVC and IVC. Masks and endpoints are unchanged. Separate level-0.5 binary-mask isosurfaces apply the original sform without padding, class merging, fitting, smoothing, decimation or component deletion: 283,148 vertices and 567,000 triangles in total. All derived position/index arrays are preserved with independent scalar-affine checks and SHA. Boundary/nonmanifold edge counts are zero, but self-intersection and anatomical correctness are not thereby established. Source SVC has two 6-connected components, 20,178 and 14,241 voxels. The RV has 266,206 and 13-voxel components; the tiny original component is preserved without deciding whether it is an error.

Optional zones are source benchmark tolerance, not evidence of complete anatomy. Some extend outside the source class: AO 3,444, PA 11,509, SVC 15,001 and IVC 4,583 voxels. The actual SVC required-zone subtraction leaves 27,012 voxels. Optional masks are preserved separately, not merged into the source geometry. Source paper reports initial ensemble labels, manual contours/corrections, island removal/mild smoothing and final pediatric cardiologist review; our derivatives introduce no additional cleanup. Source interfaces can be planar annotations where valves/boundaries cannot be resolved. LA/PV grouping, truncated vessel extents and benchmark chamber naming must not be mistaken for independently labelled complete vein trees, walls, myocardium, thin valves, ductal/ligamentous structures, airway or morphology in complex/heterotaxy patients.

Two inspected source review worksheets show three original MRI planes with source-label/optional-zone contours and complete derived class surfaces with SVC-focused context. Raster worksheets are not substitute meshes. MRI display uses a recorded percentile window and original voxel indices; source voxels were not modified. Full MRI remains in ignored acquisition storage; original small masks, optional zones, all eight derived binary arrays and source proof are retained in this packet. No runtime promotion, verified requirement coverage or clinical approval is granted. Independent source/DICOM linkage, complete branch/connection geometry, wall/valve/airway evidence, functional assessment and specialist clinical review remain required.
