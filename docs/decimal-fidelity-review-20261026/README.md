# Decimal workbench and base-ten volumes

The Decimals lesson previously offered only addition of two integer hundredth counts below one whole. Its ten original assessment objects also require decimal multiplication, comparison, quantity totals, subtraction, division, rounding, change and trailing-zero equivalence. This update preserves those questions, prerequisites and the original illustration and adds eight exact investigations with seventeen worked presets.

Inputs are plain signed decimals with at most six fractional digits and magnitude at most one million. Arithmetic uses reduced BigInt rationals. Column work records actual carries or borrows, including a separately stated magnitude operation and final sign for signed operands. Multiplication uses equally calibrated nonnegative axes and states the algebraic sign separately. Small sub-tenth products use a labeled magnified axis extent. Division retains exact nonterminating quotients as fractions and identifies its displayed window of at most twelve group positions. Rounding uses nearest decimal marks with ties away from zero; mathematical zero has no sign. The money investigation accepts nonnegative exact-cent prices and tender and integer quantities up to one thousand, and identifies an insufficient tender as a shortfall.

The separate 3D companion composes whole units, tenths, hundredths and thousandths relative to one fixed unit cube. Dimensions are respectively 1×1×1, 1×0.1×1, 0.1×0.1×1 and 0.1×0.1×0.1. Whole counts range from zero to three; fractional-place counts range from zero to ten so adjacent-place regrouping can be inspected. Every selected block is a closed outward box; selected interiors do not overlap. Inspection gaps apply in the same world-space units to every component. The companion has independent controls and illustrates three decimal places; it does not claim to mirror another activity’s arithmetic state or infer measurement uncertainty.

`check_decimal_core.py` uses independent Python Fraction and Decimal calculations, plus carry/borrow reconstruction and group-window identities. `check_decimal_spatial.py` measures the actual authored binding’s faces, volumes, nonoverlap and inspection gaps. The actual reader check uses isolated synthetic loopback data, verifies served bytes and original content, exercises all operations and 3D controls at phone/desktop widths, checks speech and invalid-draft recovery, and independently reprojects the rendered 3D vertices. It performs no production learner writes. `runtime-inverse.json` records exact deltas of six shared files to preserve historical source fingerprints without granting new approval.

This improves one mathematics lesson. Completion of the entire radiology, mathematics and physics fidelity goal remains unproven; alternative vascular-source semantic/reporting review is outside this release.
