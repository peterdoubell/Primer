# Corrected liver MRI source acquisition and reading review

LiverHccSeg version 1.1, [DOI 10.5281/zenodo.8179129](https://zenodo.org/records/8179129), is now under source review for MRI-specific liver requirements. The corrected version explicitly replaces an incorrect liver mask; older version 1.0 files must not be substituted. Zenodo publishes the derived dataset under CC BY 4.0, while the original [TCGA-LIHC image source](https://www.cancerimagingarchive.net/collection/tcga-lihc/) identifies CC BY 3.0. Original image and added-annotation attribution/versions remain separate. No source licence is silently relabelled as a model or code grant.

The source describes named original precontrast, arterial, portal-venous and delayed T1-weighted volumes, plus separately named derivatives registered into arterial image space. The latter involve nonrigid B-spline registration; they are not original acquired grids or measured finer anatomy. Two raters' liver/tumour masks are supplied separately. This work preserves both instead of creating an unreviewed average or consensus boundary.

The small clinical/reading metadata workbook passed its publisher MD5 and size checks. Its table has a repeated lower header with omitted tumour-specific columns. The parser now records that section structure explicitly and retains 19 source readings for 17 patients, rather than treating a header as a patient or manufacturing missing tumour values. Source comments include treatment/no-residual-tumour context, poor quality and incomplete-liver acquisition. Those conditions remain source-review requirements, not automatically eligible untreated observations or normal anatomy.

The metadata's tumour-size values are retained without assuming their units. Contrast-agent type is not treated as proof that a hepatobiliary phase was acquired; original DICOM sequence identity and timing must be checked. T2, diffusion/ADC, fat-sensitive and hepatobiliary requirements receive no automatic coverage from a contrast-phase T1 collection. Dataset categories remain source readings rather than recomputed diagnoses or clinical eligibility decisions.

Full corrected NIfTI/mask and original DICOM archive acquisition was started with streaming publisher-size and MD5 verification. The process was confirmed live by its exec handle, and the first archive was observed past 384 MB. Neither full archive is yet verified at this checkpoint. No partial file is treated as complete or extracted as trusted data. `source-acquisition-handoff.json` identifies the current handle and expected publisher hashes; the existing process must be re-polled or its writer inspected before any restart. No observation file or timeout alone establishes completion.

Three parser regression checks passed, and 20 related liver/fidelity checks passed in the application environment. They preserve repeated-header structure, source treatment/quality comments and unknown clinical eligibility. No source workbook was modified, no masks were combined, and no MRI image, model or requirement binding was promoted.

Next work is to finish the confirmed acquisition, verify publisher whole-archive integrity, inventory original versus registered volumes and both raters, and inspect actual native sequence/case geometry. Source sampling, phase/agent identity, registration validity, complete anatomy and diagnostic context remain unproven. The full objective remains all radiology modules; the known floor is 10,509 obligations and the complete denominator remains unknown. This source work is local and undeployed, with the acquisition still in progress.

## Verified corrected NIfTI archive and first case review

The corrected NIfTI/mask archive is now complete: 977,885,191 bytes, publisher MD5 `4382ae1301b68ab60c05de306a1e43ec`, with independent SHA-256 `8b2c20df9b0235d722eba235d2d9803be13326eaf8919a3b27729704a3b9e010`. Whole-archive integrity was independently rechecked before case extraction, and each selected member passed ZIP CRC with its own SHA-256 identity. The original DICOM archive is still downloading through the existing live handle; its full verification remains pending.

The first metadata case, TCGA-BC-A3KG, contains eleven selected NIfTI files: four original T1 contrast phases, three registered derivatives, and two independent liver plus two tumour-1 masks. Original source sampling is 1.40625 × 1.40625 × 2.5 mm on a 256 × 208 × 88 grid. The declared qform is selected, no conflicting sform is supplied, and all four masks match the original arterial selected affine/shape without source-boundary contact. These checks establish stored source geometry, not clinical phase timing or complete anatomy.

The registered files have the same dimensions but different index directions. Their declared affine relationship to original arterial indices is an exact reversal of the first two axes with translations 255 and 207. This accounts for index representation without fitting or source changes. It does not validate the author's nonrigid anatomical registration. Masks must not be overlaid by equal array indices alone or original and warped volumes mixed without their coordinate mapping. No registered derivative was overlaid in this review.

Both source raters remain separate. Liver masks contain 362,161/361,525 positive voxels, with Dice agreement 0.969846. Tumour-1 masks contain 67,428/66,105 positive voxels, with Dice agreement 0.958924. The independent original arterial overlays and unmarked MRI were preserved for review. The tumour overlay was inspected after making radiological direction ordering explicit with physical RAS axes. Agreement describes reproducibility, not anatomical truth or a reason to choose either mask as a definitive boundary. A common arterial display window does not establish dynamic enhancement or washout.

Six filename/metadata role checks and 23 combined liver/fidelity checks passed. They separate original versus registered phases, preserve each rater/observation identity, reject unknown source filenames and retain source treatment/header context. No MRI sequence, arterial/portal/delayed feature, model, patient eligibility or anatomical representation was approved. Full DICOM sequence verification, complete boundary review, rater uncertainty and modality/phase/source coverage remain work. The corrected archive milestone does not shrink the full all-radiology objective.
