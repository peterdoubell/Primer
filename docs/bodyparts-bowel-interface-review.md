# Original bowel-piece relationships and continuity limits

The sixty original bowel pieces now have a complete analytical source-pair contact audit and proximity records for all 1,770 unordered pairs. Original source coordinates, every triangle and each object identity remain unchanged. This is not a fused or anatomically approved bowel tree; no caps are repaired, surfaces snapped, pieces selected or tissue pruned.

All 30,546 source triangles were testable across 258,590 conservative candidate pairs. The audit preserves 7,865 contacts beyond ordinary adjacency, all between objects: 5,726 within a shared regional FMA group and 2,139 across different source groups. One hundred thirty-two object pairs have such contacts. The categories preserve regional source labels rather than identifying validated biological joins, loop identity or clinically correct wall/lumen relationships.

[Source-pair summary](bodyparts-bowel-source-review/complete-source-pair-summary.json) fingerprints the [complete lossless evidence](bodyparts-bowel-source-review/complete-source-pair-contacts.json.gz), including every contact point and original object/face identity. The prior sixty separate self-contact results remain intact and contain no within-piece contacts beyond ordinary adjacency. Ordinary exact shared-position/edge adjacency in the numerical method is not anatomical approval. Its tolerance is 1e-9 mm, not native acquisition resolution.

[All-pair proximity evidence](bodyparts-bowel-source-review/all-source-pair-proximity-review.json) retains original closest vertex indices/positions, exact coordinate-sharing counts and source hashes for all 1,770 pairs. Nearest vertices supply only an upper bound on minimum continuous surface distance. Every one of the 132 intersecting source pairs has a positive minimum vertex-pair distance, showing directly why that value cannot be converted into a surface-gap claim. Conversely, triangle intersection does not prove a patent lumen or correctly joined bowel wall.

For example, ascending/transverse-colon pieces FJ2566/FJ2572 have minimum vertex-pair distance about 0.342866 mm with 23 contacts beyond ordinary adjacency. Descending-colon/rectal pieces FJ2567/FJ2571 have about 0.354754 mm and 155 contacts. These are source geometry findings only. No anatomical boundary or clinical connectivity is renamed or reconstructed from them.

[Location views](bodyparts-bowel-source-review/source-pair-contact-locations.png) retain all 30,546 original triangles as context and every original affected face/contact point in XY/XZ/YZ projections, separated into all contacts, same-region contacts and different-region contacts. [Location provenance](bodyparts-bowel-source-review/source-pair-location-review.json) binds complete counts, exact face coverage and contact-evidence hashes. The figure was directly inspected for source context, category distinction, axes and attribution. Projection overlap is not a biological classification or patient-specific clinical validation.

Source and derived views retain BodyParts3D © 2008 DBCLS and CC BY-SA 2.1 Japan. Original raw ZIP/OBJ files remain ignored. Twenty-seven scientific geometry/source/contact regressions passed, binding full candidate accounting, regional categories, all pair identities and complete rendered face/point coverage. No model is promoted, no approved structure coverage is granted and no full clinical/commercial readiness is claimed.

Fine native wall/lumen anatomy, complete source extent, exact join/loop identity, segmentation/acquisition lineage and independent anatomical/clinical review remain required. Healthy source pieces cannot supply positive obstruction, transition, strangulation or viability models. The complete all-radiology goal remains active and incomplete.
