# Original HCC liver source acquisition review

HCC_003 from [TCIA HCC-TACE-Seg](https://www.cancerimagingarchive.net/collection/hcc-tace-seg/) now has its selected baseline contrast CT, source PRE LIVER series and DICOM SEG acquired independently of AMPLIFAI. The original collection explicitly supplies image, segmentation and clinical data under CC BY 4.0. Required attribution and source-page/API/workbook fingerprints are preserved. Those rights do not license the restricted annotations or harmonisation products added by AMPLIFAI.

All 190 contrast CT files (100,107,966 uncompressed bytes), 20 source PRE LIVER files (10,536,816 bytes), and one SEG file (12,774,606 bytes) passed publisher per-file MD5 manifests and ZIP CRC checks. Local archive SHA-256 identities are preserved separately from publisher per-file hashes. The acquisitions completed and no source download remains running. Original workbooks were read without edits or export.

The published clinical table and glossary distinguish baseline imaging from follow-up after TACE. The selected baseline study has a SEG; the later study is 49 de-identified calendar days later, consistent with the table's seven-week baseline/follow-up interval. Source diagnosis-relative offsets are retained as supplied; they are not converted into an independently verified real-world treatment date. Age, cirrhosis, pathology and vascular/thrombosis fields are source metadata, not new image-derived diagnoses or current staging assignments.

**The contrast series must not be stacked as one 3D volume.** Its 190 images contain two explicit acquisition numbers, each with 95 distinct source positions. They share the same position grid, at 0.703125 mm in-plane spacing and 2.5 mm slice steps. Timing tags are absent and the contrast-agent tag is empty. Acquisition numbers and the series title alone do not assign arterial, portal or delayed phase identity. The PRE LIVER series contains 20 images with 10 mm source thickness; it does not provide finer whole-lesion or phase-alignment evidence.

The SEG has 380 binary frames and source SEMIAUTOMATIC entries named Liver, Mass, Portal vein and Abdominal aorta. Its declared source list contains 95 images, split 52/43 between acquisitions 1/2. Every frame-level derivation list contains two images, one from each acquisition, for a total of all 190 unique contrast source images. Thus the declared and frame-level memberships differ. Multisource derivation can be meaningful, but these references do not establish one phase-specific mask volume. No list was silently reduced or relabelled.

The SEG reverses both in-plane index directions. Its origin is at the CT's opposite voxel corner, with the observed 0.000025 mm difference matching four-decimal coordinate serialization. This declared coordinate relation can be accounted for without fitting or modifying labels. However, the SEG's frame-of-reference UID differs from the CT UID. Numerical coordinate agreement is not accepted as verified registration or derivation lineage; that mismatch and the multisource references remain explicit holds for clinical use.

This review preserves all source segment identities and positive-voxel counts. It does not infer that the Liver class includes all tumour-bearing liver, that Mass identifies each separate observation, or that Portal vein depicts tumour thrombus. Whole-organ/foreground or source clinical thrombosis metadata cannot substitute for enhancing soft tissue, named branches or independent lesion boundaries. No volume, phase feature, model, category or reader binding is clinically approved.

Three partition checks passed, verifying that coincident positions from separate acquisitions remain separate and that duplicate positions within an acquisition or missing acquisition metadata are rejected. Together with the source integrity checks, the application environment passed six checks and explicitly skipped the optional scientific plane check. The complete acquired-file audit identified actual source-reference and frame-of-reference issues, instead of treating an archive checksum or a multiphase title as clinical fidelity.

Further work must reconcile phase/treatment identity, source conversion/registration lineage and declared versus per-frame SEG memberships, then review each native acquisition and actual lesion/host boundaries. No source reference, UID, voxel value or segmentation was repaired. The broader all-radiology objective remains unchanged; the known floor is 10,509 obligations, the complete denominator remains unknown, and no clinical completeness credit was granted. This work is local and undeployed.
