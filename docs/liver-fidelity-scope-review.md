# Liver mass and LI-RADS fidelity inventories

Both liver investigations now have separate draft structure inventories. General CT/MRI liver masses has 246 targets across 31 groups, requiring 738 image/schematic/model representations. CT/MRI liver LI-RADS has 210 targets across 23 groups, requiring 630 representations. Combined, they add 1,368 obligations. All five report checklist fields, template sections and walkthrough steps remain in scope, and each complete reporting/templates/walkthrough/reading contract is fingerprinted. The parent `rad.4.liver` lesson still needs its own explicit reconciliation.

| Shared anatomical scope | Required evidence |
|---|---|
| Whole liver and host interfaces | Actual acquired extent, capsule/surfaces, porta and relevant adjacent tissues |
| Couinaud localisation | Source-specific territories, inflow/drainage relationships and localisation planes, including IVa/IVb as subdivisions |
| Portal, hepatic venous and arterial anatomy | Actual named branches, junctions, visible walls/lumens and variants |
| Biliary anatomy | Each relevant acquired/resolved duct, confluence, host relationship and drainage variation |
| Every observation | Actual whole/solid/fluid margins, named vessel/duct/capsular interfaces, multiplicity, measurement and prior correspondence |
| Background and associated findings | Actual disease regions, collateral pathways, acquired extrahepatic sites and diagnostic uncertainty |

The linked [segmental anatomy](https://radiologyassistant.nl/abdomen/liver/segmental-anatomy) and [mass-characterisation](https://radiologyassistant.nl/abdomen/liver/characterisation-of-liver-masses) sources inform the draft expansion. Common segment names are retained as localisation references; atlas planes and gross BodyParts3D regions are not credited as measured patient territories. Caudate inflow/outflow, duct drainage, actual subsegmental branches and variant boundaries remain source-instantiation work. Microscopic structures or unseen branches are not invented from a coarse model.

General liver masses retains conditional haemangioma, FNH, adenoma, HCC, fibrolamellar, cholangiocarcinoma, metastatic, abscess and cyst examples, with their actual extent, internal regions, host interfaces, phase correspondence and associated findings. These source/case examples do not establish a new patient's diagnosis. General incidental lesions are not automatically categorised under LI-RADS.

LI-RADS retains eligible major-feature regions, targetoid/LR-M and ancillary spatial features, actual tumour-in-vein interfaces and conditional treated-context anatomy. The [current ACR source](https://www.acr.org/Clinical-Resources/Clinical-Tools-and-Reference/Reporting-and-Data-Systems/LI-RADS) remains the diagnostic/treatment-response rule authority, as reviewed in `liver-phase-source-review.md`. Category labels and a single target-lesion mask cannot establish eligibility, every observation, all phases or source-case tissue diagnosis. Treated tissue requires its appropriate separate response context rather than an automatic diagnostic v2018 assignment.

MRI-only T1/T2, in/opposed-phase, DWI/ADC, subtraction, iron-sensitive and hepatobiliary features are explicitly retained. Targetoid diffusion and transitional/hepatobiliary pattern requirements also restrict image evidence to MRI. An allowed CT/MRI list for other anatomy does not prove both modalities have been covered; full modality/sequence scope remains explicit review work.

Eight functional/acquisition evidence obligations accompany each inventory: segment localisation; size/growth; actual arterial/later phases; separate washout/capsule assessment; MRI tissue/diffusion; hepatobiliary uptake; vascular/tumour-in-vein evidence; and patient eligibility, pathology and treatment context. Static schematic geometry or a fabricated texture cannot prove these observations. Actual acquisition adequacy, contrast agent, native sampling and cross-phase/prior correspondence remain required.

Every actual lesion, relevant acquired branch/duct, variant, developmental state and extrahepatic site still requires instantiation. Original image/mask/registration rights must be independently checked. AMPLIFAI's restricted annotations remain commercially uncleared, and an independently eligible constituent image would not waive those annotation terms. No source acquisition, reader model/image or clinical representation binding was added by this inventory change.

39 relevant tests passed. They preserve both complete effective reporting contracts, each segment/branch/duct requirement, positive disease and treatment examples, MRI-only modality conditions and the general-mass versus LI-RADS distinction. Parent liver/portal/observation models cannot stand in for their children, and normal anatomy cannot satisfy positive background disease or tumour-in-vein requirements. The previous phase-rule safeguards and all earlier expanded investigation scopes remain checked.

All 1,368 new liver obligations are missing. The current all-radiology audit has a known floor of 10,509 obligations, zero clinically verified representations, 107 investigations still unexpanded and 106 curriculum surfaces requiring reconciliation. The complete denominator remains unknown; completion is not established and the completion gate still fails. Changes are local and undeployed.
