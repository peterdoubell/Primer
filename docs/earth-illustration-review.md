# Earth and space illustration review

All 27 current 800px lesson images and their curriculum descriptions were
inspected. This is a content/layout pass, not certification of every enlarged
browser view or interactive-model state. Per-lesson findings and responsive
asset fingerprints are recorded in `illustration-reviews.json`.

## Corrections

Eighteen native plates received visual corrections. Early lessons had overlapping
rock-cycle labels, water-cycle labels, geological callouts, atmosphere qualifiers,
planet captions, ocean labels and environmental-monitoring prose. These are now
separated without removing their explanatory relationships.

Advanced changes include a diffuse star-forming cloud, unobscured subduction and
greenhouse labels, biome labels painted above all regions, and a correctly focused
transfer ellipse. The two burns now occur at its apsides with consistent prograde
directions. The final circular orbit is explicitly omitted; an invented additive
speed/climb budget was replaced with the burn/coast/burn sequence.

S-wave paths terminate at the liquid outer-core boundary. Planetary AU ticks use
actual logarithmic spacing, with an explicitly illustrative snow-line position.
Ocean upwelling labels remain visible above stippling. Cosmological expansion is
shown with growing separations in local patches, not rays from one central point.
Earth-system runoff is one-way into the ocean and reservoir labels are clear.
Cosmic-frontier curve labels no longer obscure their plotted evidence patterns.

Descriptions were aligned with actual diagrams and distinguish synthetic data,
schematic ranges and omitted features. The climatology slopes were checked against
[IPCC AR6 WGI, Summary for Policymakers D.1.1](https://www.ipcc.ch/report/ar6/wg1/chapter/summary-for-policymakers/):
the central estimate is 0.45°C per 1,000 GtCO₂ and the likely interval is
0.27–0.63°C. The image is a baseline-relative teaching example, not a dated
remaining carbon budget.

## Verification and limits

Four new regression tests check the actual rendered transfer ellipse, seismic
endpoints, logarithmic ruler and disclosures. Together with chemistry, biology
and review-ledger tests, 25 focused tests pass. These tests complement visual
inspection; they cannot judge all scientific meaning or model appropriateness.

The natural-science checker passes for 93 lessons, 85 deterministic native plates
and 186 responsive rasters. The global inventory passes at 432 illustrated
lessons, 864 WebPs and 259 model entries; the review ledger has no stale records.

The ledger now has 158 of 432 current reviews. Remaining subject reviews,
model-appropriateness assessment, final runtime/browser verification and
deployment remain unfinished.
