# Offline knee source review

These 10 scientific review sheets and quantitative JSON are evidence for source
correspondence, not approved diagnostic figures. They remain outside the app's
`web/` asset directory. Full methods, findings and caveats are in
[the paired-source review](../msk-malaya-knee-paired-source-review.md).

Source data: Jeevaraaj N Vivekanandan and Juliana Binti Usman (2026),
*A Three-Dimensional Lower Extremity Musculoskeletal Geometry Model of An Asian Male*,
Universiti Malaya Research Data Repository,
https://doi.org/10.22452/RD/5T6TZ7. Source data: CC0 1.0.

The sheets display original native-plane MRI samples with disclosed grayscale
windows; author labels are nearest-neighbour sampled, and mesh curves are exact
plane intersections. No anatomical fit or image interpolation was used. Their
contour agreement is not independent tissue-boundary validation, and mesh/mask
volume differences are not biological tissue-volume error. Source contrast is
labelled by the authors; original MR acquisition metadata is not certified.

Raw image volumes are retained in acquisition staging and are not copied here
or into the clinical app. `sha256.json` pins the inspected review artifacts.
