# Radiology foundation update — targeted clinical corrections

Reviewed 23 September 2026. This records the specific existing teaching items
corrected while adding the source-linked reference library. It is not a claim
that every pre-existing question has undergone a new clinical review.

Eighteen quiz items across eight modules were corrected, along with two CCTA
template refinements:

| Area | Correction |
| --- | --- |
| Prostate | The transition-zone DWI-5 example now has a lesion at least 1.5 cm; peripheral-zone DWI scores 1 and 2 are no longer treated as equivalent overall categories. |
| CCTA | SIS 1–2, rather than a range including zero, supports P1. No plaque-burden category is assigned for no plaque. Where reliable burden methods disagree, the template uses the most severe assessment. Plaque without measurable luminal narrowing is explicitly covered. |
| Lung | Station 1 nodes are N3 on either side. N2 disease does not universally preclude surgery within selected multimodality care. N2a/N2b and solid-component measurement are described with TNM9 context. |
| Breast | Updated coarse, round, layering and interval-change terminology. Removed a universal distribution-risk ladder and historical fixed PPV claims. Menstrual-cycle timing is no longer presented as an absolute prerequisite for breast MRI. |
| Dementia | The imaging pattern and negative amyloid study can support suspected LATE; they do not establish TDP-43 pathology or hippocampal sclerosis. |
| MS | Distinguished low signal on susceptibility-weighted images from increased susceptibility on QSM for paramagnetic rim lesions. |
