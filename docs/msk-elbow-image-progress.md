## Remaining gaps and held sources

The original CMYK-byte-preservation description above is superseded by the
[nested-PDF and colour audit](msk-nested-pdf-and-cmyk-review.md). Valgaeren
Figure 2 now uses a documented native-size lossless RGB PNG with the correct
PDF colour interpretation; the earlier convenience export was re-encoded.
Mezian Figure 8 now matches its exact nested original JPEG stream. Anatomical
scope, source credits and pending clinical reviews are unchanged.

Normal bicipitoradial-bursa imaging, normal median/radial MRI relationships, a complete distal-biceps insertion MRI series, individual triceps-head footprints and detailed flexor/extensor component MRI coverage remain unproven by this batch. Ultrasound cannot satisfy MRI-only requirements. The anterior UCL figure does not independently separate anterior/posterior functional bands within the anterior bundle; the transverse UCL remains represented by the pre-existing schematic only.

The [Anderson and Chung 2021 chapter](https://link.springer.com/chapter/10.1007/978-3-030-71281-5_3) offers useful normal tendon/nerve Figures 3.12/3.16, but their captions explicitly say “Used by permission of Springer Nature.” This is different from courtesy-only attribution: the onward commercial grant for reused figures was not established, so those figures were not imported. [Lombardi et al.](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7509698/) and the [3D isotropic elbow review](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8904410/) have CC BY-NC-ND terms and were excluded. Valgaeren Figures 3–6 show signal alterations despite presumptive asymptomatic status; they were not substituted for normal tendon anatomy. No authors or publishers were contacted.
