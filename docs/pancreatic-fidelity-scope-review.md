# Pancreatic-cancer fidelity scope and reporting reconciliation

The CT pancreatic-cancer investigation now has 253 draft anatomy/feature targets and 759 image/schematic/model obligations. Its effective report, templates, walkthrough and reading contract are fingerprinted. Pancreatic regions and whole observation margins remain separate from actual main/accessory/biliary duct anatomy, named arterial and venous branches/variants, contact sectors, patent segments, local tissue interfaces, source neural-pathway context and each actual acquired metastatic observation. Every source branch, variant, observation and relevant nodal station must still be instantiated; the draft is not a closed patient anatomy list.

Actual calibrated source phases and perpendicular vessel planes are required for contact/length/contour measurements. Lumen, wall, vessel contact, filling defect and occlusion are separate targets. Normal organ/vessel models cannot replace positive contact/extension disease. Region prototypes cannot infer microscopic perineural invasion, viable post-treatment tumour, pathological nodal state or unacquired whole-body metastatic absence. Conditional MRI adjunct requirements explicitly reject CT-only evidence. No new pancreatic model or source image is approved.

The existing [Radiology Assistant staging source](https://radiologyassistant.nl/abdomen/pancreas/pancreas-carcinoma-1), [SAR/APA reporting consensus](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/24354378/) and [Cassinotto et al. post-treatment study](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/24960211/) were checked for reporting scope and uncertainty. They support complete source anatomy/descriptors and careful treatment-context interpretation. The 2014 sources are not presented as current complete treatment algorithms, and no source figure/table is copied or its rights inferred.

The walkthrough now distinguishes measured vessel anatomy from multidisciplinary reconstruction/resectability judgement. It requires a named current framework/version and treatment time point. Residual post-treatment tissue/narrowing remains viability-uncertain rather than automatically unresectable. Pathway-region soft tissue is described as concerning for perineural spread without claiming microscopic proof from CT.

The underlying lesson was also found to contain absolute venous-invasion language and a simplified table that mapped anatomy directly to treatment. The overview table, raw template and vascular explanations now retain uncertainty and framework dependence. The vascular category question supplies untreated/site/technical-reconstruction context; the numeric contact question uses a vessel-perpendicular plane and does not turn its answer into an operative rule. These changes do not approve the whole broader lesson: cystic and neuroendocrine material and remaining questions still require separate source/criteria reconciliation. That pending scope is explicit in the inventory.

The generated reasoning plate and responsive 800/1600px WebPs were regenerated from their source spec. Footer/caption now describe anatomy, treatment context and distant disease as inputs to multidisciplinary assessment. The final 800px plate was directly inspected and its visual-review fingerprint updated only for that reasoning aid. This is not independent anatomical, patient-image or clinical validation of a complete pancreatic schematic.

Seven new pancreatic checks and 63 combined reference/fidelity/walkthrough/visual checks passed. The illustration renderer check verifies structural media contracts for 96 radiology lessons and 198 responsive WebPs; it does not certify clinical accuracy. Curriculum-bank audit reports zero structural problems across 19 files. Local desktop/mobile source QA confirmed updated descriptors/limits, API-delivered plate caption, no horizontal document overflow and no browser runtime errors. Owned browser/server sessions were stopped.

The known all-radiology floor is now 12,399 representation obligations, with 104 unexpanded investigations and all 106 curriculum surfaces still requiring reconciliation. The total denominator and full clinical/commercial readiness remain unproven. These changes are local on `codex/pancreatic-fidelity-scope` after the verified PR #64 release. Original source acquisition, geometry, rights and anatomical review for these pancreatic structures remain next work.
