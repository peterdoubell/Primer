# Full-course trigeminal reporting requirements

The MRI trigeminal investigation now enumerates 112 source-qualified groups and 672 components, each requiring image, schematic and 3D evidence. Scope includes bilateral central nuclear/tract regions and root origins; sensory/motor roots and contacts; porus, CSF/dural recess and ganglion; division-specific skull-base routes; peripheral branches, communicating pathways and target muscles; and every acquired adjacent vessel/tissue or lesion interface. Listed groups are a known floor and must be instantiated for actual source anatomy and variants. Unacquired distal pathways and unresolved microscopic tissue remain missing.

The [segmental imaging review](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5750829/), [full-course imaging review](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/20227216/) and existing [RA reporting reference](https://radiologyassistant.nl/head-neck/trigeminus/trigeminal-neuralgia-and-neuropathy) were reviewed for anatomical/reporting context. The original segmental article XML is CC BY-NC-ND 4.0. Its graphics and full captions were not repackaged for commercial use; original figure IDs and caption hashes are retained as intake only. Source facts do not grant anatomical approval.

Five unsupported normal presets are removed. Actual acquisition, coverage, source side/segment, MRI/vascular sequence roles, nerve morphology and uncertainty are required. Clinical neuralgia and treatment effect are separate from simple vascular contact. Motor-root bypass of the ganglion and the different V3 versus V1/V2 routes remain explicit. No routine MRI or schematic establishes every tiny nucleus/fascicle, histological transition, flow, muscle function or microscopic perineural invasion. The existing trigeminal model is retained as explicitly partial pathway orientation.

Ninety-five scope, reporting, source-role and hosted checks passed. The full all-radiology fidelity gate still fails: 64 investigations remain unexpanded, the known floor is 78,456 obligations, and the total remains unknown. No image, schematic or model coverage is granted by enumeration or software tests. The concurrent original NLM head/neck acquisition remains a separate live process and does not supply matched patient MRI or automatically approved neural anatomy.
