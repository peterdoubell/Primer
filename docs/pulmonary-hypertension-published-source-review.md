# Original pulmonary vascular source figures

All nine complete original publisher JPEGs from Jung et al., *Clinical Presentations and Multimodal Imaging Diagnosis in Chronic Thromboembolic Pulmonary Hypertension*, DOI 10.3390/jcm11226678, are preserved unchanged. Original PMC metadata/XML/media checksums and decoded pixel hashes are recorded. The original article grants CC BY 4.0; no separate figure permission or credit was found in the captions or complete image pixels. The illustration acknowledgment to Hyejin Kim is retained. The academic editor is excluded from author attribution.

The source figures were individually inspected on 2026-10-06. Figure 1 is a conceptual schematic, not source anatomy. Figure 2 is the article's 46-year-old female index case, with three axial and three coronal views; the source negative CT caption does not independently exclude distal chronic disease. Figure 3 retains the ultrasound/Doppler distinction and rounded 4.2 m/s versus displayed 415 cm/s. Figure 4 retains source diameter arrows without inventing calibrated measurements or cardiac phase. Figure 5 compares source chronic and acute examples, without claiming that all eccentric defects are chronic or that the panels show one longitudinal case. Figure 6 retains mosaic, calcium and a flat rendered view, without establishing a unique cause or same-patient registration. Figure 7 retains two iodine-map/rendered-view rows and their source RUL/RLL labels; flat renderings are not interactive models. Figure 8 is planar nuclear medicine ventilation/perfusion, preserving all six projection labels and both column groups. Figure 9 preserves an original haemodynamic tracing above two DSA projections; tracing A is ancillary to angiographic B, not a CT or anatomical image. Printed catheter pressures do not supply flow, PVR or independent calibration.
