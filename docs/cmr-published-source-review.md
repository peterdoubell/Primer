XML/grants, figure-specific captions/credits and full pixels were inspected. No explicit separate permission exception was found for selected figures. Original borders, contours, arrows, maps/curves and scales are unchanged; publisher MD5, file/pixel SHA, dimensions/modes and authors/DOI are recorded.

SCMR Figures 5/10 say modified/adapted from separately cited upstream work (DOIs 10.1161/CIRCIMAGING.116.005242 and 10.1016/j.jacc.2010.02.015). Their original commercial derivative rights were not verified, so these are held and not packaged from the broad article grant. No whole source-set rights or full guideline review is inferred.

Method roles: LV/RV contour ED/ES slice grids preserve differing papillary/plane conventions; they are not all native temporal frames or measured EF/mass. Perfusion defect/artefact/ROI pictures are not a dynamic perfusion acquisition. Unlettered left/right magnitude-LGE source examples demonstrate inversion-time sensitivity without universal TI/PSIR or independently measured scar claims. STIR source oedema is a local case, not independent clinical criteria. Actual uppercase T2-star panels A/C are MRI grids; B/D/E are tables/decay fits with source measurements and truncation, not acquired anatomy. Unlettered phase-contrast top grid is representative/grouped images and bottom graphs include source Qp/Qs/flow; no raw calibration or independently measured shunt is supplied. Ancillary repaired-aorta MRA remains a local source case.

Five mapping-review cases preserve complete source SSFP/T2/LGE/map panels and annotations for hypertrophic, amyloid, genetically reported Fabry, biventricular arrhythmogenic and thalassaemia/iron interpretations. These are author-described cases, not independently confirmed pathology/genetics or quantitative function. Map colours/site thresholds and source measurements are not interchangeable across methods. The iron case caption combines hepatic iron concentration in mg/g with T2-star terminology without showing the liver, and uses cardiac map/T1 source statements; the context/unit ambiguity is retained rather than silently converted to measured relaxation time or complete iron diagnosis. Its f panel is a decay graph, separately scoped from MRI images. Source statements of reduced EF cannot be independently assessed from the still montage.
