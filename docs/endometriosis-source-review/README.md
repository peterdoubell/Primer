# MRI endometriosis: complete source artwork and reporting scope

The `ra.mri-endometriosis` investigation now has six complete original figures
and twelve original Table 2 images from the 2025 [ESUR protocol/lexicon consensus](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s00330-025-11611-3).
Five figures contain clinical MRI examples; Figure 2 and the table images are
conceptual drawings. Figures 4 and 6 also retain distinct clinical photographs,
and Figure 6 retains a laparoscopic operative observation. Those panels are
not relabelled as MRI. Different cases and local views remain separate from a
complete native examination, any candidate model and the current patient.

Original publisher XML/PDF MD5 values are verified against the archived source
metadata. The source PDF is retained for independent readback. Each JPEG is
byte-identical to its original PDF DCT stream. Each PNG retains every decoded
8-bit grayscale/RGB PDF sample and exact dimensions, compared with independent
Poppler extraction. No cropping, scaling, contrast adjustment, painting,
relabelled annotations or new anatomical geometry is introduced. The original
article's CC BY 4.0 grant, attribution, source links and logos are retained;
no separate restrictive figure credit was found. This is source-rights and
transport evidence, not clinical/anatomical approval or colour calibration.

The source caption for Figure 3 assigns the axial round-ligament view to `a`,
while displayed `d` is axial. Figure 5's distance-caption text contains a
corrupted token. The original captions remain unchanged, with these limits
explicitly recorded. The Table 2 image cell covering extra-pelvic sites spans
wall, digestive and diaphragm rows; its upper-abdominal drawing is not
relabeled as a scar or bowel lesion. Figure 4 includes a source adolescent
case; ages and cases are not replaced by a generic adult patient context.
The table graphics now display their actual table/artwork identifiers instead
of being mislabeled Figure 2. Existing non-table figure/native-source label
branches retain their previous behavior.

The reporting workflow now uses seven assessment areas: adnexa; anterior
central; medial central; posterior central/bowel; lateral/ureteric/neural;
uterus/superficial disease; and covered extra-pelvic sites. Presets insert
unfilled assessment prompts with source adequacy, actual observations,
measurement method and unresolved/unassessed fields. They do not insert
unobserved normality or negative disease statements. A classification requires
an actually adopted system/version and assessed inputs; no score is computed
from examples. Missing contrast, kidney, neural or diaphragm acquisitions stay
missing rather than becoming negative findings.

The complete named report scope is expanded into 190 groups and 877 fine
requirements, each needing independent clinical-image, schematic and 3D
review. Both sides, fine organ/wall/lumen layers, tubular and neural courses,
lesion interfaces, measurement references and conditional covered sites remain
explicit. The 2,631 representation obligations are requirements, not evidence
of completion. None of the new published images receives complete-structure
coverage or an anatomical approval. Compartment divisions are reporting
conventions, not newly invented biological septa.

The held CVH5 source has unresolved independent whole-geometry decoding. The
West Bohemia software templates have independently preserved arrays, but
clinical anatomy, physical calibration and fine layer boundaries remain
unverified. Neither is promoted as a clinically faithful 3D model. The
procedural pelvic guide remains explicitly conceptual orientation. Every
reported structure still needs the requested faithful native/source model
and specialist review; this release does not complete the module or full goal.

`tests/test_endometriosis_fidelity.py` independently reads all original PDF
objects and runtime samples, checks case and non-MRI roles, preset behavior,
shared-lesson separation, fine requirements and zero parent-only coverage.
The source intake and integration scripts in `tools/anatomy_sources` preserve
reproducible identities. The complete radiology audit remains not ready, with
46 investigations lacking an expanded inventory and 113,094 known obligations;
its total remains unknown. The compact current audit records the full audit
hash and reproduction command instead of duplicating all detailed leaf rows.
