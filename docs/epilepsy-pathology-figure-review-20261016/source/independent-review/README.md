# Independent audit of six preferred epilepsy figures

All six preferred assets can support the proposed **bounded publication-example roles**. Their complete images, publisher captions, case boundaries and annotations were inspected. No visible caption/plane contradiction or figure-specific third-party exception was found in these six. This is not a finding of complete anatomy or clinical approval. Exact asset and source-document identities, panel metadata and rejected claims are recorded in `independent-review.json`.

| Figure | Accepted role | Required boundary |
|---|---|---|
| Clinics 2015, Fig 1 | Selected hippocampal-head T2/FLAIR sclerosis example | Right is publisher-assigned; two slices do not establish complete head-to-tail extent, volume or fornix/mammillary findings. |
| Frontiers 2021, Fig 4 | Source-described internal dark-band/atrophy comparison | Two source images plus lower enlargements. The normal comparison is not the opposite patient hemisphere or a normal-population atlas. Caption left/right denotes layout, not patient laterality. |
| Frontiers 2021, Fig 3 | FCD visibility on 2D/3D FLAIR and altered display range | A contains six selected 2D views; B/C are six corresponding 3D-derived display views and their contrast variant. C is not a new sequence, follow-up or independent confirmation. The article explicitly reports resection pathology as IIb; MRI does not demonstrate balloon cells. |
| Clinics 2015, Fig 6 | Source-labelled ganglioglioma, cystic/solid appearance and enhancement | A is T2 and B post-contrast T1, not a T1 pre/post pair. Left is publisher-assigned. No measured size or stated histological confirmation is supplied. |
| Clinics 2015, Fig 7 | Familial cavernous-malformation morphology and additional susceptibility lesions | A and B show distinct selected lesions/planes in one reported case. Do not create a registered T2/GRE same-lesion comparison. B is T2-star GRE, not SWI. |
| Frontiers 2021, Fig 2 | Selected T1/CISS encephalocele context | Four boxes, two sequence groups. No patient side, CT bone confirmation, measured defect or complete herniation extent is supplied. |
