# Complete original bladder MRI source study

The TCGA-BLCA public collection supplies the selected MRI study of TCGA-DK-AA6P under CC BY 3.0, DOI 10.7937/K9/TCIA.2016.8LNG8XDR. Current NBIA metadata and a retained IDC snapshot agree on the same 22 series. All 1,359 original DICOM objects were acquired intact, with every publisher per-object MD5 and archive CRC checked. Their study/patient/series/SOP identities are retained; source dates are de-identified.

An independent explicit-VR little-endian element walker and pydicom agree on all 107,937,792 original signed-int16 pixel samples. The walker follows metadata sequence/item delimiters rather than searching for a PixelData-like byte pattern. Complete source geometry, frame windows, processing declarations, acquisition matrices and source times are recorded per object.

Important source limits remain. Diffusion series each have 56 frames at 28 repeated positions, with different scalar arrays but no sufficient tags to assign the repeat identities to specific b-values/acquisitions. ADC maps are producer-derived and their physical units are not verified from real-world mappings. The source perfusion sequence retains all 30 temporal identifiers; an absolute bolus start is not supplied. Small orientation-declaration differences and nonorthogonality are recorded rather than repaired. A shared frame identifier is not proof of anatomical timepoint registration, and reconstructed sampling does not establish effective fine-layer resolution.

The complete native-plane viewer transports each original series losslessly and loads it on demand. It retains all frames, source temporal identifiers, signed values, source-window controls and a raw sample readout. It does not average diffusion repeats, mask an ROI, invent a segmentation/surface, fit one series to another or assign VI-RADS/pathological stage. Browser checks verified exact original-window pixels on all 44 first/last series frames at a 390-pixel viewport. The archived viewer is a source-review tool, not a complete clinical examination assessment.

Original archives and the full viewer transport are in ignored acquisition staging. This directory retains the complete immutable receipts, compressed all-frame metadata reviews, scalar fingerprints and browser evidence. No original source bytes, native surface models or treatment assertions were added to the clinical runtime. Full reported anatomy, suitability for VI-RADS, treatment context, tissue correlation and independent clinical review remain outstanding.
