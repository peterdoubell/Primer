# Original cardiac mimic/thrombus figures — 6 October 2026

Original complete Figures 1–14 from [Cardiac Masses and Pseudomasses](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10818366/) (DOI 10.3390/medicina60010070) were inspected in full and preserved byte-identically under the actual CC BY 4.0 grant. No explicit separate reproduction/permission exception was found in these captions or complete image pixels. Publisher MD5, original file/pixel SHA, dimensions/modes, source captions and metadata/XML/licence/author attribution are recorded. Original notes, arrows, panel labels and colour scales are retained; no sharpening, cropping, recolouring or added annotation. Highest-resolution master and full raw source examinations remain unavailable. The article has 38 main figures; remaining 15–38 are not acquired/packaged here, and are explicitly outstanding.

Roles:

- Figures 1/3/10: whole TEE stills of source Coumadin ridge, Eustachian remnant and prosthetic aortic vegetation. No acquired temporal motion or independent diagnosis is supplied.
- Figures 2/4: MRI cine-labelled stills with T1/LGE/T2-map panels as captioned. Static pixels and the colour scale do not supply the native mapping acquisitions, quantitative calibration or temporal study.
- Figure 5: source CT lipomatous septal thickening, with incomplete independent contour/tissue/functional validation.
- Figure 6: CT a/b, flat CT VR c and ancillary PET/CT fusion d. Source-described benign lipomatous tissue has uptake; uptake is not automatic malignancy or calibrated SUV, and the flat rendering is not actual 3D geometry.
- Figures 7/11: CT b with ancillary TEE a. Source describes appendage thrombus/catheter-associated endocarditis, but clinical confirmation and full attachment/phase/registration remain unverified.
- Figure 8: LGE MRI still of source infarct-associated LV thrombus; complete extent, native inversion/contrast timing and function remain unavailable.
- Figure 9: CT c/d, ancillary 2D TEE a and preserved rendered 3D TEE picture b. Panel b is not a native echo volume, patient mesh or temporal study.
- Figure 12: confirmed precontrast CT a. Caption calls b a cine sequence without explicitly identifying modality; b is retained unassigned and not selected as confirmed MRI or motion. Body discusses MRI generally but does not resolve this panel identity.
- Figure 13: source MRI T2 STIR/LGE cyst views; complete wall/communication and independent tissue diagnosis remain unresolved.
- Figure 14: CT a/b with preserved flat volume renderings c/d of source partially thrombosed coronary aneurysm. These pictures do not establish complete coronary/thrombus/fistula geometry or perfusion.
