No explicit separate reproduction/permission exception was found in these captions or complete image pixels. Publisher MD5, original file/pixel SHA, dimensions/modes, source captions and metadata/XML/licence/author attribution are recorded. Original notes, arrows, panel labels and colour scales are retained; no sharpening, cropping, recolouring or added annotation. Highest-resolution master and full raw source examinations remain unavailable. All 38 main figures are now acquired, visually inspected and preserved; this completes that source figure set only, not complete reportable anatomy or clinical validation.
