# Original mechanical-support source figures — 6 October 2026

All 12 complete publisher JPEGs from [CT and chest radiography in evaluation of mechanical circulatory support devices for acute heart failure](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10350447/) (DOI 10.1186/s13244-023-01469-8) were inspected and preserved byte-identically under the original CC BY 4.0 grant. XML/figure-specific credits, complete in-pixel notes, publisher MD5, original file/pixel SHA, dimensions and modes are recorded. No explicit separate reproduction/permission exception was found. Figure 1's original signatures/UR Medicine marks are retained and the original acknowledgement of medical illustrators Nazdezhda D. Kiriyak and Jane Lichorowic is included in attribution. No manufacturer website/brochure pictures are reused.

Roles are explicit: Figure 1 a/b/c are conceptual route schematics, not acquired CT or device CAD. Figure 2 has radiographs a/b and CT c/d; Figure 3 radiograph a and CT b/c; Figures 5/6/7/9/12 radiograph a and CT b. Figure 8 is a single CT panel. Figures 4/10/11 are radiographs. Creator-applied CLAHE is disclosed for 4 a/b, 6 a, 7 a, 10 whole and 12 a. Unchanged publisher pixels are not a claim to possess the original unprocessed acquisition.

The source examples illustrate why an acceptable upper IABP marker does not validate balloon length/lower branch relations, and why projected pump tips cannot resolve papillary apparatus entanglement or wall penetration. Source vertebral levels, 5-cm IABP distance and Impella CP 3.5-cm depth are case/model context, not universally calibrated safe positioning. Original CT shows local complications but does not independently quantify perfusion, prove all causal clinical statements or establish tamponade. Figure 6's source ECMO contrast-distribution explanation is not independently measured flow. Figure 11 describes a custom right-sided Impella 5.5 via a PA graft, not a routine approved right-support configuration or procedure instruction.

Figure 12's source caption says an outlet should be in the RV, while the article body/table and the [manufacturer's current product description](https://www.livanova.com/cannulae/en-us/specialty-cannulae) describe proximal RA drainage and distal main-PA return for ProtekDuo RA-PA bypass. The original caption is preserved in the source record, and the conflict is explicit in the displayed limits and reporting tip; the RV-outlet sentence is not adopted as a normal target. This review covers product-description context only, not the full current model-specific IFU or a device-position clearance.

All images are connected to actual device identification/course/position/complication steps; the original support schematic is added to the intro. Requirements now separately name outer drainage and inner return lumens, sideholes/fenestrations, traversed anatomy and actual distal endpoint/IFU/function evidence. Original acquisition calibration, complete temporal/3D volumes, cross-figure/same-series registration, device model/system and independent anatomical/clinical validation remain unavailable. No schematic is relabelled acquired anatomy and no mesh geometry or physiological performance is inferred from a picture. All verified leaf bindings remain empty.

The separate PMC6837777 source metadata/XML was acquired and its CC BY 4.0 grant inspected, but its figures have not yet been acquired/packaged; no imaging evidence is borrowed from it. PMC9059237 metadata snapshot was unavailable (404), and its rights/pictures have not been assumed. Further implanted LVAD, ECMO, catheter and current-IFU sources remain outstanding. New images are local, not deployed.
