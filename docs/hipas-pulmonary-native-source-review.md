# HiPaS original pulmonary source case review

This review examines released case 001 from author-deposited Zenodo record 14879605 (version v2), linked to Chu et al., Nature Communications 16:2262 (2025), DOI 10.1038/s41467-025-56505-6. It is an offline source review, not a native DICOM geometry approval or reader model.

The complete 68,883,107-byte original annotation archive matched publisher MD5 404076d6728455383ce77ac9839cf05f. Original artery/vein case members were retained without filtering. The 80,401,166-byte original CT NPZ member was acquired from the 23,978,140,355-byte CT archive using eight exact HTTP206 ranges and the original ZIP CRC. Archive metadata identity was rechecked after acquisition. The server supplied no ETag. The whole 24GB CT archive was not downloaded and its publisher MD5 was not verified; selected-range hashes and complete member SHA/CRC are recorded separately. The original spreadsheet matched publisher MD5 859c1c04e7fd07d7be8c88b54f7d2e2d.

All three original volumes contain a single numeric `data` array, shape512×512×258. CT is int16 with source scalar range−1024 to3071, distinct from the older Google Drive sample README's described 512³ normalized release. The two releases are not assumed identical. That README describes restricted sample reuse; the Zenodo record labels its released files MIT. The paper itself is CC BY-NC-ND4 and its figures are not commercially reused. Dataset-grant scope/provenance remains a separate review; the article grant cannot be substituted for the dataset grant or vice versa.

The case spreadsheet gives spacing vector[0.6464844,0.6464844,1.0]. Original NPZ members contain no DICOM IPP/IOP/origin/direction/affine or rescale tags. Shape equality and a spacing vector do not establish original patient-coordinate alignment, axis order, laterality, raw DICOM acquisition correspondence or HU calibration. None was invented. Published methodology uses super-resolution and human-in-the-loop segmentation in portions of its workflow; the exact preprocessing and annotation status of this released case remain unresolved.

The source artery mask contains355762 labelled voxels in30 six-neighbour connected components. The vein mask contains411430 voxels in6 components. They overlap at6656 original voxels. All original components and overlaps are retained. Neither the component count nor overlap is silently repaired or diagnosed as error. The reviewed array-index planes show a substantial central chamber/pool-like region in the source venous class and central/peripheral overlap; a generic source class name cannot approve a pure vessel wall or lumen, all actual branches, thin tissues, pathology or physiological measurements. Full native correspondence and independent pulmonary vascular anatomical review remain required.

Three orthogonal source-index plane pairs were rendered from unchanged samples for offline inspection, selecting the highest overlap plane per axis. The plot displays the original scalar window−1000 to600 without claiming calibrated HU and shows artery green, vein red and overlaps orange. Axes are array indices rather than inferred patient orientation. The image is retained in the ignored source cache; its source file hashes, complete PNG hash and selected planes are recorded. No image, segmentation, mesh or leaf binding from this case was promoted to runtime.

`original-case-array-review.json` preserves the numerical findings and acquisition provenance. `original-index-plane-review.json` binds the inspected plot to the original files. Acquisition tests exercise truncated/shifted ranges, changed archive length/entity tag, missing-ETag provenance and out-of-bounds seeks. These checks establish acquisition integrity, not clinical fidelity or commercial readiness. Original files remain in the ignored `.research/hipas-source-review` cache.

## Source surface extraction and primary alternative review

Untouched source masks were extracted with scikit-image0.25.2 Lewiner marching cubes at binary level0.5, step1, unit array-index spacing. No physical transform, axis permutation, sample resampling, source label repair, overlap subtraction, component removal, smoothing or decimation was applied. All extracted geometry remains in the ignored source cache. Vertex positions are serialized float64 and triangle indices uint32 with deterministic gzip and exact compressed/uncompressed hashes.

The artery extraction has277236vertices/554428triangles/75connected surface components; the vein extraction has263215vertices/526470triangles/1surface component. Source foreground connectivity itself depends on the discrete definition: artery6-neighbour30/26-neighbour10; vein6-neighbour6/26-neighbour1. Surface-component counts are not identical to foreground-volume counts, and surfaces can include inner cavities. They do not independently prove anatomical branch connections. The artery mesh has3nonmanifold edges at the retained original index positions aroundaxis0=244..245,axis1=378,axis2=123..124. Neither mesh has boundary edges or zero-area triangles. No numerical repair or clinical approval follows from these checks.

277186artery and263204vein vertices are exact original binary-edge midpoints. Another50artery/11vein vertices are algorithmic interior samples in active source cells resolving ambiguity. An initial assertion that every binary-mask vertex must be an edge midpoint failed; that false assumption was corrected by separating these source-cell roles. Their maximum trilinear field-level residual is0.125. Every triangle barycentre was independently sampled against the original binary field: maximum level residual0.208335876 for both classes, mean0.045259651artery/0.040766094vein. These dimensionless field residuals describe triangulation approximation, not geometric distance, voxel calibration, histology or anatomical accuracy. Complete source sample correspondence and numerical topology do not approve native tissue boundaries.

An all-triangle static3D render was inspected for both classes in the ignored cache, preserving the full geometry and explicitly labelling source array axes. Source masks, extracted geometry and all-triangle display provenance are bound by hashes; no patient laterality or millimetres are inferred. `source-surface-review.json` records the results and cache manifest hash; `source-surface-display-review.json` binds the complete render to that extraction manifest.

The alternative ImageVessel source was checked through its primary Kaggle API, datasetversion2. Its actual license is CC BY-NC4.0, contradicting the secondary catalog's Apache2.0 label. The primary description states that most scans contain annotations for only the operated left or right lung, with a few bilateral cases. Neither commercial reuse nor complete bilateral branch geometry is inferred. No scan files were downloaded from that candidate. The actual repository metadata hash and source limits are preserved in `pulmonary-native-candidate-review.json`.
