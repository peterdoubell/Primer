# Original sinonasal lesion source figures

Original Figures 2–14 from [Radiologic overview of sinonasal lesions](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11392720/) are preserved whole and unchanged. The original article XML expressly grants CC BY 4.0. All complete publisher JPEG MD5 values, file SHA256, decoded-pixel SHA256 and dimensions were checked, and each complete image was visually inspected. Figure 1 is withheld: the illustrator credit explicitly describes permission for use in that article; broader derivative/distribution scope is unresolved. The illustrations are not repackaged under an assumed blanket grant.

MRI sequence and plane roles are attributed to the original captions; their acquisition headers are unavailable. Figures 4 and 7 retain separate CT panels. Figure 7's caption calls panel F PET, but the displayed whole panel is fused PET/CT; it remains a nuclear medicine comparator with that explicit subtype. Tracer, SUV and metabolic specificity are not inferred. No source CT, MRI or nuclear panel is treated as an interchangeable modality or independently registered native series.

Figures 5, 7, 8 and 9 show DWI without a supplied ADC map. Their publication statements about restriction are retained as source claims; no new restricted-diffusion finding, ADC measurement or unique histology is established from DWI brightness. Figure 8's immunohistology is publication text, not image-derived molecular evidence. Figure 9 A/B/C and D/E/F are separate rhabdomyosarcoma and meningioma examples, not one patient progression. Figure 13's possibly pleomorphic-adenoma wording remains uncertain.

Two caption disagreements are exposed without altering source pixels or captions. Figure 12 labels B sagittal and C coronal; displayed B is coronal and C sagittal. No plane-specific coverage is granted. Figure 14's heading says dermoid, its body says desmoid and it uses “coraxial” for B. The original wording uncertainty remains explicit, without claiming a unique diagnosis, a verified plane or complete native tract.

The figures show source-local signal, enhancement and lesion relationships. They do not prove complete acquired extent, every nerve or wall, independently calibrated dimensions, native laterality, age, drainage physiology or clinical readiness. All anatomical reviews remain pending and structure coverage remains empty. They are not registered to the separate NasalSeg masks or source models. Original author credit, DOI, captions, grants and source pointers remain in the runtime gallery and the provenance JSON in `docs/sinonasal-lesion-source-review/`.
