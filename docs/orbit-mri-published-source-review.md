# Original orbital MRI and compartment source figures

Sixteen complete unchanged MRI figures and one original compartment schematic from [Magnetic Resonance Imaging in Orbital Pathologies: A Pictorial Review](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6095049/) are packaged. Actual original XML grants CC BY 4.0; no separate figure-credit restriction was found. Complete author, DOI, grant, original captions and source pointers remain attached. All publisher JPEG MD5 values, complete file/pixel hashes and dimensions were checked, and all 17 complete figures were visually inspected. Figure 1 remains Schematic, not MRI or a native 3D source.

Original source case ages and sexes are retained. Figure 2 is a four-year-old girl and Figure 7 a seven-year-old boy; these remain paediatric cases. The other source patients are adults. No age or case identity is transferred to the current reader patient. Each figure is a separate source example; different sequences/stills do not imply a full registered volume, diagnosis confirmed for a new patient or physiological function.

Source caption limits are exposed without changing pixels or captions. Figure 2 does not explicitly letter-pair every sequence description. Figure 8 has an unreported sequence/contrast role for displayed b. Figure 12 references b for both pre/postcontrast descriptions and does not reference the displayed third panel c; new phase/quantitative enhancement is not assigned. Figure 6 b, Figure 13 a, Figure 16 a/b and Figure 17 b/c have caption-plane descriptions that disagree with the displayed views. These disagreements are retained; no plane-specific coverage is granted.

Figure 4's dimensions and histopathology remain publication statements, not independently calibrated measurements or new tissue evidence. Figure 17 retains the historic source cavernous-hemangioma wording without inferring unique identity, flow or progressive fill-in from static enhancement. No ADC, pressure, vision, motility, neural function or MRI-safety clearance is invented from these images.

Original resolution and annotations are preserved. The larger original PDF image masters replace the HTML thumbnails: MRI figures are 1331 pixels wide and the schematic 649 pixels wide. Original JPEG streams or exact indexed-RGB PDF sample expansion were independently checked without upscaling or enhancement. Numbered HTML/PDF figure identity was visually checked and compared quantitatively; PDF object order places Figure17 before14–16, so explicit mapping is used. Raw acquisition resolution remains unverified. Selected stills and the schematic do not establish every globe layer, muscle/tendon/nerve/sheath/canal/vascular or intracranial interface. All anatomical reviews remain pending with empty structure coverage. Replaced scoped investigation media are archived; other investigations and lessons are unchanged. Full high-fidelity images, schematics and native models plus independent clinical validation remain outstanding.
