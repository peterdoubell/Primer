# Pancreatic cyst reporting scope and framework corrections

Expanded `ra.pancreatic-cysts` to **183 targets and 549 image/schematic/model obligations**. The scope covers whole acquired host pancreas, each actual cyst/locule, complete walls/septa, internal contents, suspected nodules/solid components, scars/calcifications, main/accessory/branch ducts and actual communication, host parenchyma, biliary/vascular/adjacent-organ interfaces, source modality/phase/sequence evidence, suspected cyst-type examples and actual altered/intervention anatomy. Prototype entries require patient/source-specific instantiation and specialist review; parent pancreas or normal atlas labels do not supply positive nodules, debris or presumed neoplasm anatomy.

Risk, histology, symptoms, fitness, cytology and operative eligibility remain functional evidence requirements, not properties inferred from a 3D mesh. MRI/MRCP source evidence cannot be borrowed from CT, and source gland/duct geometry cannot prove enhancement or exact cyst histology.

Corrected concrete shared-lesson/reporting shortcuts:

- Replaced the bare main-duct `>8 mm` malignancy indicator with explicitly qualified presumed-IPMN assessment under **Kyoto 2024**. Selected duct/nodule descriptors retain the named framework; the overview is not its complete criteria/management algorithm.
- The feature-count quiz now explicitly uses **Fukuoka 2017**, retaining its answer of three without calling the complete Kyoto 2024 set identical.
- Duct communication is reported as an observed interface and diagnostic possibility, not standalone proof of branch-duct IPMN. Nonvisualization may be a limitation.
- Actual adequate enhancement/subtraction evidence is required for a suspected mural nodule; position, a single slice, wall partial volume or artifact are not a reliable substitute.
- Serous morphology favours a diagnosis without proving histology or a universal no-follow-up rule. The MCN/IPMN comparison no longer makes cyst type alone an operative directive; selected presumed MCN may follow a conservative pathway under the named framework.

Primary framework references are preserved in the effective reader: [International evidence-based Kyoto IPMN guidelines, 2024](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.pan.2023.12.009) and [European pancreatic cystic neoplasm guidelines, 2018](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5890653/). These references support the limited corrections; they are not represented as a complete newly reconciled current clinical algorithm. Remaining shared pancreatic solid/cystic lesson claims, other neoplasm management, full surveillance criteria and independent specialist review remain explicitly pending.

The loader ignored those fields. The entry is now wrapped correctly, and its full effective contract/source snapshots were refreshed. A regression checks the **effective reader**, not just saved JSON, for the Atlanta and AGA primary references and the added clinical-severity pitfall. The shared solid-pancreatic source contract was refreshed for the modified shared reading; its target scope is not silently changed.
