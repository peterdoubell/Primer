# LiverHccSeg MRI source attribution

Gross, M., Arora, S., Huber, S., Kücükkaya, A. S., & Onofrey, J. A. LiverHccSeg: A Publicly Available Multiphasic MRI Dataset with Liver and HCC Tumor Segmentations and Inter-Rater Agreement Analysis (version 1.1). https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.8179129

Data paper: https://doi.org/10.1016/j.dib.2023.109662

The corrected derived dataset and masks identify CC BY 4.0. Original MRI source: Erickson, B. J., Kirk, S., Lee, Y., et al. Radiology Data from The Cancer Genome Atlas Liver Hepatocellular Carcinoma [TCGA-LIHC] collection. https://doi.org/10.7937/K9/TCIA.2016.IMMQW8UQ — CC BY 3.0 as recorded on the current TCIA collection page. Original-image and added-annotation attribution/versions remain separate.

Review figures select native source arterial MRI sections, apply a display-only percentile window and explicit radiological horizontal ordering with physical RAS axes. Marked figures retain each source rater independently. No source voxels, masks, registered data or stored geometry are edited; no boundary consensus, source phase feature, diagnostic eligibility or clinical approval is inferred. Author-generated registered derivatives are not displayed as original acquired anatomy.

Raw MRI archives and case arrays stay in ignored research storage. No reader asset or model is promoted. Required attribution and the sampling, rater, registration and clinical context limits must accompany any later permitted derivative.
