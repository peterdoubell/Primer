# Original tinnitus clinical source intake

The seven complete published figures in [Pegge et al.](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5263210/) were matched to their original PDF image objects after inspecting PDF pages 4–8 and each extracted master. The original XML specifies CC BY 4.0; no figure or supplement permissions/attribution exception was found. The color-coded 4D-CTA reformat credits Rashindra Manniesing and Midas Meijs of the Diagnostic Image Analysis Group. Preserve that credit along with all four article authors, the source, license link and modification statement.

The 1500- or 2031-pixel PDF masters have higher resolution than the numbered HTML JPEGs. They retain each original decoded grayscale or RGB sample and the original RGB ICC profile. The master is the complete published figure, with original arrows, panel separators and publication field of view. No crop, sharpening, brightness normalization, new annotation or reconstruction was applied. Figure 2 differs in encoded contrast from its HTML thumbnail (RGB thumbnail RMS 17.919); inspection of its PDF page and both whole figures establishes the binding. This discrepancy is retained, not corrected by changing pixels or relaxing the automatic threshold.

Figures cover right temporal AVM (T2/MRA), right sigmoid sinus dAVF (4D-CTA/MIP, processed color-coded reformat and selective external-carotid DSA), aberrant ICA/persistent stapedial artery source context (CT), glomus tympanicum (CT/MRI), glomus jugulotympanicum (CT/MRI), fenestral and cochlear otosclerosis examples (CT), and left cerebellopontine-angle “Meningeoma” source context (MRI). Original unlettered panels are indexed by row/column without inserting letters into the image. Do not relabel processed projection graphics as native 3D meshes or full acquired volumes.

Age, sex, acquisition date and exact contrast timing are not reported for these cases. Laterality is recorded only where the source caption supplies it. Do not treat Figures 1–7 as one patient or assume that the two otosclerosis examples are from one patient. The source lesion names do not establish histology, tinnitus causation, pressure, calibrated flow or a new-patient treatment decision. The article itself describes absent foramen spinosum as an indirect sign; that finding alone cannot establish a persistent stapedial artery in a new case.

Both original AVI supplements are preserved in the ignored source cache and publisher-MD5 verified. Their captions explicitly connect them to the right sigmoid sinus fistula of Figure 2. Each has 12 encoded 1024×1024 frames, Microsoft Video 1 encoding, 5-bit RGB channel precision, a 3 fps playback rate and four seconds of encoded playback. All frames were inspected in order using separate analysis contact sheets. Video 1 is a lateral 4D-CTA display with original source-display overlay text; Video 2 is a selective angiographic sequence. Neither playback rate nor frame ordering supplies an independently verified acquisition clock, native voxel grid, injection-to-frame interval, pressure measurement or complete fistula classification. Retain every frame and original precision if these are subsequently packaged; analysis sheets are not clinical replacement media.

AVI frame presentation/decode times run from 0.000000 to 3.666667 seconds in encoded thirds of a second, each with an encoded duration of 0.333333 seconds. These are playback timestamps. The first movie's frame 7 retains the source display text “MIP Segmented”, “7 of 12”, “VR: Inverted MIP” and “LAO105 CRA12”; this is a processed/inverted CT angiographic projection, not DSA, raw CT, or a verified 3D mesh. The second movie is DSA; selective external-carotid injection is supplied by the Figure 2 legend. No pulse/ECG/sound synchronization, producer intermodal registration, absolute/bolus-relative clock, or proof that all 12 display frames are distinct acquired timepoints is supplied. Both movies are associated with the same Figure 2 source case by their captions, without becoming evidence for a current patient.

The original [PMC8917066 manuscript](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8917066/) grant provides text-mining/fair-use access rather than a reusable commercial image license. Its five figures are held; no restricted pixels or full captions were repackaged. Attempts to retrieve PMC3719451 through the OA S3 listing, OA utility and Europe PMC full-text endpoint did not establish a reuse grant. A missing or failed endpoint is not evidence of a restrictive or permissive license.

The report and visual review are source-intake evidence. Every clinical approval, structure coverage and runtime promotion remains pending. No fidelity requirement is closed by publication licensing or a successful byte-integrity check.
