# Original acute-aortic CT figures and conceptual diagram

Reviewed 6 October 2026. Nine complete original CT-bearing figures and the separately typed conceptual Figure 2 from [Ueda et al., ECG-gated AAS pictorial review](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3505562/), DOI 10.1007/s13244-012-0195-7, are preserved locally. Original NLM XML explicitly grants CC BY 4.0; copyright/authors and complete captions remain attached. Publisher XML/PDF/figure checksums, PDF object placement and independent original encoded/pixel readback are verified. Metadata SHA-256 is 4cbde10caededb5e29c67669144f339810ea3729c582046a479923e83f5499fe.

Five original grayscale JPEG streams are byte-identical. Four RGB clinical figures and the conceptual chart preserve original PDF decoded samples and ICC profiles in lossless PNGs. All ten source artworks were visually inspected; labels, arrows, orientation markers, embedded signatures, blank panel spaces and complete original compositions remain unchanged. No artificial anatomy or patient 3D geometry is generated.

Source phase/gating is not inferred where captions do not name it. Cardiac gating is distinct from contrast-bolus timing; MIP/MPR/VR artwork does not supply original voxel geometry. Each numbered clinical figure has its own source-case identifier; cases are not joined by similar appearances. Figure 2 has no patient or clinical CT panels.

Clinical figures are linked to actual extent, wall/lumen, branch or comparison steps. Figure 3 offers local brachiocephalic/root context, not complete coronary/visceral/access or perfusion coverage. The complication step retains its existing references because this selected source does not supply complete haemorrhage/physiological evidence. The conceptual chart is explicitly selected for the introduction and Diagram tab. Original captions and historical nomenclature stay separate from the corrected reporting guidance.
