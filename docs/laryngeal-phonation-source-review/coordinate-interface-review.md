# Independent source-coordinate review — Stiff phase 01

This is a read-only review of the original matched NRRD and Blender-exported STL from [Zenodo record 19629778](https://zenodo.org/records/19629778). The source arrays and all 29,326 original surface faces remain unchanged. No fitted transform, repair, threshold optimisation, alignment, smoothing, or fragment deletion was performed. Reproduction is `coordinate_interface_audit.py`; all numeric results are in `coordinate-interface-independent-audit.json`.

## The affine disagreement is real for the same published array indices

The primary NRRD fields declare LPS, with pitch p = 0.3839285671710968, directions diag(+p, −p, −p), and origin (−39.095104217529297, +39.526317596435547, +42.801071166992188). Under the convention-only change from LPS to RAS, directions become diag(−p, +p, −p), and origin becomes (+39.095104217529297, −39.526317596435547, +42.801071166992188).

The inherited `srow_x/y/z:=` strings instead give directions diag(+0.383929, +0.383929, +0.383929) with nearly the same RAS origin. Thus x and z directions conflict after accounting for coordinate convention; rounded values alone cannot explain this. Across the eight original index corners, the maximum Euclidean separation is 242.1589699643108 source numeric units. Reversing x and z array indices could change axis directions but would also require origin translation, absent from these inherited strings. The original NIfTI and conversion pipeline were not supplied, so the historical cause is unproven.

This distinction follows the primary [NRRD specification](https://teem.sourceforge.net/nrrd/format.html): `space`, `space origin`, and `space directions` define the published array geometry; `srow_x:=...` is a generic key/value entry rather than a standard NRRD orientation field. The primary [NIfTI header definition](https://github.com/NIFTI-Imaging/nifti_clib/blob/master/niftilib/nifti1.h) defines its affine world convention as RAS and the sform multiplication explicitly. Its retrieved source copy is `primary-nifti1-header.h`. The NRRD contains no explicit space units and inherited NIfTI `xyzt_units` is zero, so these differences are stated in source numeric units; the source paper supplies nominal millimetre-scale reconstruction context, not independent calibration.

## Original geometry and image correspondence

Original NRRD sample-centre bounds in LPS numeric coordinates are x[−39.0951042, 46.5209663], y[−46.0897529, 39.5263176], z[−42.8149993, 42.8010712]. Original STL bounds are x[−5.5922146, 10.0681934], y[−10.2701645, 11.5363665], z[−22.0828571, 7.4796443]. Treating original STL coordinates as LPS places all 14,831 unique vertices inside the source grid, spanning original indices x[87.2633414, 128.0532418], y[72.9040596, 129.7024664], z[91.9999966, 169.0000012].

All eight fixed origin-centred sign interpretations were tested, with no translation or scale fitting. All vertices fit inside the image for each interpretation, so containment cannot determine orientation. At every original triangle centroid, trilinear interpolation of the unchanged MR array was evaluated at the centroid and on both sides along the original face normal at fixed distances ±0.383928567 and ±0.8 source numeric units. Results were weighted by original triangle area, including all four components. These samples are derived interpolations, not acquired intensity samples or independent tissue labels.

The unchanged LPS interpretation is the strongest of these bounded hypotheses. At ±0.8 units, 77.5431% of total surface area has two side samples on opposite sides of the filename value 0.0058. The alternate RAS interpretation (flip x/y into primary LPS) gives 11.2270%; all remaining sign hypotheses give 5.3342–18.6112%. The area-weighted mean absolute centroid-signal residual relative to that same filename value is 0.00266794 for LPS, versus 0.00787485 for RAS. At one exported-grid pitch the LPS straddling fraction is only 30.1893%, demonstrating that this is not a proof of exact isosurface derivation. The filename threshold has not been independently confirmed as the full segmentation rule or anatomical ground truth.

The existing three original-index plane overlays (axes 0/1/2, indices 107/101/130) were visually inspected. Under the LPS hypothesis, source surface contours broadly follow the visible central airspace at the displayed reconstruction scale. Source surface ends truncate this airspace while MRI signal continues beyond the surface. The display uses original index planes, nearest-neighbour presentation, and the recorded signal window. It provides no labelled cartilage, tissue-layer or swallowing-function evidence.

## Supported interpretation and remaining hold

The article reports use of 3D Slicer for geometric analysis; [current official Slicer documentation](https://slicer.readthedocs.io/en/5.10/user_guide/data_loading_and_saving.html) says untagged model files default to LPS and [its coordinate guide](https://slicer.readthedocs.io/en/5.10/user_guide/coordinate_systems.html) describes internal RAS versus file LPS. This is consistent with the stronger LPS correspondence, but it does not establish this producer's exact version, export options, Blender import/export transforms, or anatomical correctness. The original STL header identifies Blender 3.4.1 and contains no `SPACE=LPS`/`SPACE=RAS` marker. The archive lists no original segmentation mask or transform file.

The strongest supported statement is therefore: “The author's original Stiff phase-01 airway-interface surface and published MR volume share a matching source phase; interpreting unchanged surface coordinates as LPS produces substantially stronger image-interface correspondence than the seven fixed sign alternatives.” Producer-proven patient registration remains unapproved. An original source surface can be shown alone as a scoped, unregistered phonation/airway reference, with exact source coordinates and all original components/open edges preserved. A patient-aligned overlay must continue to disclose the LPS assumption. Do not replace the NRRD primary affine with inherited srow strings or infer absent fine tissue layers.

The source native MRI resolution is nominal 0.8 mm; the 0.383928567 exported pitch is a zero-filled grid. This is sustained phonation in one subject, with phase bins aggregated over a prolonged scan, not swallowing/VFSS or ten consecutive live frames. Neither registration nor clinical/commercial anatomical coverage is approved by this audit.
