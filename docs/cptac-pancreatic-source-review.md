# Original paired pancreatic CT source acquisition

The [original CPTAC-PDA imaging collection](https://www.cancerimagingarchive.net/collection/cptac-pda/) (DOI 10.7937/K9/TCIA.2018.SC20FO18, current collection version 15) and [tumour annotation collection](https://www.cancerimagingarchive.net/analysis-result/cptac-pda-tumor-annotations/) (DOI 10.7937/BW9V-BX61, annotation version 2 updated 20 October 2025) identify CC BY 4.0 for the selected source series. Original imaging and added-annotation grants remain separate. The older TCIA wiki reports an earlier CC BY 3.0 image grant; that historical page is not silently substituted for the current selected-series metadata. Source page/manifest/CSV/NBIA metadata hashes are preserved.

Case C3L-02112 was selected because current annotation metadata explicitly references two same-study original CT series labelled `ART THINS` and `VENOUS A/P THINS`. Each original CT and its own RTSTRUCT match source case/study identities and selected-series licence fields. Both metadata rows share the tracking ID `PANCREAS - 2` and a tracking UID, but neither the label number nor ROI names establish whole-pancreas segmentation or lesion count. A negative assessment/seed point is not substituted for a segmentation.

All four original archives passed ZIP CRC and publisher per-file MD5. The two CT series contain 365 and 713 objects (193,178,242 and 377,363,608 uncompressed DICOM bytes); their separate RTSTRUCTs contain one object each (178,416 and 207,436 bytes). Every original member has a local SHA-256 fingerprint, and exact publisher-manifest membership/count/byte totals are checked. Raw archives stay in ignored research storage and are not uploaded into the reader.

Each original CT is one distinct regular acquisition with 512 × 512 pixels, 0.703125 mm in-plane spacing, 0.625 mm slice thickness and 0.625 mm physical centre spacing. Original InstanceNumber order descends by −0.625 mm, consistent with the preserved negative spacing tag. The geometry audit uses increasing actual positions for its analysis indices and records the original ordering separately; no tag is corrected and no voxels are resampled. There is no mixed/deduplicated volume.

The source-labelled arterial acquisition records time 114104.584535 and the venous-labelled acquisition 114147.324919, a 42.740384-second field difference. Contrast-agent fields are empty and injection start/stop times are absent. Those facts and series names do not independently prove pancreatic/portal phase adequacy or relative injection timing. Both source series declare the same coordinate frame, which does not prove motion-free anatomical registration or justify fitting/averaging one annotation into the other.

All 43 and 49 CLOSED_PLANAR source contours reference objects in their own acquired CT series. Every point lies on its exact referenced source plane (maximum offset zero) and within acquired image bounds. Original source names, codes, manual generation category and ROI-volume fields are retained. No contour is interpreted as a whole pancreas, nerve, duct, vessel wall or complete staging examination. Limited target annotation protocol coverage does not make other structures normal.

An important cross-representation identity discrepancy is preserved. The two CSV rows share tracking UID `2.25.213034165703120324893647141780714047966`, while both original RTSTRUCTs share `2.25.165084154860889254540124087221208413343`. Each representation is internally consistent across the two source roles, but their UIDs differ. No identifier is overwritten to manufacture correspondence. Source CT/contour references remain directly verified per object; metadata-to-DICOM tracking reconciliation, original diagnosis/treatment context and complete anatomical correspondence remain held.

Ten source-selection/reference regressions, six time-field checks and combined archive-integrity checks passed. They reject seed/negative substitutes, wrong tracking/study/case/treatment state, unrelated/noncommercial series and duplicated CT references, and preserve actual DICOM time values. Geometry is audited on every acquired header and contour, separately from software fixtures. Source images, contour raster/surface derivation and clinical anatomical review remain next work. Full all-radiology scope and current 12,399 known obligations remain unchanged.
