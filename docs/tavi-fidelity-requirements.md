# TAVI/TAVR: complete reporting-scope draft

Reviewed 6 October 2026. The five-field effective planning reference is bound to **54 groups, 454 leaves and 1,362 image/schematic/model obligations**. This covers annular plane/contours, LVOT, individual cusps/sinuses/commissures, bicuspid/other variants, calcium/septal/support interfaces, both coronary ostia/courses, combined obstruction geometry, STJ/ascending/angulation, full aortic and bilateral iliofemoral routes, candidate alternatives and neighbours, prior graft/devices, conditional SAVR/TAVR/virtual-valve geometry, functional context and actual acquisition/planning limitations.

Every actual site, variant and candidate route requires instantiation. Existing generic root/aorta models and source rights do not supply all structures or establish device suitability. Simulation assumptions remain separate from measured source anatomy. Root fields do not stand in for unacquired access paths.

The worksheet now qualifies adapted basal landmarks, exact available phase/quality, device-specific sizing, actual scoring acquisition/method, integrated obstruction anatomy and full route/device coverage. It does not infer a valid Agatston score from contrast calcium, functional stenosis from morphology, obstruction from height alone, or access/device suitability from a universal diameter cutoff.

Source review includes the effective Radiology Assistant reference, [ESCR consensus extracts](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7160220/), [SCCT consensus extracts](https://www.jacc.org/doi/10.1016/j.jcmg.2018.12.003), the original step-by-step CT source and conditional native/SAVR/TAVR planning extracts. Full current consensus/device instructions and multidisciplinary clinical approval remain outstanding; excerpts are not a full guideline review.

Reproduce with the expand_tavi_requirements module. Tests bind all five report fields, retain separately reported sides/device interfaces, reject parent-only model coverage and keep source/scoring/heart-team limits explicit.

Full goal remains active: **37,833 known-floor obligations, 81 unexpanded investigations, 106 unreconciled curriculum surfaces and unknown total**. Runtime rights: **708 unique images, 265 cleared and 443 unverified**. No clinical/model approval follows from the draft or those counts.
