# Cardiovascular device reporting fidelity — 6 October 2026

The actual `ra.cardiovascular-devices` module now requires identification of every component and intended device-specific interface, rather than normal presets or a generic heart shape. Five unsupported normal statements are removed. Templates require active/abandoned/retained components, actual routes and connections, inflow/outflow/landing geometry, adjacent tissue and source-based function/comparison limits. Generator shape or an MRI marker alone is not complete-system MRI clearance. A projected balloon marker or generic distance below the arch is not a universal safe device position. Static geometry does not prove electrical capture, sensing, shock, pump, valve or haemodynamic function.

The reproducible inventory contains 92 groups/894 leaves (2,682 image/schematic/model obligations) across generators/headers/pockets, transvenous/conduction-system/epicardial/subcutaneous/abandoned leads, leadless fixation, valve prostheses and repair, occluders, stents/grafts, LVAD inflow/pump/outflow/driveline, left/right temporary pumps, balloon pumps, pulmonary-artery/other catheters/support conduits, surgical material, bilateral access routes, native cardiac/vascular interfaces and complication compartments. Each actual model/version/connection/variant must be instantiated; the inventory is a known floor, not an exhaustive current device catalogue. No modality is assumed to resolve every item, and any MRI use remains conditional on actual system/protocol assessment.

Sources reviewed: the [Radiology Assistant module](https://radiologyassistant.nl/cardiovascular/devices/cardiovascular-devices); original XML body/tables/captions of [Mathew et al., chest cardiac devices review](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6837806/) (DOI 10.4102/sajr.v23i1.1730, actual CC BY 4.0); relevant original XML lead-course/complication passages of [Torres-Ayala et al.](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4286824/); and relevant complete-system/abandoned-lead passages of the [2024 SCMR consensus](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11211236/) (DOI 10.1016/j.jocmr.2024.100995). This is not a full contemporary manufacturer-IFU or MRI-protocol audit. Metadata/XML hashes, licences and review limitations are recorded in the source proof.

Two source problems are explicit. The Radiology Assistant LVAD paragraph calls the apical cannula outflow; actual inflow and arterial outflow are separately required and must follow device/procedure evidence. The Torres-Ayala review's original licence URL is CC BY-NC-SA 3.0 despite broader attribution prose; none of its images is commercially cleared or reused. The [Mathew erratum](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8661294/) (DOI 10.4102/sajr.v25i1.2256) changes Original Research to Review Article; it adds no anatomical/image approval. The subsequent [original figure review](cardiovascular-device-published-source-review.md) preserves 20 complete radiographs and excludes permission-only Figures 13/21 after inspecting their printed in-pixel notices. Independent anatomical/current-device-system validation remains pending. PMC7758755 snapshot retrieval was unavailable (S3 404, EuropePMC XML 500); no uninspected content or rights was borrowed.

The generic heart model explicitly lacks actual implanted devices and patient-specific interfaces; it grants no device anatomy coverage. The initial reporting expansion added no media; the subsequent original figure packet replaces two unverified remote items with 20 locally preserved radiographs. No actual device mesh geometry is added. Existing images remain governed by their actual rights/clinical evidence. Independent image, schematic and model coverage, precise device/manufacturer geometry, calibration, full current clinical review and commercial readiness remain unproven.

Full gate still fails: 137 investigations, 106 unreconciled curriculum surfaces, 79 unexpanded investigations and at least 42,717 representation obligations. Total full scope remains unknown.
