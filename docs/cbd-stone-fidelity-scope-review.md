# Common-bile-duct stone ultrasound fidelity scope

The common-bile-duct stone ultrasound investigation now has 187 draft reportable targets and 561 image/schematic/model obligations. Every new representation remains missing/unverified. Parent duct envelopes and a complete walkthrough cannot represent all actual segments, filling defects, wall boundaries, vascular interfaces or complications.

Targets retain common-hepatic/cystic-junction/supraduodenal/retroduodenal/intrapancreatic/intramural regions; acquired hilar/intrahepatic branches; pancreaticobiliary outlet, papilla and host interfaces; each stone/material margin and acoustic region; actual calibre/transition and unassessed-field boundaries; portal/hepatic artery discrimination sites; gallbladder neck/cystic impaction and compression; acquired pancreas, infection/collection and postoperative/device anatomy. Actual CT/MRI adjuncts are separate from ultrasound-specific evidence.

The checklist, templates, walkthrough and reading contract are fingerprinted. Each case still requires branch/variant/material/site instantiation and adequate source sampling. Transabdominal, endoscopic and intraoperative ultrasound are distinct examination subtypes; neither performance claims nor field coverage is automatically transferred between them. Real source observations are needed for shadowing, positional mobility and Doppler discrimination, and a playable cine file alone does not establish these clinical functions.

The prior walkthrough's normal preset asserted a complete course to the papilla and a stone-free duct even though its own guidance warned against clearing an unseen distal duct. The revised preset retains actually visualised segments/outlet and unassessed regions. A negative material statement is confined to visualised regions and explicitly does not establish whole-duct clearance. Calibre interpretation remains context-dependent, and pancreatic/inflammatory fields retain clinical/laboratory assessment instead of an imaging-only syndrome verdict.

The original [Radiology Assistant source](https://radiologyassistant.nl/abdomen/biliary-system/lk-jg-2), [ASGE 2019 guideline](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.gie.2018.10.001) and [TG18 cholangitis criteria](https://doi.org/10.1002/jhbp.512) were checked for scope boundaries. The reader now delivers those three references. No diameter-only ERCP algorithm or universal negative-ultrasound rule is introduced. Full current clinical/management reconciliation remains required; guideline availability is not a licence to reuse its figures.
