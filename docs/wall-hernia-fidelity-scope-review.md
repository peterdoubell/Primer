# Abdominal-wall hernia CT fidelity scope — 2026-10-05

The abdominal-wall hernia investigation now defines 337 draft targets and 1,011 clinical-image, schematic and model obligations. Source-resolved wall regions/layers, bilateral rectus/oblique/transversus muscles and sheaths, midline/semilunar/groin landmarks, each fascial defect margin, sac/neck/content entry/exit, mesenteric/vascular and host interfaces, repair/mesh/fixation/scar, collections/tracts and measurement compartments are separate. Every actual site still needs source-specific instantiation and independent review. Groin/lumbar/stoma and finer fascial/vessel regions require actual field and relevance; a parent wall model cannot supply their detail automatically.

The five presets now retain adequate, limited and unassessed alternatives rather than excluding all hernia, mesh or complications. Diastasis and a true defect are separate observations and can coexist. Source aperture dimensions, sac dimensions and muscle measures remain distinct, with actual planes, posture and calibration recorded. Static contents do not establish reducibility, every adhesion, physiological compliance or clinical incarceration; actual clinical/dynamic findings and unavailable sources remain explicit.

Loss-of-domain values require actual hernia sac volume (HSV), residual abdominal cavity volume (ACV), complete compartment boundaries and the actual segmentation/approximation method. HSV/ACV and HSV/(HSV+ACV) are different definitions; the vague percentage of “visceral volume” is removed. The new volume measurement is linked to the contents reporting step. No universal numerical threshold, automatic volumetric calculation or guarantee of fascial closure, pressure/respiratory tolerance or operative outcome is introduced. Missing volumetry remains not performed/unassessed.

Source visibility does not guarantee full mesh material, repair plane or fixation detail. Nonvisualisation is not absence; actual operative history and limitations remain necessary. Repair-region fluid/gas is an observation rather than independent microbiological infection proof. Source CT contrast, bowel reference, sac fluid/gas/inflammation and tract endpoints support interpretation with unresolved viability/extent retained. Prompt communication of suspected strangulation/high-grade obstruction is preserved.
