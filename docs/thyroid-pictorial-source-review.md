# Original thyroid ultrasound figures

All 21 figures from Pires et al., “The 2017 ACR TI-RADS: pictorial essay”, [PMC8864691](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8864691/), were acquired from original publisher metadata. XML, PDF and every numbered figure match the supplied publisher MD5 values. The original XML grants CC BY 4.0; no separately attributed or restricted figure element was found. This clears the article content, not native patient volumes or clinical certification.

Larger PDF image masters are preserved with every original colour-channel sample. The PDF does not store figures in numerical order: the first three image objects are Figures 3, 1 and 2; other reordering affects Figures 8–11 and 14–15. All six rendered pages containing the 21 figures were visually inspected, and their caption/placement bindings are recorded. An automatic assumption that object order equals figure number would attach incorrect source descriptions.

Twenty masters use single-component DeviceN `/Black` JPEGs. Every one of the 256 tint inputs was evaluated against the original restricted PDF function, confirming output CMYK (0,0,0,t). An ordinary grayscale JPEG browser interpretation would reverse the source brightness. Original encoded streams and decoded tint-channel hashes are retained. Figure 10 uses an indexed DeviceCMYK palette; all four expanded channels were independently written/read as CMYK TIFF without alterations. RGB display conversion and device colour calibration remain separate unapproved steps. Preliminary grayscale inversion/CMYK preview files are research diagnostics, not published source masters or enhancement claims.

The original captions and source scores are preserved. Selected still frames do not establish full nodules or 3D internal tissue boundaries. In particular, a halo is not an independently identified histological capsule; source-labelled extrathyroidal extension in Figures 12/20 is not new proof of microscopic or adjacent-organ invasion; and Figure 17's shadowed-content scoring assumptions do not reveal the hidden physical material. Separate figures are not assumed to be the same patient, serial examinations, matched planes or supplied histology. Original calipers, scales and labels are source annotations, not newly calibrated measurements. The examples remain source educational scores, distinct from current patient-specific management applicability.

Original PDF/HTML appearances have different encoded contrast and resolution. Thumbnail comparisons helped locate candidates but did not approve anatomy or justify changing brightness to fit another version. Full colour-channel preservation and source-page inspection are the authoritative acquisition checks. No image, schematic or model obligation is closed, and nothing from this acquisition is published in the runtime yet. The next step is independently checked display interpretation followed by source-specific reader integration; all 21 figures remain in scope.

The display pipeline now preserves each complete source image object and its colour definitions in an isolated PDF, retains the original FOGRA39 output intent, and renders with Poppler into an explicit embedded sRGB display profile. Output dimensions equal original pixel dimensions, using a 72-DPI page sized in points to the original pixels; there is no post-render spatial resampling or anatomical editing. The RGB output is explicitly a derived PDF display, not unchanged native samples. A direct LittleCMS CMYK conversion did not match DeviceN PDF rendering (Figure 1 mean RGB difference 11.95, maximum 163) and is not used as a substitute. Source ultrasound acquisition and diagnostic display calibration remain unverified. All 21 derived displays were inspected in a complete comparison sheet against previously inspected source pages; independent full-size colour/anatomical acceptance and runtime integration remain outstanding.

All 21 reviewed derived displays are now integrated locally, replacing the archived inherited thyroid investigation figures. Complete source captions and licensed rendering attribution are retained; the displayed captions lead with the source feature and keep limitations separate. Figure 11 keeps its two source panels without independent registration claims; Figure 17 explicitly leaves shadowed physical material unresolved. Images are linked to background, nodule, feature and source-extension steps, without automatically recommending management. No current node depiction or complete gland/adjacent/3D coverage is inferred. All 21 local API image hashes match packaged display bytes; the 390 × 844 mobile gallery and Figure 1 popup were visually inspected, with 1033 × 725 natural dimensions and no browser errors. Eighty-nine targeted tests passed.
