# Original upstream duct and vessel geometry inspection

Inspected every vertex, normal and triangular face record in all **25 original upstream elements**, totalling **26,488 triangles**. [Source acquisition and rights](bodyparts-pancreatic-upstream-review.md) establish the separate version-4.3 manifest, actual returned IS-A/PART-OF representation identities and upstream CC BY-SA 2.1 Japan terms. Original OBJ arrays and ZIP remain ignored/offline; no mesh has been fitted to HRA or the acquired CT case, repaired, smoothed or promoted into runtime.

The OBJ reader preserves original face winding, vertex positions, normal values and separate position/normal indices. Relative OBJ indices are interpreted at their actual source record position. Unsupported face attributes, non-triangle faces and out-of-bounds/nonfinite geometry are rejected rather than silently triangulated or normalized. Actual position/index/normal arrays and complete source-file fingerprints are retained in the [geometry review](bodyparts-pancreatic-upstream-review/original-obj-geometry-review.json).

Topology is reported twice: original exported indices, and an **analysis-only exact-position index**. This distinguishes export seams from physical open boundaries without welding the source model. Original source-normal seams are not removed. The pancreatic duct tree has 3,941 vertex records, 3,368 triangles and 2,255 repeated position records. Its exported indices form 484 components with 3,594 boundary edges; exact-position analysis yields one component with no boundary or nonmanifold edges. That is a numerical topology result, not independent duct anatomy, lumen/wall separation, patency or complete main/accessory duct coverage.

All 25 elements have zero zero-area triangles. After exact-position analysis, the posterior cecal artery FJ3553 retains **12 open boundary edges**, while marginal colic artery FJ2025 contains **five disconnected components**. Other elements have one connected component and no exact-position boundary/nonmanifold edges. Source defects or fragments are not capped, bridged, deleted or assigned pathological meaning. Source compound groups retain all constituent elements rather than asserting that a parent label identifies every required local trunk, branch, wall or lumen.

Original header bounds differ from actual vertex bounds in **13 elements**. The largest absolute coordinate difference is **0.1586 mm** for parenchyma FJ1895; the duct-tree discrepancy is **0.0314 mm**. Both declared fields and computed bounds remain separate. No source mesh is translated, scaled or edited to force agreement. These discrepancies do not by themselves establish the original reduction method or acquisition accuracy. Source volume strings remain original assertions, not independently approved tissue or lumen measurements.

The two parenchymal source elements FJ1895 and FJ2629 have different triangle counts (4,890 and 4,272) and slightly different actual bounds. Each remains separate; neither is discarded as a duplicate nor counted as a second pancreas. The coordinate-context figure shows the duct alone and with each original parenchymal element independently. Shared declared source coordinates are retained, but an overlay is not proof of matched donor anatomy, containment, biological interface accuracy or patient CT registration.
