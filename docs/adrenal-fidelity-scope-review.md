# Adrenal fidelity scope and clinical boundaries

The adrenal reader previously had no expanded structure inventory. Its curriculum also silently blended distinct incidentaloma frameworks and presented macroscopic fat and historical washout percentages as definitive diagnoses. The new draft defines 224 reportable targets and 672 image/schematic/model obligations, with no verified representations.

The inventory includes separate right/left gland bodies and limbs, conditional resolved capsule/cortex/medulla, individual suprarenal arterial supply and venous drainage, each lesion component, actual venous thrombus margins, organ interfaces, bilateral pattern, source-corresponding interval change and acquired distant disease. CT, MRI and conditional PET-CT requirements retain their actual modality. Every patient-specific branch, lesion and interface still requires explicit instantiation; this is a known floor, not exhaustive case anatomy.

ESE/ENSAT 2023 and the Seow 2025 proposed true-incidentaloma pathway are distinguished in the lesson, reader and quizzes. The newer proposal is not silently presented as the older guideline. The original reading source is [Radiology Assistant's adrenal update](https://radiologyassistant.nl/abdomen/adrenals/lesion-characterization-1); clinical framework details were checked against [ESE/ENSAT 2023](https://doi.org/10.1093/ejendo/lvad066) and [Seow 2025](https://doi.org/10.1186/s13244-025-02015-4).

Whole-lesion homogeneity, representative true noncontrast sampling and endocrine function remain separate. The lesson no longer calls every 11–20 HU lesion a lipid-poor adenoma requiring no work-up, treats small fat foci as uniquely myelolipoma, or diagnoses adenoma from calculated washout alone. Historical arithmetic answers remain 69.7% and 53.1%, with explicit diagnostic limits. Growth uses the ESE/ENSAT conjunction of >20% and at least 5 mm. A suspicious adrenal mass with venous extension prompts expert staging and endocrine assessment instead of claiming proven carcinoma. Biopsy prerequisites remain distinct from imaging diagnosis.

The generated reasoning plate was rendered at both responsive sizes and the 1600 px image directly inspected. It now distinguishes benign imaging features, endocrine function, paired MRI interpretation and timing/framework limits. The shapes and curve illustrate reasoning; they are not acquired gland anatomy, calibrated signal or an anatomical schematic supplying fine-structure credit.

The corrected growth walkthrough no longer pre-fills prior stability when no comparison is available. The reporting contract, checklist and complete visual descriptors are fingerprinted in the all-radiology scope. Parent-gland models cannot satisfy limb/component requirements; normal anatomy cannot provide positive lesion or thrombus evidence; CT cannot provide chemical-shift MRI or FDG uptake.

Independent clinical review, original acquired images/models for every target, source resolution and rights checks, endocrine/genetic management reconciliation and complete curriculum review remain unfinished. No model was promoted, no new source image was acquired, no coverage approval was granted and no production release is claimed for these local changes.

A direct current-manifest check found that the morphology selection IDs FJ3147/FJ3145 are right/left kidneys and the added extent IDs FJ2038/FJ2046 are right/left renal arteries. There are no adrenal-gland or renal-vein/IVC parts in that source manifest. Both incorrect selections are now explicitly empty; renderer instructions retain schematic positioning only. This removes false anatomical highlighting without granting the missing adrenal/tumour/venous representations.
