# Brain localisation fidelity scope

The deployed reporting contract for `ra.brain-anatomy` now has a draft expansion into 210 anatomical/localisation requirements, each requiring an image, schematic and model (630 obligations). All five checklist fields and their exact wording are preserved. The snapshot hash covers reporting, templates, walkthrough and linked reading. This is requirements work, not clinical approval or new runtime geometry.

Separate right and left requirements cover cortical/deep white-matter regions, caudate segments, pallidal subdivisions, thalamic localisation regions, internal-capsule limbs, cerebellar components, ventricular segments, cisterns, arterial anatomy, tissue territories and venous drainage. Midline requirements include callosal segments, brainstem, vermis, commissural landmarks, CSF spaces and vascular connections. The source-linked hippocampal and pituitary regions remain explicitly conditional.

The investigation's template accepts an arbitrary gyrus/sulcus and cerebellar lobule. The finite inventory therefore cannot close the scope: every reported site and adjacent relevant structure must be instantiated. Broad thalamic regions are not named nuclear segmentations. Cerebellar lobes do not replace individual lobules. Potential extra-axial spaces must not be depicted as normal open fluid cavities. Arterial tissue territories are distinct from vessels, and a normative colour map cannot establish patient-specific blood supply or venous drainage. Angiographic evidence and calibrated image measurements remain separate obligations.

The linked [Brain Anatomy](https://radiologyassistant.nl/neuroradiology/brain/anatomy) and [Vascular territories](https://radiologyassistant.nl/neuroradiology/brain-ischemia/vascular-territories) textual regions were reviewed on 2026-10-01. Their embedded labels, full adjacent anatomy and disease-specific contexts still require reconciliation. These references define scope; their images have not been granted reuse rights by this review.

Existing whole-hemisphere and ventricular meshes have no automatic child coverage. The 23 offline BodyParts3D objects remain source candidates. Gross capsule, caudate, pallidal, thalamic and callosal objects cannot supply their required subdivisions by name alone. Source reduction and boundary validation remain unresolved. See `brain-source-candidate-review.md`.

The all-radiology known floor increases from 5,460 to 6,090 representation obligations; 113 investigations and all 106 curriculum surfaces still require further scope work. The complete denominator remains unknown. The audit records all 630 new obligations as missing and retains the appendix's three partial, unverified image candidates. The completion gate must still fail.
