# Original thyroid geometry candidates

All 102 thyroid masks in the original TotalSegmentator v2.0.1 subset were screened after rechecking the complete archive against its publisher MD5. There are 26 empty annotations, 30 touching a volume boundary and 46 nonempty contained annotations. Empty masks do not prove absent gland tissue. Containment does not prove complete or correct anatomy. The earlier thoracic case s0011 touches the upper volume boundary and cannot establish a complete gland reference. Case s0358 is the first eligible candidate by sorted source ID; normality is not inferred.

The author has released [TotalSegmentator v3.0.0](https://zenodo.org/records/22688904), with corrected labels and 1,939 CT images. Its actual dataset record grants CC BY 4.0. Complete original s0358 CT, thyroid, tracheal, esophageal, bilateral common-carotid, brachiocephalic trunk and bilateral brachiocephalic-vein members were acquired through exact ZIP ranges. All nine original member CRCs pass, and publisher archive size/checksum are unchanged before/after retrieval. The server supplies no entity tag, and the full 37.4 GB archive MD5 was not independently computed. Object immutability and whole-archive validation are explicitly unverified; no checksum assertion is fabricated.

All 306,072,675 original scalar samples agree with an independent reader. Nine volumes share the supplied 255 × 255 × 523 grid and sform, with declared 1.5 mm sampling. This is source dataset sampling, not independently verified native DICOM/HU/physical calibration. Published metadata identify study type `ct pelvis`, trauma and abdomen; it is not relabelled dedicated neck CT. The reviewed CT planes actually contain upper thorax/lower-neck anatomy. Original unmarked/marked planes at source indices 485, 495 and 503 were visually inspected, using a fixed source-value display window without resampling. No pathology, normality or contrast phase is inferred.

Eight original annotation surfaces retain 52,040 triangles. Derivation is each full source binary mask at level 0.5, with original sform only: no padding, fitting, smoothing, decimation, class merge or component deletion. Both carotid annotations reach the upper volume boundary; their open edges remain intact. No invented termination or cap is added. Thyroid source-envelope containment does not independently identify every lobe/isthmus boundary, histological capsule, nodule component or adjacent interface. Tracheal/esophageal/vessel masks are source annotation envelopes, not independent wall/lumen/material or functional anatomy.

The CT case is separate from every published ultrasound example. It cannot supply their echogenicity, Doppler, patient-specific nodules, biopsy status or matched 3D geometry. All tiny tissue, variant, node and adjacent-interface requirements remain required. Geometry and source-case review remain offline with anatomical/clinical approval and representation coverage ungranted. This candidate improves actual source geometry evidence; it does not shrink the all-radiology objective or establish clinical/commercial readiness.
