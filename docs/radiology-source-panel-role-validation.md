# Source panel role validation

The reader catalogue now checks declared panel facts before serving a source gallery. This applies across MSK and non-MSK figures, without rewriting source pixels, inferring missing panel identities or granting anatomical approval.

A schematic must retain its schematic modality. Every proposed primary panel must have the displayed primary modality in its source type map. Where an explicit display selection exists, it must agree with the source proposal. Duplicate, missing, contradictory or null selections are rejected. Ancillary roles must use known, distinct panels and match their source type map; they cannot overlap the proposed primary panels or one another.

The current inventory contains371typed source records. A schematic's schematic-panel selection is distinct from an empty clinical selection. Five legacy records explicitly describe their original single/unlettered source panels without duplicating a display-selection field; those source facts are preserved. A missing duplicate field is not replaced by an invented alias. Six records deliberately propose no selected panels for anatomical evidence; their empty proposals remain empty, and the evidence verifier still rejects them as an anatomical binding. Neither compatibility nor display integrity creates coverage credit.
