# Finer original head/neck photographic sources

The coarse 1.5 mm thyroid CT annotation is retained as source evidence, not smoothed into an apparently finer model. The next source route is the original [NLM Visible Human library](https://www.nlm.nih.gov/research/visible/visible_human.html). NLM currently describes the core male/female library as public-domain data and states that access licences are no longer required since 2019. Historical raw-file READMEs retain old licence wording; current governing [NLM terms](https://www.nlm.nih.gov/databases/download/terms_and_conditions.html) are recorded separately. Required attribution is “Courtesy of the U.S. National Library of Medicine”; endorsement must not be implied, and any redistributed snapshot must be identified appropriately.

The female head/neck sequence is avf1001a through avf1285c: 855 original full frames. Original README specifications are 2048 × 1216, nominal 0.33 mm x/y/section spacing, 24-bit noninterleaved RGB, no header and UNIX compress. These are declared source values, not independently established effective optical resolution or physical calibration. Three sampled original `.raw.Z` files match every decoded RGB sample in the corresponding NLM PNGs; compressed raw objects also match their single-object HTTP entity-tag MD5 values. Frames avf1240a, avf1260a and avf1280a were visually inspected and show lower neck/upper thoracic specimen context. They are not relabelled current-patient ultrasound, CT or functional/pathological proof.

Complete original head/neck acquisition is now running as a resumable per-frame process. Every raw/PNG pair is compared without crop, contrast enhancement or sample changes. Pending frames are not counted as acquired, and a partial download does not establish a complete reconstructed volume. Native series alignment, optical colour calibration, every tissue boundary, source anatomical approval and matched clinical imaging remain unverified. The full objective remains all structures in all radiology modules; this source does not replace missing living-patient morphology, functional acquisition or histology.

The separate Additional Head dataset is a different source 72-year-old male specimen, preserved in formalin with araldite-F-filled vessels. Its original README declares 0.147 mm sampling and 1,477 sections, but identifies sections 172, 173, 174 and 1395 as provider-computed averages replacing lost originals. Those slices must retain that provenance, not become acquired evidence. This separate dataset's individual commercial grant is unreviewed. The Denver derivative models reviewed have an explicit CC BY 4.0 grant but cover lower extremities; their corrected alignment and subjective manual segmentation do not supply neck labels or an unchanged native stack. These sources and donor/preparation contexts remain separate.
