# Original mesenteric vessel interfaces

The complete 38-object source packet is now compared in its original coordinates. All 26,700 triangles remain numerically testable; [complete contact evidence](bodyparts-mesenteric-source-review/complete-source-pair-summary.json) accounts for 307,880 conservative candidate pairs and preserves every reported contact, original face identity and numerical contact point in lossless compressed JSON. No source coordinate, face, normal, open boundary or disconnected component is repaired, fitted, capped, pruned or fused.

There are 3,823 contacts beyond ordinary numerical adjacency, across 78 original object pairs. None is within a single original object, consistent with the separate-object checks. Of these contacts, 177 occur between objects sharing the same actual returned FMA label and 3,646 between different actual labels. A separate category contains 713 artery-labelled/vein-labelled contacts. These are geometric observations, not pathological findings, fistula diagnoses, approved physiological junctions or vessel patency evidence.

Actual returned source labels and overlapping requested groups are distinct. 1,606 contacts share a requested compound group; that does not turn a trunk and one of its branches into the same returned source label. The complete per-object group memberships are retained, rather than overwritten by whichever parent/trunk group was visited last. Shared labels likewise do not independently prove biological identity or a particular tributary.

[All 703 object-pair comparisons](bodyparts-mesenteric-source-review/complete-pair-proximity-contact-review.json) join original nearest-vertex results to complete continuous triangle contacts. In 77 of the 78 affected pairs, nearest vertices have a positive separation despite triangle intersection. Nearest-vertex separation is only an upper bound for minimum continuous surface distance; it cannot establish a gap or justify constructing a missing bridge.
