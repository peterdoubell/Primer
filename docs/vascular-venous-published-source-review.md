# Original venous drainage source figures — 6 October 2026

Eleven complete publisher JPEGs were inspected and preserved byte-identically. Source XML/figure-specific credits, publisher MD5, original file and decoded-pixel SHA, dimensions and modes are recorded in the source proof. No source samples, labels, arrows or annotations were altered. Figure-specific anatomical validation and highest-resolution acquired master are unproven.

Ten figures (2/3/4/13/17/18/20/21/22/23) come from [Azizova et al., Persistent left superior vena cava: clinical importance and differential diagnoses](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7561662/) (DOI 10.1186/s13244-020-00906-2), under its exact CC BY 4.0 grant. The separate [published correction](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8052389/) (DOI 10.1186/s13244-021-00983-x) corrects reference numbering and name spelling, states the original article is updated, and does not grant additional clinical/image approval. Full original correction text and metadata/XML hashes are preserved. No separately excluded figure rights were found in these selected captions/credits.

The original pixels print uppercase panel letters; these are retained even where captions use lowercase. Figure 2 has actual A radiography, B/D/E/F/G CT and C/H flat VR. Its caption's axial-F/rendered-G/H wording does not match the actual last-row panel layout. The original caption is retained and mismatch is explicit; no silent correction or invented additional panel is made. Source ASD and coronary-sinus ostial atresia are explicitly not shown. Figure 23 caption omits actual D, so D remains a complete preserved CT panel without an invented caption mapping.

Figure 3 depicts different patients; Figure 4 shows separate septal, ductal and venous associations. Figure 13 has separate right/left isomerism groups and does not show the source-described PLSVC in A-D; local enlarged azygos/IVC-absence context does not validate complete hepatic/caval continuity. Figure 17 separates partial left-upper return A-E from another total-return patient F-H. Figure 18 retains source-described contrast-density flow inference without treating it as independently measured flow. Figure 20 separates SVC-obstruction and portal/Budd-Chiari patients and retains non-enhanced D versus enhanced E/F. Figure 21 is source-described same patient before/after SVC obstruction, with no dates/registration inferred. Figure 22 separates actual subaortic and retrooesophageal courses. Figure 23 separates postoperative venous Glenn and arterial-shunt contexts. Flat VR panels C/H (2), G/I (4), F (17), E/F (18) and D (22) are excluded from clinical CT-panel selections and do not supply actual mesh geometry.

One complete figure comes from [Nepomuceno et al., Multimodality imaging of anomalous pulmonary veins](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3038141/) (DOI 10.1186/1476-7120-9-3), under its exact CC BY 2.0 grant. Original A/B are TTE, C TEE and D CMR. Only D is selected as MRI; A-C are separately labelled ancillary ultrasound. The article describes a single case and Qp/Qs 1.1:1, but the still JPEG is not the complete echo/MR temporal study and does not independently quantify shunt or prescribe intervention. Historical case discussion thresholds are not universal recommendations. Supplementary echo movies exist but have not been acquired here.

The original images are attached to actual pulmonary/systemic venous reporting steps. Requirements also cover conditional bilateral levoatriocardinal/pericardiophrenic pathways, source-resolved appendage morphology and venous collateral connections. All grants clear redistribution/commercial rights only; no leaf receives independently verified geometry, motion, haemodynamics or clinical approval. Full vein/collector mapping, obstruction, native volumes, calibration, actual variants and independent clinical review remain outstanding.
