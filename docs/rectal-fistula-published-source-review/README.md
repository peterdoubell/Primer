# Rectal and fistula source figure release

The rectal cancer reader adds 15 complete figures from PMC9849549. The perianal fistula reader adds 11 complete figures from PMC13512459 and four shared anatomy figures. Existing key-image examples remain available. The fistula Parks drawing appears under Diagram; the other new fistula references appear under Images.

The 26 unique files preserve the acquired original bytes or decoded PDF samples and colour profiles. Nothing was cropped, resampled, enhanced or relabelled. The review JSON records source captions, source files, PDF page/object bindings, dimensions and hashes. Original article XML retains the CC BY 4.0 grant and author attribution. The review does not establish the original acquisition matrix or that publisher files are the authors' highest available masters.

MRI, histology, the TME specimen photograph, schematics and the MRI-derived overview have separate display roles. Descriptive position identifiers for Figures 8 and 14 describe their source layout; they are not additional printed panel labels. Separate patients and treatment examples remain separate. The ambiguous Figure 27 reference does not merge patient identity with Figure 26. A lithotomy clock convention does not establish MRI acquisition position.

Both reporting inventories now require actual layers, interfaces, routes, lesions, nodes and treated anatomy. Seven unsupported normal presets were removed. Primary staging and restaging limits remain distinct. The illustrative 3D guide remains partial and unregistered. No new native 3D model or full anatomical, clinical or reporting approval is granted by this release.

Browser checks used an isolated fake reader profile: all 15 rectal MRI references, 14 fistula MRI references and the separate Parks schematic loaded; desktop and 390-pixel mobile views fit their viewport. Source picture enlargements retained their composite and annotated/unannotated layouts. Production authenticated reader access was not tested.
