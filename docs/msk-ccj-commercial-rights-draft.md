Subject: Commercial educational reuse rights for the CCJ ligament models

Dear Dr Rodriguez Rubio,

We are developing Primer, an educational and reporting-reference application,
and are evaluating sources for detailed craniocervical anatomy. Your paper
“Immersive Surgical Anatomy of the Craniocervical Junction” links to the
SBCVL_UCSF models, including Model 4: Ligaments
(https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/model-4-ligaments-c4e9b8f578ea414db9b05fb69b9838bb).

The model listing appears to specify noncommercial reuse, separately from the
article licence. Could you clarify whether a commercial-use licence is available
for this model and any models containing the transverse/alar ligaments,
tectorial membrane and related joint capsules?

Our proposed use is local hosting of an interactive anatomical reference,
including distribution of its mesh/texture assets with the application and
source-attributed screenshots. We may need format conversion, selectable named
structures and display optimisations, while preserving anatomical geometry and
clearly identifying any changes. We would independently review the anatomy;
publication or permission would not be presented as clinical certification.

Please confirm which model versions/files and textures could be covered, any
fees or restrictions, required attribution and applicable adaptation or
redistribution terms. The listing credits Anatomography reference meshes under
CC BY-SA 2.1 Japan; please also clarify the upstream permissions and obligations
that would apply to the delivered models.

If files are available for evaluation, a component inventory and description of
which structures are scanned, segmented or artist-reconstructed would help us
assess coverage and fidelity. We have not downloaded or incorporated these
models into the application.
