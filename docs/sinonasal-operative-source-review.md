# Original operative sinonasal CT source examples

Seventeen unchanged figures from [Preoperative computed tomography evaluation of the paranasal sinuses: what should the physician know?](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6472854/) are added to the sinonasal investigation. The original XML expressly grants CC BY 4.0; no separate credit or permission exception was found for these figures. All publisher JPEG MD5 values and full decoded-pixel hashes were checked. All 17 complete images and their original annotations were visually inspected. Full original captions, authors, DOI, grant and source links are retained.

Figures 1–4 show source uncinate attachment variants, 5 maxillary atelectasis/uncinate apposition, 6–7 olfactory-fossa depth/asymmetry, 8 an ethmoidal canal region, 9 an orbital-wall defect, 10 infraorbital ethmoid cells, 11–12 frontal cells, 13 an Onodi/optic relation, 14 pterygoid/foraminal relationships, 15 sphenoid pneumatization, and 16–17 turbinate/septal variants. These examples remain CT, including within the MRI investigation; they do not supply unacquired MRI tissue characterization.

Original Figure 8 has a caption/plane disagreement: the caption says sagittal, but the whole displayed image is coronal. Neither the pixels nor source caption are corrected. The gallery explicitly exposes the mismatch, and no plane-specific anatomical coverage is granted. Figure 6's approximately 7.5 mm and Keros type 3 are source-caption values, not independently calibrated measurements. Original side assignments are attributed to the source without claiming native DICOM laterality verification. Source patient ages and native orientation/series identity are not inferred.

Complete files retain their original resolution and annotations. They are selected local stills, not full native CT, verified operative thin-bone effective resolution, calibrated patient measurements, physiological drainage or complete nerve/vessel/wall geometry. Source labels do not establish an independently assessed operative-risk class. Source-local variant and pathology examples are not the current reader patient. All anatomical reviews remain pending with empty structure coverage. The five offline NasalSeg meshes are a different case and are not registered to these images or promoted by this packaging step.

Replaced investigation images are archived in `docs/sinonasal-operative-source-review/packaged-source-images.json`. Source licence and individual file/pixel provenance are in `original-source-review.json` in that directory. Other investigation and lesson images are unchanged. Actual acquired MRI lesion, extension and tissue sequences, complete appropriate schematics/models and independent clinical review remain necessary for the full goal.
