# Fluids companion implementation

The new `web/fluids-lab.js` adds a quantitative companion to `phys.4.fluids`. It preserves the existing Venturi illustration, three-state Venturi interaction and contextual laboratory object. All new figures and explanatory text are original. No OpenStax figures, prose or assessment content is copied, and no generated photo is presented as a fluid-flow solution.

The module exposes `window.PrimerFluidsLab` and CommonJS exports. `render(item,hooks,frame)` accepts scenario `phys.4.fluids`; a supplied shared model frame retains the application's heading, instructions and speech controls. The renderer returns `frame.root`, or creates a standalone shell when no frame is supplied. Other supplied scenarios return null.

## Reproducible numeric interface

All numeric arguments are SI unless explicitly named otherwise. Inputs are finite typed numbers, with bounded positive geometry/properties and explicit domain checks; unsupported input states show an error and remove stale diagrams.

| API | Representation and governing constraints |
|---|---|
| `continuity({flow,areaIn,areaThroat,inletGauge,ambientPressure,vaporPressure,rho,g})` | Maintained volume flow, `U=Q/A`, circular radius `sqrt(A/pi)` and horizontal lossless Bernoulli with uniform section speeds. Inlet/outlet areas and ideal pressure recovery match. The exact quiz preset is Q=0.0008 m³/s, A1=0.0004 m², A2=0.0001 m²: 2 and 8 m/s, 30,000 Pa drop. Gauge/absolute pressure and equivalent head remain distinct. |
| `hydrostatics({ambientPressure,topPressure,vaporPressure,rho,g})` | `p(z)=p_atm-rho*g*z`, `h=(p_atm-p_top)/(rho*g)`, ideal zero-pressure and declared vapor limits. The vapor input is not an undocumented temperature fit. A violated single-phase bound is explicitly identified as hydrostatic extrapolation. |
| `pistons({areaIn,areaOut,forceIn,strokeIn})` | Horizontal cylinders at identical fixed elevation, so quasistatic pressure, swept volume and work balances do not omit changing fluid gravitational energy. Radius derives from actual area. Both piston strokes are bounded by finite travel. |
| `hull({length,width,height,dryMass,cargoMass,retainedWaterMass,rho,g})` | Fixed-orientation sealed box with water-excluding volume LWH. The mass/draft relation, reserve displacement and surface-pressure result use this same geometry. Retained water has the surrounding fluid's stated density and counts as added mass only. Retained water exceeding `rho*LWH` is rejected: this is merely a necessary whole-box, ideal zero-thickness upper bound, not sufficient internal capacity after shell/cargo volume. Cargo overload remains allowed and visibly has no floating equilibrium. |
| `pipe({radius,length,mu,rho,pressureDrop})` | Signed fully developed laminar circular-pipe solution, `u(r)=dp*(R²-r²)/(4*mu*L)`. Annular flux weighting, wall shear, pressure-force balance, Darcy factor and viscous dissipation use the same profile. Rest gives an undefined Darcy factor rather than infinity. The conservative teaching Re guard is explicitly not a universal transition threshold. |
| `blasiusSolve({etaMax,step})` | RK4 shooting solution of `f''' + 0.5*f*f''=0`, with wall f=f'=0 and finite far-field f'=1. The returned immutable full grid and `at(eta)` expose the solved field; no copied profile table is used. |
| `boundaryLayer({speed,nu,x,rho,probeEta})` | Dimensional u/v, wall stress, local skin friction and thicknesses derived from that Blasius solution using `eta=y*sqrt(U/(nu*x))`. The leading edge is excluded. Thinness, attachment and laminar assumptions remain explicit; no transition or separation is calculated. |
| `settling({radius,particleDensity,fluidDensity,mu,g,time})` | Signed downward-positive terminal Stokes balance and the declared memoryless particle-inertia transient. Stable `expm1`/small-time series evaluation preserves early displacement. Added mass and Basset history are deliberately omitted and never represented as absent simply because Re is small. Neutral density gives zero motion and no meaningful fractional settling time. |
| `joukowski({chord,epsilon,alphaDegrees,speed,rho,samples})` | A finite-thickness symmetric Joukowski airfoil with an explicitly exposed generating circle, mapped surface, Cp, velocity, normals, pressure-force quadrature and circulation. Defaults use 720 midpoint panels. |
| `plume({referenceHeight,referenceSpeed,referenceWidth,height,nu})` | Normalized far-field pure-plume scaling only: U ratio=h ratio^(-1/3), width ratio=h ratio and radius-based Re ratio=h ratio^(2/3). It supplies neither a resolved turbulence field nor an onset prediction. |

## Two solved fields

The Blasius solver uses eta_max=12 and step0.02 by default, with a bracketed shooting value. It obtains f''(0)=0.3320573362298743 and eta99=4.909989517687649. Refinement to step0.01 gives0.33205733621619354; extending the far boundary to16 gives0.332057336229799. The displacement integral equals eta−f at the far boundary, and an independently integrated momentum thickness agrees with2f''(0). The physical coordinate mapping gives delta99 proportional to sqrt(x) and local Cf proportional to1/sqrt(x). These are the stated steady zero-pressure-gradient boundary-layer equations, not the full Navier–Stokes solution at a leading edge or transition. [MIT boundary-layer lecture](https://web.mit.edu/13.021/demos/lectures/lecture16.pdf).

For the airfoil, the generating circle has centre(-epsilon,0) and radius1+epsilon. The map is `z=zeta+1/zeta`, subsequently scaled to the requested chord. Its pole is zeta=0; its critical points are±1, and zeta=1 is the trailing-edge preimage. The distinction is encoded correctly in the exported metadata. A clockwise-positive circulation `4*pi*R*scale*U*sin(alpha)` cancels the trailing-edge numerator singularity. The derivative ratio has an explicitly evaluated finite trailing-edge limit; the cusp has no invented unique normal. Midpoint surface samples avoid that cusp in pressure integration.

The actual outward-normal pressure integral produces body-axis force, then lift/drag resolved against the incident stream direction. Independently integrated CCW circulation has the opposite sign to the exported clockwise-positive circulation. The result agrees with Kutta–Joukowski lift; inviscid pressure drag is zero analytically. At the supported coarse128-panel setting, epsilon0.05/alpha10° has a small quadrature drag residual, which converges strongly by256 panels; it is not a physical drag prediction. UI/default720-panel results are near floating-point roundoff. Pressure arrows encode local excess-pressure traction with one declared gain. Their colours indicate Cp sign; the separate Cp-curve legend identifies upper and lower surfaces. Real stall, viscosity, wake/induced drag and compressibility are outside this model. [MIT classical aerofoil theory](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/18-354j-nonlinear-dynamics-ii-continuum-systems-spring-2015/62405b4d167552f452b8df52513a0ef8_MIT18_354JS15_Ch20.pdf).

## Stable renderer contract

- Root: `[data-fluids-lab]`; `root.fluids.getState()`, `getModels()`, `reset()`.
- Station selector: `[data-fluids-control="station"]`; values `continuity`, `hydrostatics`, `pistons`, `hull`, `pipe`, `boundary`, `settling`, `lift`, `plume`.
- Fields: `[data-fluids-control="station.field"]`, native labelled numeric inputs committed on `change`.
- Panels: `[data-fluids-panel="station"]`; invalid inputs set `data-error` and remove the panel's diagrams.
- Actions: `[data-fluids-action="enlarge"]`, `[data-fluids-action="reset"]`; presets use fixed `data-fluids-preset` identifiers.
- Each SVG carries `data-fluids-plot` and `data-axis-domain` with physical domain, pixel projection and equal-scale flag. Profile polylines, piston/hull geometry, pressure/speed arrows and probes have deterministic semantic selectors. Returned model arrays retain physical coordinates and quantities.

Piston/hull/airfoil geometry uses equal metre scales. Venturi axial metres and radial millimetres have separate clearly stated display scales; radius ratios and common speed-arrow gain remain calibrated. Suction-column width is explicitly schematic while elevation is calibrated. Flow profiles plot physical velocity against distance, not decorative motion. Tables retain force, volume, work, pressure, regime and source-model meaning. Enlargement scrolls inside the figure, and current-station speech includes its assumptions and quantities.

## Verification and scope

`tools/check_fluids_core.cjs` checks scalar fixtures, independent surface/annular quadrature, pressure/viscous-power balances, step/far-boundary refinement, an independently integrated dimensional settling ODE, mapped velocity/normal and pressure/circulation identities, and invalid inputs. `core-proof.json` records exact case counts, including27 separate dimensional boundary-layer cases; these counts are not a module-completeness denominator.

`tools/check_fluids_browser.cjs` checks actual source-derived SVG coordinates at390 and1440 px through presets, field extrema, blank/domain failures and recovery. Its final run passes234 valid states,60,158 geometry checks and8 invalid-input cases, with no page errors, text clipping or horizontal page overflow. It also verifies equal geometry scales, native labels, enlargement/keyboard scrolling, reset and independent-instance IDs. Proof and18 screenshots are under `browser/`. This checker verifies the standalone renderer with current production styles. Root-owned `reader/` evidence separately verifies the actual authenticated lesson.

The independent reviewer used separate SciPy boundary-value/collocation and adaptive complex-potential/force oracles and inspected the actual plotted coordinates. Its final evidence index is `independent-review/final-review.json`. That evidence and root's actual-reader result must remain separately identified from this implementation's checks.

The ten quiz relationships are now connected to explicit model evidence, including the exact30kPa flow fixture. This does not assert full fluid-mechanics coverage or certification. Real flooding/stability, general unsteady sphere history, transitional/turbulent fields, viscous real-airfoil performance and an observed plume transition remain outside the models. A labelled scaling law is not promoted to a flow simulation. The complete radiology/mathematics/physics objective remains open.
