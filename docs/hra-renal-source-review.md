# HRA renal source geometry and collecting-system review

Three original Human Reference Atlas reference objects were acquired directly from their versioned metadata distributions: female right kidney v1.3 (DOI [10.48539/HBM968.HSNL.854](https://doi.org/10.48539/HBM968.HSNL.854)), female right ureter v1.2 ([10.48539/HBM375.FNHZ.563](https://doi.org/10.48539/HBM375.FNHZ.563)) and female right renal pelvis v1.0 ([10.48539/HBM445.MNJL.333](https://doi.org/10.48539/HBM445.MNJL.333)). Raw-data metadata identifies CC BY 4.0 and original creators/reviewers; that is not inherited clinical approval of Primer's use or conversion. Metadata, model and crosswalk byte identities are retained. Raw GLB files stay in ignored research storage. No original Visible Human image volume was downloaded or its image rights inferred from the model grant.

The original kidney GLB contains 24 mesh primitives and 75,138 triangles, including capsule, hilum, outer cortex, renal column, ten renal pyramids and ten renal-papilla-labelled parts. The ureter object contains 15 primitives and 32,316 triangles: ten minor calyces, three major calyces, renal pelvis and ureter. The separate pelvis file contains one 3,012-triangle primitive. Its position and index accessor bytes are identical to the pelvis primitive inside the ureter file. The extra file is an alternative packaged source object, not a second anatomical pelvis. The 40 file-level primitives therefore do not imply 40 distinct reporting structures.

Version comments describe relocating papillae from kidney to ureter, but actual kidney v1.3 retains ten papilla-labelled primitives and ureter v1.2 has no papilla-labelled primitive. Neither statement is silently preferred: both metadata and actual source geometry remain preserved for version reconciliation. The separate pelvis crosswalk uses right-specific UBERON:0018116 while its GLB extras use generic UBERON:0001224. This may reflect semantic specificity rather than anatomical error; the relationship is not automatically normalised or treated as independent validation. Unmapped nonmesh grouping nodes are retained without invented anatomical identities.

Geometry inspection handles the original embedded GLB buffers, accessor offsets/strides, scene tree, mesh identities and explicit crosswalk records. Unsupported external, dynamic, sparse or transformed geometry is rejected rather than ignored. Source coordinates remain in the original glTF frame. The [glTF 2.0 specification](https://github.com/KhronosGroup/glTF/blob/main/specification/2.0/Specification.adoc) defines right-handed coordinates, +Y up and metres; this is a declared-format unit/frame, not measured original imaging resolution or a clinical RAS registration. No scale/fit to the CPTAC CT or liver MRI is performed.

Both indexed and exact-coordinate edge counts are retained to distinguish exported seams from physical coordinate boundaries. Many papilla/pyramid parts have open interfaces, which may be intentional partitions; no holes are filled or biological absence asserted. The renal-column primitive has two zero-area triangles and three exact-position nonmanifold edges, retained unchanged. Continuous self/inter-part contacts, internal lumen versus wall identity, underlying acquired boundary accuracy and microscopic thickness remain unaudited/unapproved. No generic cortex/calyx/pelvis label supplies all renal reporting structures or a pathological cyst/tumour feature.

Two 3D views preserve every selected source triangle and base colour, with display-only review lighting and a documented rigid +90-degree X-axis rotation to present original Y-up geometry on plot Z-up axes. Panel view extents differ and remain labelled in source metres; no tissue texture or matched patient pose is invented. Initial label overlap was fixed before final visual inspection. Seven format/accessor/scene regressions and 24 combined renal/source checks passed. These checks preserve source bytes and semantics; they do not establish full anatomical fidelity or clinical readiness.

The objects remain offline candidates. Version/semantic reconciliation, interface and continuous-contact review, original imaging evidence and missing renal vessels/remaining source structures are next work. Full radiology scope, 11,640 known obligations, 105 unexpanded investigations and 106 unreconciled curriculum surfaces remain unchanged. No model or requirement binding is promoted.
