# Native mediastinal-node CT and source-surface review

The [Mediastinal-Lymph-Node-SEG collection](https://www.cancerimagingarchive.net/collection/mediastinal-lymph-node-seg/) now supplies a separate original CT/SEG case for nodal review. CC BY 4.0 applies to the released CT, segmentation and clinical table; source-page/API fingerprints, required data citation and per-case context are preserved. This case is independent of NSCLC-Radiogenomics R01-001. No coordinates, annotations or diagnosis were merged between patients.

Selection inspected the public clinical table and all 1,026 CT/SEG series records. Fifty-eight clinical cases carry the primary-condition label Lung Non-Small. The first such case, case_0006, is explicitly Partially Annotated. Two other source slices were probed: case_0504 has 315 CT images and partial annotations; case_0571 has 138 CT images and fully annotated source scope. Both probes state 2.5 mm slice thickness, demonstrating that larger image count alone does not establish finer sampling. Case_0571 was selected from the fully annotated Lung Non-Small subset for acquisition and review, not declared the finest or clinically complete case. Probe/selection evidence remains in `lung-cancer-source-review/lnq-case-selection.json`.

All 138 CT files (72,700,258 uncompressed bytes) and the one SEG file (1,006,708 bytes) passed their publisher per-file MD5 manifests and ZIP CRC checks. Independent SHA-256 fingerprints bind every file/archive. The downloads completed; no source process remains running. These are original source files, not inferred masks from an AI model.

The native CT grid is 512 × 512 × 138, with 0.833984 mm in-plane spacing and 2.5 mm slice spacing. Every acquired CT slice has matching case, study, series, frame of reference, orientation and in-plane spacing. Slice positions are regular without accepted gaps, duplicates or shear. The SEG's single segment is manually coded Mediastinal lymph node (SCT 62683002). Its thirty encoded frames reference thirty specific CT source instances; all references and declared memberships match.

SEG position values differ from CT by at most 0.000006 mm. They are consistent with a float32-to-six-decimal serialization of the original coordinates. That numerical agreement is recorded, rather than presented as proof of the author's conversion pipeline or silently fitted away. Checks reject offsets that do not match either exact source positions or that specific numerical roundtrip, including another CT plane. The rendered contour coordinates and derived surface retain the tiny original SEG/CT difference. No registration or source resampling is applied.

All 9,423 positive annotation voxels remain present. There are five connected source components under both six- and twenty-six-neighbour connectivity; that is not a verified individual-node count or station identity. The 108 CT planes without encoded SEG frames remain unlabelled in this source representation, not clinically negative. ## Complete boundary and topology follow-up

The continuous-geometry audit checks all 9,537 vertex links and 158,190 conservative candidate triangle pairs (149,863 explicit contact tests; other pairs resolved by noncoplanar shared-edge geometry). It records no boundary/nonmanifold edges, inconsistent closed-edge orientations, zero-area triangles, duplicate positions, vertex-link defects or unexpected contacts. All five surface components have positive oriented volume. These are geometry results, not anatomical approval.

Four surface components have Euler characteristic 2. The largest has Euler characteristic −8, hence genus 5 under the verified closed, consistently oriented manifold conditions. Its handles have no established anatomical cause. The candidate is now explicitly held for topology interpretation despite its prior exact voxel-centre agreement. Original mesh and annotation values remain unchanged.

The largest native annotation component has Euler characteristic 1 under six-neighbour connectivity and 0 under twenty-six-neighbour connectivity. Its 28 adjacency-sensitive cubes were exhaustively located among 32,509 native cubes in the complete component bounds. All cube origins/configurations are retained; three representative sites from the most populated source Z planes have unmarked/marked CT patches for both planes of each original 2×2×2 support block. These are candidate review sites, not one-to-one handle locations, diagnoses or proof of annotation artefacts. No hole filling, pruning or inferred node separation was performed.

Fourteen new source-boundary figures include marked/unmarked local views for every component and lung/soft-tissue sheets for all thirty encoded planes, including three zero-label frames. Every positive source voxel is represented in the encoded-plane review. Component views, the complete soft-tissue contour sheet and all three candidate-site figures were inspected directly. Figure/source hashes and the precise extent of inspection are retained in the review manifests. This expands review evidence without declaring unseen or unlabelled anatomy negative.

Twenty-five scientific checks passed, covering pinched-vertex detection despite closed edges, face orientation, disconnected-component semantics, continuous contacts, numerical native-position checks, source integrity and the complete cube lookup logic. Remaining clinical requirements include anatomical interpretation of source/derived topology, accurate node boundaries, named station landmarks, primary-side context, disease confirmation and full clinical coverage.
