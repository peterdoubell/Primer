# Original bladder T2 and tumour annotation references

The complete FedBCa version2 archive from [Zenodo13622759](https://zenodo.org/records/13622759) is acquired under CC BY4.0. Its 1,028,171,551 bytes match publisher MD5 `350e81f66ed67413438eac544f1be70c`. The full archive stays in ignored research storage. Primary metadata, paper XML, original member names, tables and complete source review are retained here.

The archive contains 221 distinct T2 image files and 275 annotation files. All 275 nonblank table associations map to existing images and distinct masks; three blank Center1 rows remain recorded. Multiple lesions share some source images. The paper describes 275 tumours and228 patients, while the distributed image-file count is221; these numbers are not silently treated as interchangeable patient/examination counts. Original DICOM acquisitions and independent patient/examination linkage are not supplied.

A manual little-endian NIfTI-1 reader and nibabel independently agree on every stored value and selected spatial form in all496 files: 1,937,729,152 samples. Original headers, scales, raw value types, qform/sform declarations and fingerprints remain. Float32 quaternion decoding near180degrees uses an explicit precision policy; original coefficients are never rewritten. The maximum difference between declared image/mask grid corners across all275 pairs is about0.314mm, and only38 selected-affine pairs are bit-identical. Neither a matching array shape nor a small numeric discrepancy supplies independent biological registration.

Four complete source pairs, one from each centre, are delivered as separate examples. Their maximum declared corner discrepancies are approximately0.000023,0.002143,0.000363 and0mm. The original T2 and annotation arrays are losslessly retained in separate gzip scalar payloads; each uses its own source header. The viewer's optional boundary display uses the producer’s table/index association and explicitly retains the transform discrepancy. It does not fit one series or mask to another. There is no invented DWI, DCE, physical ADC calibration, contrast-bolus timing, wall segmentation or VI-RADS assessment.

The 3D references are exact boundaries of the union of original label1 voxel cells at index centres±0.5, transformed by the original mask affine. Outward triangles are verified by signed-volume agreement with labelled cell count×source-affine determinant. Every source component remains, including the separate two-voxel component at Center2 indices(315,167,12) and(316,167,12). No interpolation of the annotation, island deletion, smoothing, decimation, fitting, bridging or hole repair is used. The retained nonmanifold edge counts are1,12,5 and3, arising from digital cell adjacency. Render transport uses Float32 positions with measured conversion error; original Float64 positions and triangle identities remain in the review. Display normals and colours are illustrative.

These models depict annotations, not independently verified biological tumour surfaces, microscopic invasion or histological extent. Source pathology fields are attributed producer records. The cohort criteria allow untreated or diagnostic-TURBT cases, so pretreatment eligibility and exact treatment timing are not inferred for an individual example. Current stage, full examination quality and tissue correspondence remain unapproved. The models do not fulfil the full bladder reporting inventory, and the complete all-radiology goal remains active.

Browser verification confirms all72 first/annotation-centred/last native planes and original label-boundary display states across the four cases. Their original window pixels, array dimensions, source basis aspect and390-pixel layout match independent source-array computation. This is display/transport verification, not anatomical or diagnostic approval.
