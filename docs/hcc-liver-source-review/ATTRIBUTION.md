# HCC-TACE-Seg source attribution

Moawad, A. W., Fuentes, D., Morshid, A., Khalaf, A. M., Elmohr, M. M., Abusaif, A., Hazle, J. D., Kaseb, A. O., Hassan, M., Mahvash, A., Szklaruk, J., Qayyom, A., & Elsayes, K. (2021). Multimodality annotated HCC cases with and without advanced imaging segmentation [Data set]. The Cancer Imaging Archive. https://doi.org/10.7937/TCIA.5FNA-0924

Original images, segmentations and clinical data: [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/), as recorded on the [TCIA collection page](https://www.cancerimagingarchive.net/collection/hcc-tace-seg/) and per-series metadata. Apply the [TCIA usage policy](https://www.cancerimagingarchive.net/data-usage-policies-and-restrictions/).

The review records metadata, acquisition grouping and source reference/geometry correspondence without editing original data. No protected AMPLIFAI annotation rights, phase classification, registration, patient diagnosis or anatomical approval are inferred. Raw DICOM/workbook archives stay in ignored research storage and no runtime asset is added.

The separate-acquisition review figure selects unmarked original source CT planes, applies original rescale values/display windows and preserves native sampling with nearest-pixel display. It does not name phases, blend acquisitions, overlay masks, fit registration or change source voxels.
