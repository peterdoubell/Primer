# Carotid obstruction: source and reporting fidelity review

The previous investigation supplied two remote thumbnails, a generic neck
orientation model and five presets asserting unobserved normality. It had no
expanded fine-structure inventory. The current guide instead retains actual
source adequacy, observations, unassessed territories and unresolved evidence.
An explicitly declared `assessment-prompts` mode labels its buttons and live
announcements as prompts. It rejects filled claims without observation markers;
other guides retain their existing behaviour. A distinct per-entry walkthrough
review date leaves the older specialty and investigation contracts unchanged.

## Original publication sources

Eight complete licensed figures are bound to their relevant reporting steps:
six clinical figures and two conceptual wall diagrams. They are not eight new
patient examinations. Two newly acquired JPEG masters are preserved exactly as
original PDF DCT streams, with independent Poppler extraction, publisher
XML/PDF MD5 verification, SHA256s, grayscale readback and native dimensions.

- Johansson, Gu, Aviv and Fox, [2020 CTA study](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7160198/), Figure2:
  one source case in axial/coronal/sagittal views. The attributed source values
  2.8/3.9/2.7 mm remain publication measurements, not values independently
  recovered from display pixels. The actual narrowed site is not fully shown
  in every supplied plane.
- Johansson, Barud and Strömberg, [2026 diagnostic review](https://doi.org/10.1093/esj/23969873251355158), Figure1:
  two distinct left-sided cases in separate panels, with and without full
  collapse. These panels are not one patient, a registered comparison or a
  temporal sequence.
- Chengazi and Bhatt, [Pathology of the carotid space](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6377693/),
  complete previously preserved Figures11–16: conceptual wall/dissection and
  pseudoaneurysm diagrams, CT plus grayscale ultrasound flap views, trauma and
  iatrogenic pseudoaneurysm examples with distinct angiographic roles, and a
  flat MRA bead-like morphology display. These are new scoped bindings to
  unchanged source bytes, captions and acquisition roles. Actual tissue
  identity, precise dissection planes, voice function and flow are not inferred
  from a diagram or static appearance.

The selected articles/figures have explicit CC BY4.0 grants; complete original
credits and annotations remain. The unverified commercial rights of the two
legacy remote thumbnails are held. The 2020 Figure1 is also held: its comparative
caption wording conflicts with its illustrated small-distal-ICA example.
Neither an invented correction nor an uncalibrated measurement is adopted.
Complete PDFs remain in ignored research storage; only the selected, reviewed
figure streams are published. Original case/sequence interpretation is attributed
to its source, not granted as an independent clinical assessment.

## Original three-dimensional source objects

The new `bp3d-carotid-4.3` reference contains the complete original right common,
external and internal carotid trunks FJ4986/FJ4989/FJ4993. Actual returned
Compatibility4.3, FMA and part-of representation headers remain recorded;
requested and returned representation IDs are not silently equated. Every
original position, authored normal, face index and face order survives BP3D
transport, with measured Float32 conversion error and independently retained
original OBJ masters. There are 5,580 original triangles. No smoothing,
decimation, repair, new caps, branch bridges or fitted registration occurs.

Exact-position diagnostic edge checks find no boundary/nonmanifold edges or
zero-area faces in these three source objects. Diagnostic welding does not
modify output geometry. Numerical closure is not an anatomical endpoint, vessel
patency or validated branch-junction continuity. Source contacts/overlaps,
effective acquisition resolution, true lumen versus wall layers and biological
completeness remain unapproved.

These are curated adult-male TARO reference surfaces with manual editing,
not native patient CTA/MRA segmentation or an approved normal atlas. The
upstream4.3 data and geometric adaptation retain **CC BY-SA2.1 Japan** with
copyright, source and licence links. The archive4.0 CC BY4.0 grant is not
transferred. Public source transport includes the attribution and manifest.

Unmapped left/mirrored producer entries returned no objects through the source
download service; their metadata is not fabricated into geometry. FJ7564 was

The earlier FJ3483 internal/common-carotid identity/extent conflict remains

## Reporting and retained full scope

Conventional percentage grading requires an actual suitable minimum lumen and
distal ICA reference, appropriate method/plane and adequate source. Near-occlusion
includes subtler non-threadlike distal reduction; small calibre alone is not a
diagnosis. A reduced/collapsed reference does not receive a routine NASCET
percentage. Source non-opacification, true occlusion, slow filling and distal
pseudo-occlusion require the actual covered examination and supplied timing/flow
evidence. Missing delayed phases are not invented. Static MRA, source surface
shapes and visible arteries do not prove patency, collateral function or perfusion.

The inventory retains 142 groups and 816 individual components/interfaces on
both sides, each requiring images, schematics and models: 2,448 obligations.
It includes obtained origins and segments, each encountered branch, separately
resolved or explicitly unresolved wall/lumen interfaces, every actual lesion and
component, valid reference/comparison sites, occlusion/reconstitution, adjacent
tissues, variants, actual repairs and comparison timepoints. Actual acquired/
reported territories are instantiated separately; unknown or unacquired extent
is retained. A parent vessel label, gross mesh or source caption does not fulfil
its children automatically. The list is a draft floor, not the patient denominator.

Every candidate anatomical review remains pending. No complete carotid anatomy,
clinical/commercial readiness or whole-radiology/math/physics completion is
claimed. The broader goal remains active.
