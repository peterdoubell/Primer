# Published cervical-node ultrasound examples

The soft-tissue neck and cervical-node reports include two examples from Möller and colleagues. The paediatric neck-mass report includes a separate figure from Çalışkan Kamış and Yağcı. The source card opens the journal image at its published pixel dimensions. [Möller et al., 2026](https://doi.org/10.3390/cancers18061045) · [Çalışkan Kamış and Yağcı, 2025](https://doi.org/10.3389/fped.2025.1663515)

| Original journal image | What the panels show | Scope of the example |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Möller, Figure 4, first published image (a–b), 2,134 × 3,081 | A cervical node in a healthy subject (a) and an inguinal comparator (b), with grayscale calipers | The cervical side and level are unspecified. The paper describes the distinct bright hilum in panel b. |
| Möller, Figure 14, 2,133 × 4,244 | A left periauricular inflammatory node in parotitis. Panel a shows grayscale anatomy and panels b–c show hilar vessels on Doppler | The source gives the side and a size of 50 × 31 mm. The numbered cervical level and named acquisition plane are unspecified. |
| Çalışkan Kamış and Yağcı, Figure 3, 1,893 × 541 | Three grayscale examples from a paediatric lymphadenopathy cohort, classified in the paper as reactive, suspicious and highly suspicious | The source caption discusses vascularity. The delivered panels show grayscale calipers. Side, level and individual histology are unspecified. |
