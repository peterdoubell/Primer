# Original implanted-support/ECMO radiographs — 6 October 2026

Eleven complete original publisher JPEGs from [Chest radiographs of cardiac devices (Part 2): Ventricular assist devices](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6837777/) (DOI 10.4102/sajr.v23i1.1732) were inspected, including in-pixel notes, and preserved byte-identically under the original CC BY 4.0 grant. No explicit separate reproduction/permission exception was found in these captions/complete figures. Publisher MD5, file/pixel SHA, original dimensions/modes, source captions, metadata/XML and attribution are recorded. No pixel changes or new calibration were applied; highest-resolution master and original acquired examination are unavailable.

Figures 1–4 cover an IABP marker, a jugular dual-lumen VV ECMO projection, separate VV cannula projections and a paediatric carotid/femoral VA ECMO case. The original proposed 2-cm IABP advancement is historical case text, not a repositioning instruction. Projections and source labels do not independently prove complete VV/VA circuit configuration, drainage/return port assignment, return-jet alignment or recirculation. Figure 4 is a specific paediatric configuration, not an adult access/placement template.

Figures 5/6 preserve paediatric/neonatal Berlin Heart LV/biventricular support; LV-to-aorta and RA-to-PA components are distinct circuits. Figures 7/8 are source-described HMII before/one-year-follow-up with original 72-degree/11-cm and 2-cm migration descriptions. Those are not independent calibration or universal acceptable values; actual projection, dates, source registration and physiology remain unverified. Figures 9/10 distinguish source HeartWare and HeartMate III projected hardware. Figure 11 describes decommissioned HeartWare with an intentionally severed driveline; that state must not automatically be labelled accidental fracture or current pump support.

The [FDA's current HVAD information](https://www.fda.gov/medical-devices/cardiovascular-devices/medtronic-heartware-ventricular-assist-device-hvad-system) states that sale and distribution stopped on June 3, 2021. Historical images remain useful for recognising existing implants; the 2019 review's “currently common” framing is not adopted as present new-implant suitability. This review covers that regulatory context only, not the complete current advisories/IFU or individual serial-number/patient management. No FDA/manufacturer illustration is reused and no device replacement/removal instruction is inferred.

All original a/b pairs remain complete (5/9/10/11); single unlettered figures explicitly use whole-panel scope. Existing normal presets remain absent. Reporting steps require actual ECMO circuit roles and documented decommissioning rather than assumed malfunction. Original device/native interfaces, full geometry, radiolucent segments, patient/model identity, original calibration and electrical/haemodynamic/MRI conditions remain unresolved. No leaf receives verified anatomical/clinical coverage and no 3D device model is inferred from a radiograph. New figures are local, not deployed.
