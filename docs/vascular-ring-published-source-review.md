# Original vascular ring/slings figures — 6 October 2026

All 11 original publisher JPEGs from [Etesami et al., Computed tomography in the evaluation of vascular rings and slings](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4141344/) (DOI 10.1007/s13244-014-0343-3) were inspected as complete figures and preserved byte-identically, including source arrows, panel labels and annotations. Original XML explicitly grants CC BY 4.0; no separate figure credit/permission was found. Publisher MD5, file SHA, decoded-pixel SHA, mode and dimensions are recorded. The original grant is retained, with authors and DOI attributed. Highest-resolution acquired master, source CT volume, independent calibration and anatomy approval remain unavailable.

Panel roles:

- Figure 1: a/b conceptual developmental/normal arch schematic. The source explicitly warns that six embryonic arches do not coexist and the fifth is absent in most fetuses.
- Figure 2: a schematic; b/c/d/e clinical CT/projections; f/g/h flat vascular/airway renderings. Source e/g are another patient and are described as the same patient. These are separate configurations, not a single patient model.
- Figure 3: a/b local clinical CT; c flat VR; d virtual bronchoscopy. Atretic/non-opacified components are source-described/inferred, not fully directly resolved. Static endoscopic rendering does not demonstrate motion.
- Figure 4: whole postoperative flat VR. No acquired clinical CT panel or original mesh is supplied.
- Figure 5: a schematic; b/c/e CT/projections; d flat VR. Full ductal/ligamentous ring attachments and actual measurements remain unresolved.
- Figure 6: a schematic; b clinical MIP; c flat VR. The caption describes a brachiocephalic-to-LPA ductus and ring; full topology/encirclement is not independently proven from these views.
- Figure 7: a/b/c local axial CT. Original caption side wording is ambiguous/incomplete; it is preserved, with no silent side correction or inferred registration.
- Figure 8: a schematic; b/c clinical CT/projection; d flat VR. An aberrant subclavian alone does not prove a complete ring.
- Figure 9: a/b source-described same-patient CT of a partially thrombosed aberrant subclavian aneurysm. Original dimensions, full extent, independent registration and clinical urgency are not established.
- Figure 10: a schematic; b axial CT; c sagittal MinIP. Static indentation is separated from dynamic malacia or clinical significance.
- Figure 11: a schematic; b CT; c MinIP of bridging bronchus. Complete cartilage rings, dynamic function and all lobar/vascular connections remain unverified.

The original figures are connected to arch, airway and pulmonary-artery reporting steps. Introductory figures are source developmental and sling examples rather than the generic module overview. Pulmonary/systemic venous steps receive no borrowed figure coverage. The complete original images retain all panels; clinical selections explicitly exclude schematics and flat renderings. No native anatomy mesh is derived from a JPEG and no verified leaf coverage is granted. Figures from the separate CC BY-NC-ND PMC9705143 article are not commercially reused.
