## Acquisition requirements

New source material must identify the actual anatomical component, its extent,
side/population, acquisition or reconstruction method and source version.
Complete-course/attachment and internal-architecture requirements cannot be met
by a local slice or an outer surface alone. Every representation type needs its
own evidence. An appropriately reviewed collection may eventually provide full
coverage, but multiple partial examples are not automatically complete.

## Rights decisions

The current runtime reference-image audit records 221 image references: 80 with
cleared rights evidence and 141 still unverified for the intended commercial
use. This is not a finding that every unverified image has no permission;
educational reproduction terms do not necessarily establish commercial rights.
The existing publisher inquiry remains separate from anatomical validation.

The new [CCJ model rights draft](msk-ccj-commercial-rights-draft.md) is ready for
sender details. Explicit permission to send to the named published contact was
requested in this chat on 27 September and remains unanswered at this record.
No email, model download, registration, purchase or external access request has
been made. A grant must cover the exact files, textures, adaptation/distribution
scope and applicable upstream rights; the article licence is insufficient by
itself. Granting rights would not establish anatomical fidelity.

## Independent clinical review

A qualified MSK reviewer needs to inspect source-specific representations and
the reporting-scope decomposition. The queue includes hashes, modality/context,
partial-coverage claims, exact licences and current blockers. No blanket approval
of a region can approve its missing components. Missing assets must be acquired;
a reviewer cannot approve their absence away. Review evidence must identify the
reviewer, exact artifact/claim scope, findings and limitations. None has been
fabricated or marked clinically approved by source-preservation/software tests.

These are prerequisites to the original objective, not a reduced definition of
success. The goal remains active and clinical/commercial readiness remains false.
