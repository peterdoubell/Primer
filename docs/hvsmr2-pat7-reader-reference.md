# Partial native blood-pool reader reference — 6 October 2026

The actual `ra.vascular-anomalies` reader now exposes HVSMR-2.0 pat7 as a separate anatomy reference under the 3D anatomy tab. Eight source classes preserve their original patient coordinates and topology: LV, RV, LA with limited source pulmonary-vein extents, RA, AO, PA, both source SVC channels and IVC. This reference remains independent of the generic aorta guide and every published figure patient. Matching native MRI planes/label contours are displayed alongside the model.

All 283,148 vertices and 567,000 source triangles are retained. Reader transport stores float32 positions (maximum absolute error 3.814697265625e-6 in source-declared mm), calculated lighting normals and exact original uint32 indices in BP3D/gzip. No source fitting, padding, smoothing, decimation, class merge or component deletion. The original small RV component remains. Original label arrays are already the creators' processed segmentation annotations, not unresolved original tissue boundaries. Transport proof is in `hvsmr2-pat7-native-source-review/reader-package-review.json`.

Native acquisition, source cropping, case selection, labels/optional extents and missing clinical CSV record are described in the [source review](hvsmr2-pat7-native-source-review.md). Attribution and display adaptations are explicit in the manifest and reader.

The viewer explicitly identifies this as one adult, partial blood-pool reference, not a normal whole-heart atlas or the anatomy of the patient being reported. It does not independently supply complete walls, myocardium, thin valves, pulmonary/systemic branches and drainage interfaces, coronary sinus, ductal/ligamentous ring or airway. Optional benchmark zones and capped/class junctions are not clinical anatomic termination. Source-declared physical units are not independently linked DICOM calibration. No cine, flow, shunt ratio, pressure gradient, clinical severity or procedural suitability is inferred. Rights records remain distinct from anatomical review; all nine display assets have empty verified leaf bindings and pending anatomical review.
