Complex outputs are `{re,im}`. An evaluated sample carries `value`, natural-log `logAbs`, phase in radians and a status. Genuine poles return a null value and undefined phase. Zero has undefined phase. Underflow/overflow retains the analytic log magnitude and an explicit log-only status; tiny representable complex values remain visible in scientific notation. No expression evaluator accepts user input.

The pathological preset is defined by exp(−1/z⁴) away from zero and 0 at zero. At zero, the exact axis partials vanish; along the 45° ray log|f|=1/h⁴, demonstrating discontinuity. Its directional quotient is handled directly in logarithms to avoid exponentiating an unbounded number. The \|z\|² preset has derivative 0 at zero and is holomorphic on no open neighborhood.

C2 retains `a/z+b/(z−2)` with the original real residue bounds as a regression preset. Zero coefficients remove the corresponding genuine pole. Signed winding ±1/±2 supports orientation reversal. Pole order and complex residues are independent fields. A path pole suppresses both integral evaluation and the output trace; no principal-value substitution or visual bridge is made. Near-pole numerical quadrature increases its power-of-two panel count using the pole-to-path gap, capped at 65,536 panels with a disclosed unresolved status if capped. Exact residue sums remain separate from this numerical check.

Taylor partial sums use exact coefficient rules for the geometric series, 1/(z²+1) and exponential. Error bounds apply only inside the relevant disk (every finite probe for exponential). A finite algebraic remainder outside the radius is not labeled a convergence result. The inference selector is explicitly a witness for the assessed evidence, not a claimed determination of an unknown series.

The Liouville station holds a hypothetical global M fixed while R changes and distinguishes it from the actual disk maximum M(R). Its FTA proof contains root-free assumption, entire reciprocal, leading-term exterior control, compact interior boundedness, Liouville and contradiction. Neither sampled minima nor root searches establish the proof.

The mean station compares complex circle mean, center value, mean modulus and exact boundary maximum separately. The selected disk must avoid the reciprocal preset's pole; nonholomorphic presets fail the theorem hypothesis. The text derives the equality case, local constancy and identity-theorem extension on a connected domain, and requires continuity on compact closure for boundary attainment.

Jordan's finite real integral is independently numerically integrated. The finite closing arc is computed stably from finite residue balance and compared with direct parameter quadrature. For the matching half-plane, the valid bound is πM_R/|k|. Only decaying g presets supply M_R→0; g=1 has exact arc −2 sin(kR)/k and need not vanish. At k=0 its arc is −2R. The wrong half-plane has growth and no Jordan certificate. The lower closure is clockwise.
