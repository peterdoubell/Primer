# Candidate interpretation of encoded normal angles

The [original CVH5 resources](cvh5-pelvic-source-review.md) now have a separate experimental interpretation of their Type-3 normal payloads. It retains all 642,362 normal vectors across 47 resources, original quantized alpha/theta values, full encoded-span identities, candidate normal-index correspondence and numeric summaries. Earlier geometry candidates and the original U3D are unchanged.

The first resource was used to compare 16 explicit alternatives: integer alphabets of 5759/5760, planar/interleaved angle layout, alpha/theta order and the angle denominator. The planar alpha-then-theta interpretation with an alphabet of 5760 strongly matches its triangle directions. Alphabet 5759 alternatives have near-zero median agreement. The denominator 5759 follows Adobe's published spherical formula. These tests identify a numerically consistent candidate; geometric smoothness does not uniquely prove every scalar or original authoring value.

The same interpretation was checked without changing parameters on the other 46 resources. Every reconstructed vector is finite and unit length. Per-resource median corner-to-face normal dot products range from approximately 0.956 to 0.997. Zero-area candidate faces are counted and excluded from this directional calculation only; no triangles or source coordinates are removed or repaired. A source-smoothed normal need not equal its incident facet direction, so the numeric distribution is retained without declaring a universal angular acceptance rule.

This interpretation uses the original encoded normal data, rather than replacing it with normals generated from geometry. Its payload layout is a single combined integer stream containing planar alpha and theta halves. The source scalar is treated as the maximum integer index, yielding alphabet size `value+1`, and angles use `2π × integer / value`. The applicable archived Adobe format reference and its provenance remain in the [geometry review](cvh5-candidate-geometry-review.md).

Whole-decoder verification is still unfinished. Candidate face/normal indices retain the experimental UIC1 bootstrap, and both arithmetic implementations share the broader RH interpretation. Directional agreement is internal numerical evidence, not an independently decoded original, source-section registration, biological boundary validation or certification. Quantized source angles cannot restore unavailable precompression authoring normals. Source materials, scene transforms, anatomical partitions and complete course/extent also require their own review.

Every normal artifact therefore retains `normal_attribute_interpretation_verified=false`, `whole_decoder_independently_verified=false` and no clinical/model coverage credit. No runtime display uses these candidate normals. Full radiology requirements and image-rights counts are unchanged, and the original CC BY attribution/correction continue to apply.

Reproduce with `python -m tools.anatomy_sources.review_cvh5_normal_interpretation --source docs/cvh5-pelvic-source-review --candidates docs/cvh5-candidate-geometry-review --output docs/cvh5-normal-interpretation-review`. Tests cover independent axis cases, all original spans and hashes, every vector and the retained non-approval scope.
