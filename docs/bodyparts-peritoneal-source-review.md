# Original upstream peritoneal 3D candidates

Acquired four original upstream BodyParts3D version-manifest 4.3 elements under three labels: small-bowel mesentery (FJ3396/FJ4650), transverse mesocolon (FJ3398) and posterior abdominal peritoneum (FJ4651). Source component selection, original IS-A/PART-OF representation identities, ZIP CRC and individual OBJ headers/fingerprints were checked. The original ZIP/OBJ arrays remain in ignored research storage; no source object is promoted into runtime or credited as complete reporting anatomy.

The original upstream default **CC BY-SA 2.1 Japan** grant is preserved separately from the archived v4.0 CC BY 4.0 grant. Source credit: BodyParts3D © 2008 Database Center for Life Science (DBCLS). Original metadata/license tables are reused from the retained 4.3 snapshot and fingerprinted in [acquisition evidence](bodyparts-peritoneal-source-review/acquisition.json); no newly retrieved current table is claimed. Review renders retain applicable ShareAlike attribution. Source grant and local hashes do not verify anatomical quality or original acquisition resolution.

The complete detailed inventory is preserved losslessly in gzip with separate compressed/uncompressed fingerprints; no component records are omitted. All **79,516 original triangles**, vertex/normal records and source index identities are preserved. Exact-position topology is a diagnostic analysis index, not welding or a repaired model. Closed individual fragments do not prove a complete continuous mesenteric sheet or complete peritoneal surface. Source-declared versus actual vertex bounds differ by up to 1.9818 mm in FJ4651; original header values are not edited or used to fit the geometry.

The transverse-mesocolon/posterior-peritoneum pair has exactly the same face-index array and very nearly the same vertex positions. Its maximum same-index displacement is **0.010049876 mm**. Because the face indices correspond, this also bounds the displacement of corresponding barycentric points anywhere on each indexed triangle, without fitting. It is a source geometric correspondence bound, not anatomical registration, biological identity or proof that both labels denote distinct complete structures. Both objects and labels remain unchanged; a whole posterior-peritoneum claim is unapproved.

The two small-bowel mesenteries have matching coarse bounds/counts but different vertex/index/triangle arrays. Their maximum bidirectional nearest-vertex distances are approximately **1.4243 / 1.9253 mm**. Those point-cloud statistics are not continuous surface-error bounds; no vertex ordering or fitted triangle correspondence is invented. Their duplicate/variant/complementary biological roles remain unresolved.
