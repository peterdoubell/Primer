# Cardiomyopathy MRI reporting fidelity — 6 October 2026

The actual `ra.mri-cardiomyopathy` reporting contract now requires acquired temporal/contour/tissue evidence and applicable references. All five unsupported normal presets are removed. Templates identify actual ED/ES coverage, basal/apical/papillary/trabecular/outflow conventions, body-size indexing, region/layer localisation, source quality and uncertainty. Mapping/ECV requires acquisition/processing/ROI/reference context and haematocrit source/date or explicitly named alternative inputs. No quantitative result is supplied from a still, colour picture or generic heart.

The exclusive LGE cause rule is replaced by pattern-based differential: coronary-type subendocardial involvement is relevant, but global subendocardial, mixed, midwall/epicardial or artefactual patterns do not uniquely prove a cause. Absent LGE does not exclude every cardiomyopathy or diffuse tissue abnormality. Static jets, effusions and filling defects do not independently quantify regurgitation, tamponade or prove thrombus.

The reproducible inventory contains 67 groups/636 leaves (1,908 image/schematic/model obligations). All 17 named LV regions have separate source endocardial/compact-midwall/epicardial and adjacent trabecular/papillary interfaces, actual phase-thickness/motion and disease/map regions. RV inflow/body/outflow/insertion regions are separately named, without borrowing the LV model. Chambers/appendages, septa, valves/papillary-chordal tissue, relevant vessels/pericardium/extracardiac anatomy and each actual ischaemic/nonischaemic/mixed/thrombotic/no-reflow component remain conditional source-specific requirements. Apical and unresolved motion assessment must record actual model/source limitations. Known floor only, not exhaustive disease or current guideline coverage.

Sources: effective [RA cardiac anatomy](https://radiologyassistant.nl/cardiovascular/anatomy/cardiac-anatomy) and [cardiomyopathy](https://radiologyassistant.nl/cardiovascular/cardiomyopathy/ischemic-and-non-ischemic-cardiomyopathy) scope; relevant original XML [SCMR 2020 interpretation/post-processing recommendations](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7066763/) (DOI 10.1186/s12968-020-00610-6); and relevant original XML [mapping in cardiomyopathy review](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11084160/) (DOI 10.3390/jcm13092621). Both article grants are CC BY 4.0; the subsequent [original CMR figure review](cmr-published-source-review.md) preserves thirteen inspected figures and holds SCMR adapted/modified Figures 5/10 pending upstream rights. Native source data and complete clinical validation remain outstanding. Hashes/grants/extract scope recorded in `cardiomyopathy-source-review.json`. No complete current clinical guideline/software validation audit is claimed.

The generic heart model explicitly lacks patient-specific myocardium/region/layer boundaries, scar/maps, motion and measured haemodynamics; source blood pools cannot substitute for those tissues. The initial scope expansion added no pictures; the subsequent source packet adds thirteen original examples. No native myocardial models, quantitative measurements, verified leaf coverage or clinical approval are granted. Original cine/scar/map sources, faithful schematic/geometry and independent CMR clinical/anatomical review remain outstanding.

Full gate fails: 137 investigations, 106 unreconciled curriculum surfaces, 77 unexpanded investigations and at least 46,455 known-floor representation obligations. Total unknown and clinical/commercial readiness unproven. Local change, not deployed.
