# SPL limbic source: anatomical meaning and presentation review

This review examines what the **twelve selected source structures can support**, separately from transport, pixel and GPU correctness. I read the pinned SPL publisher description, label table/metadata and MRML scene; independently inspected label components and physical positions; and visually inspected native T1/T2 axial, coronal and sagittal panels for each selected structure. I also inspected the actual viewer's label-boundary and publisher-surface screenshots. This is a bounded gross-anatomy/source-meaning review, not independent clinical segmentation approval.

## Source meaning and identity

The [primary SPL/NAC publication page](https://www.openanatomy.org/atlas-pages/atlas-spl-nac-brain.html) describes one healthy 42-year-old male volunteer, 0.75 mm isotropic MPRAGE and T2-weighted acquisitions, and labels based on the 1 mm volumes. FreeSurfer parcellation was followed by manual segmentation of many structures. The source does not document a separate clinical validation or manual accuracy assessment for each of these twelve objects. “Healthy/normal” is the publisher's case description, not a population norm or this review's diagnosis.

All twelve names and codes agree among the selected manifest, original colour table, metadata rows and MRML model/storage names. The selected main-scene models have no parent transform. The six bilateral pairs have the expected left-to-right ordering in source RAS coordinates; native-image inspection found **no demonstrated left/right swap**. A few near-midline cells cross x=0. This alone does not establish a side error: the coordinate origin is not an independently fitted anatomical midline, and the cells are 1 mm wide. No extra mirror or alignment should be imposed from the sign of a few such cells.

`structure-audit.json` retains the exact source rows, model names, bounds, centroids, components and inspected plane indices. `label-<code>-planes.png` contains six original-sample views per structure, using physical-coordinate nearest-label lookup. The picture uses 0–1100 stored intensity units for display only; T1 and T2 values are not quantitative tissue parameters or comparable calibrated contrasts. Cropping changes the view, not the source arrays. These panels do not substitute for every-slice expert delineation.

The observations establish useful **source-defined gross localisation**, not biological correctness of every boundary. Coarse partial volume, manual/automatic segmentation decisions and source-model processing remain material limitations.

## Components and the fornix discrepancy

Connected-component counts are source-grid observations, not counts of biological structures. The left hippocampus has 4636+4+1 cells, the right 4375+3; each parahippocampal parcel has a main component plus one cell. The left fornix contains 739+1+1 cells; the right has one 760-cell component under 26-neighbour connectivity. Other selected labels have one such component. Different 6-neighbour connectivity or duplicated VTK vertices must not be substituted for biological interpretation.

The remote left-fornix cell at RAS **(−2,−39,−4) mm** is preserved. Its continuity with the main tract is not demonstrated in the original-plane inspection (`fornix-island-2-planes.png`). It should not be called a confirmed normal anatomical island, a disease finding or an error to be silently removed. Another one-cell component at (−3,4,−4) mm is also retained. The main component's face-centre-to-publisher distance reaches about 3.45 mm; the remote cell drives the overall 12.24 mm sampled maximum. In the opposite direction, original left/right fornix vertices reach about 5.48 / 7.17 mm from label-cell boundaries. These are different finite query sets, **not continuous Hausdorff bounds**, and the exact processing cause or anatomically preferable surface is unestablished.

The original product presentation disclosed small components and general fornix differences but did not identify that known remote cell. I recommended an explicit selected-fornix note. Root has implemented a note explaining the retained cell, unvalidated continuity and sampled 12.24 mm distance, plus an ordinary **Inspect retained fornix outlier** control. The right-fornix note now states the 7.17 mm vertex discrepancy. Neither data representation was deleted, repaired or relabelled as ground truth. This resolves the identified source-presentation omission; the underlying anatomical uncertainty remains. Focused screenshots from the authenticated mobile reader confirm that the disclosure, MRI and voxel readout are readable after the ordinary outlier control. The readout locates source label 3007 at label index (167,132,130), with nearest native MRI index (196,165,106).

The focused screenshot also exposed a stale sentence describing every selected slice as near the label centroid. That is false after the outlier action or arbitrary slice navigation. The integrator changed it to describe the **Find structure in MRI** action instead. `review.json` records this corrected presentation issue, and the pre-correction disclosure image is retained specifically as finding evidence; it is not a screenshot of final wording.

Default exact cell boundaries are a defensible way to show precisely what the source categorical label says. They are **not thereby proven closer to biological anatomy** than the producer's processed surfaces. The separate comparison, source labels, grid limits, orientation conventions and research-use notices should remain visible/available.

## Contribution to current reporting requirements

`requirements-snapshot.json` records the effective current reporting guides/templates, and `requirement-map.json` records individual dispositions. “Missing” below means absent from **this selected package**, not a claim that Primer's other references lack it.

- **Epilepsy:** partial support for bilateral gross hippocampal orientation/shape, adjacent amygdala/parahippocampal context, and source fornix/mammillary localisation. Original T2 gives source signal context. Separate CA/subicular/internal architecture truth, FLAIR, a complete HARNESS examination, validated volumetry/normal asymmetry, hippocampal sclerosis, cortical dysplasia/migration, focal lesions, susceptibility/enhancement, skull-base encephaloceles, peri-ictal changes and electroclinical concordance are not supplied. Whole-head background MRI is not a labelled whole-brain pathology/dual-pathology audit.
- **Dementia:** partial support for locating both hippocampi and surrounding medial-temporal anatomy on source coronal images. One volunteer supplies no age-adjusted normal distribution, atrophy spectrum, longitudinal comparison or MTA calibration. The native coronal control does not itself select a validated standard scoring plane. Posterior/global atrophy examples, white-matter/lacunar disease, Fazekas scoring, microbleeds/siderosis, acute diffusion findings and treatable mimics are not established.
- **Brain anatomy:** supported at the narrow level of identifying these six paired source labels and their gross relationships in multiplanar MRI and separate 3D views. The broader surface, deep-grey/capsular, brainstem/cerebellar, ventricular/cisternal and vascular-territory reporting requirements are not completed by twelve limbic objects. Unlabelled T1/T2 background gives context, not full selected-model coverage or lesion-localisation/clinical validation.

SPL and HCP101006 are different source subjects and coordinate contexts. Their complementary references must not be fused or compared as if registered to one patient.

## Disposition

No additional demonstrated naming, side-swap or gross-location defect was found in the inspected twelve structures. The corrected fornix disclosure makes the important known source discrepancy reviewable; the stale centroid sentence was also corrected. The package is useful as a **partial source anatomy reference with explicit uncertainties**. Clinical boundary accuracy, all-reportable-structure fidelity and complete epilepsy/dementia/brain-anatomy coverage remain unproven. Matching hashes, perfect pixels, connected components and triangle counts are not evidence of those broader claims.
