SCORING CHECK
PZ: DWI determines the category; DWI 3 becomes PI-RADS 4 only with positive DCE. Positive DCE requires focal early enhancement corresponding to the suspicious finding.
TZ: T2 determines the category, except T2 2 with DWI ≥4 becomes PI-RADS 3, and T2 3 with DWI 5 becomes PI-RADS 4. DCE does not upgrade TZ lesions.
A score-4-pattern lesion is score 5 on the relevant sequence at ≥1.5 cm or with definite extraprostatic extension/invasion.
No DCE means no DCE-based upgrade. If DWI is inadequate, consult the PI-RADS alternative-assessment table and state the limitation.

TEMPLATE NOTES
PI-RADS categories express imaging suspicion: 1 very low; 2 low; 3 equivocal; 4 high; 5 very high likelihood of clinically significant cancer. They do not independently prescribe biopsy or treatment.
Choose the index lesion by highest PI-RADS category; resolve ties by extraprostatic extension, then size. Map up to four principal suspicious lesions and identify all sectors involved.
Measure AP and longitudinal gland dimensions on mid-sagittal T2, transverse on axial T2. Avoid mixing millimetres and centimetres in the ellipsoid formula.
PI-RADS diagnostic scoring is not a post-treatment recurrence system. A negative MRI does not exclude clinically significant cancer; interpret with clinical risk.
If both DWI and DCE are unavailable or inadequate, do not imply a complete PI-RADS assessment; the formal guidance limits assessment to staging for extraprostatic extension.
Use actual study images in the image slots and retain matching series/image references in the report.
