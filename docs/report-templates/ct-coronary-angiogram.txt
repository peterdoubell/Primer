CAD-RADS 2.0
CAD-RADS category:
0: no plaque or stenosis.
1: 1–24% stenosis, or plaque with positive remodelling but no luminal narrowing.
2: 25–49%.
3: 50–69%.
4A: 70–99% in one or two vessels.
4B: LM ≥50% or severe three-vessel disease.
5: total occlusion.
N: non-diagnostic.
Final CAD-RADS: CAD-RADS [0/1/2/3/4A/4B/5/N] / [P1/P2/P3/P4 when applicable] / [N / HRP / I+ / I− / I± / S / G / E as applicable].
If a relevant segment >1.5 mm is non-evaluable and evaluable segments show only <50% stenosis, use category N. If ≥50% stenosis is demonstrated elsewhere, retain the stenosis category and append N. Report P only if reliable.
