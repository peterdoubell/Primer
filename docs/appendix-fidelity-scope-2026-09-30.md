# Appendix investigation fidelity scope

The first non-MSK draft expands `ra.appendicitis` into 19 anatomical/entity groups and 56 reportable parts, each requiring independent clinical-image, schematic and model evidence: **168 obligations**. Three image slots now have partial, unapproved source candidates; 165 remain missing. Existing generic bowel graphics receive no automatic credit. See [the source-figure review](appendix-source-review.md).

The draft in `data/radiology/non-msk-structure-requirements.json` is tied to the effective reporting checklist, templates and complete walkthrough by a contract hash. It includes the appendiceal base, segments, tip, lumen and wall; caecal/ileal/colonic relationships; periappendiceal tissues; mesenteric nodes; focal fat structures; urinary and adnexal alternatives; and conditional complications and postoperative anatomy.

The source review covered the linked [US findings](https://radiologyassistant.nl/abdomen/acute-abdomen/appendicitis-us-findings), [mimics](https://radiologyassistant.nl/abdomen/acute-abdomen/appendicitis-and-mimics) and [US/CT pitfalls](https://radiologyassistant.nl/abdomen/acute-abdomen/appendicitis-pitfalls-in-us-and-ct-diagnosis) sections. The normal-appendix question is distinct from conditional disease examples. Sonographic layering is not supplied by routine CT; a normal reference cannot establish perforation. Side-specific targets cannot inherit evidence from the opposite side.

Compression, Doppler flow, contrast enhancement and calibrated measurements need their corresponding acquisition evidence. A static anatomical model does not establish those observations. No treatment rules or diagnostic thresholds were added by this scope expansion.

The linked articles extend beyond the current routine guide, including postoperative and additional mimic contexts. Those boundaries, site-dependent lesions, developmental context and complete wall-layer decomposition still require clinical reconciliation. The draft is not an exhaustive approved specification. Unseen anatomy is not declared normal, and uncertain scope is not waived.

The all-radiology audit now records 5,460 known representation obligations, including the unchanged 5,292 MSK obligations. The remaining 114 investigations lack structure inventories, and all curriculum surfaces still need reconciliation. The total all-radiology requirement count remains unknown. Scope and software tests do not grant clinical approval.
