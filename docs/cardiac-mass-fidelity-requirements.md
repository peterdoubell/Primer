# Cardiac mass reporting fidelity — 6 October 2026

The actual `ra.cardiac-masses` guide now requires source-specific lesion/site/attachment/component and affected-interface assessment. Two unsupported normal presets are removed; no mass, obstruction or spread is inferred absent from an unexamined region. The categorical “thrombus does not enhance” claim is replaced: thrombus usually lacks enhancement, but organised chronic thrombus can rarely enhance peripherally. One phase or a static picture does not establish thrombus/mixing, histology, malignancy, mobility, valve prolapse, perfusion, haemodynamic obstruction or tamponade. Templates record actual phase/timing/sequence/calibration, reference tissue, comparison and uncertainty. Source-supported urgent findings have an explicit communication prompt.

The reproducible inventory contains 67 groups/588 leaves (1,764 image/schematic/model obligations). It includes four chambers/appendages, septa/outflow, native/prosthetic valves, papillary/chordal apparatus, coronary/venous/arterial interfaces, pericardium/epicardium and relevant extracardiac spread. Normal mimics include Coumadin ridge, crista terminalis, pectinate/sagittal bundles, valve remnants, Chiari network, moderator band, actual septal/postoperative variants, lipomatous septum, annular calcification, cyst and aneurysm/extracardiac mimics. Every actual neoplastic/thrombotic/vegetative lesion needs separate complete extent, attachments, internal components and affected structures; diagnostic categories are not spatial models. Conditional source controls preserve actual temporal/tissue/functional observations. This remains a known floor, not an exhaustive histological catalogue or full staging/acquisition guideline.

Source review: effective [Radiology Assistant module](https://radiologyassistant.nl/cardiovascular/cardiac-masses1/cardiac-masses), relevant original XML of [Cardiac Masses and Pseudomasses](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10818366/) (DOI 10.3390/medicina60010070, CC BY 4.0), and relevant original XML of [Imaging of Cardiac Masses](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9558634/) (DOI 10.4103/jcecho.jcecho_18_22, CC BY-NC-SA 4.0). Metadata/XML hashes, actual licence URLs and review scope are in `cardiac-mass-source-review.json`. The subsequent [original mimic/thrombus figure review](cardiac-mimic-published-source-review.md) preserves all 38 original main figures with reviewed credits/pixels and explicit mixed/static/unresolved roles and map-caption conflicts; native lesion geometry and independent clinical validation remain outstanding; no figures are commercially reused from the second grant. The initial reporting expansion added no pictures; the subsequent source packet preserves all 38 original source figures without granting anatomical/clinical coverage.

The investigation-specific model note states that generic heart geometry does not contain actual lesions, stalk/base, thin valvular/chordal attachment, thrombus, invasion or pericardial/extracardiac disease. No native lesion model, verified leaf representation or clinical approval is granted. Images/schematics/models faithful to each actual structure and pathological state, complete native source acquisitions/geometry, independent anatomical validation and specialist clinical review remain outstanding.

Full gate still fails: 137 investigations, 106 unreconciled curriculum surfaces, 78 unexpanded investigations and at least 44,547 known-floor representation obligations. Total full scope remains unknown. Changes are local, not deployed.
