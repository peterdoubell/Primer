# History illustration review

All 29 history illustrations have current direct 800px review records in
`illustration-reviews.json`. This milestone does not certify browser enlargement,
interactive states, the remaining subjects, or deployment.

Corrections include ordinal versus scaled time; concrete water/crops/storage
relationships rather than inevitable urbanization; an actual shared-swing
schedule; river versus drainage-basin labels; Qin unification marked later than
the 300 BCE comparison; earlier Asian printing histories; decolonization as an
overlapping wave; government procedures qualified by country; GDP's nonmarket
component; human rights in rule-of-law comparisons; relative archaeological
dating; and the precise Rawlsian difference principle.

History regression tests inspect actual drawn geometry and payoff values, not
just asset presence. They cover river label position, Qin chronology, ordinal
timeline wording, supply/demand intersection and Prisoner's Dilemma responses.
Institutional source checks and per-plate findings are stored in the ledger.
