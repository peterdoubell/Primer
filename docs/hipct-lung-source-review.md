# Native human lung phase-contrast source blocks

This review acquired original publicly served Human Organ Atlas (HOA) N5 level0 data from two named left-lung volumes of donorLADAF-2020-27. Source metadata are pinned to the official metadata-schemas repository revisionf8dc2928234ac46a6d126cc293aab7ddbd446f61, reached through official hoa-tools revisionb69494e8d8cb373e4f451d6b03e77e6d09602cea. The original2022source publicationPMC9163096 and its metadata/XML MD5 were inspected. Its article grant isCCBY4; that article grant is kept separate from raw-data rights.

The official publicGCS store specifies axesx/y/z, uint16,128³blocks, Blosc/zstd compression and micrometre units. The acquired blocks are:

- DOI10.15151/ESRF-DC-572237999,6.5µmVOI03b-interlobular-fissure, source chunkXYZ15/15/5, index origin1920/1920/640. The128³block spans832µm per displayed voxel-cell axis.
- DOI10.15151/ESRF-DC-572222987,2.5µmVOI03-upper-lobe-apical, source chunkXYZ15/15/21, index origin1920/1920/2688. The128³block spans320µm per displayed voxel-cell axis.

Each complete encoded object matched its publisherGCSbyte length and base64MD5; object generation and metadata SHA are recorded. Original N5 headers were decoded according to the official specification: big-endian uint16, x fastest. Every one of4194304decoded scalar samples was independently compared with struct scalar readback. Derived arrays remainzyx with original sample values; no registration, resampling or anatomical segmentation was applied. The installed scientific decoder is numcodecs0.17.0 in the ignored research dependency directory, not runtime dependencies.

Source preparation is formalin fixation, agar-cube stabilisation, vacuum degassing and70%ethanol scanning. These are ex-vivo prepared specimens, not an in-vivo diagnostic HRCT or a healthy-patient reference. Phase-contrast scalar intensities are not clinicalHU. Published voxel pitch is not proof of effective tissue resolution or preservation of in-vivo dimensions. The source gives registrations to the25.08µmwhole-organ volume; they are retained but not applied or independently anatomically validated. CurrentGCSdimensions3851×3851×1401 and3877×3877×5600 differ from some2022published table dimensions; no unverified TIFF/JPEG2000-to-current-N5 pixel identity is claimed.

Three midpoint orthogonal planes per block were inspected. The local plots disclose the percentile scalar windows, source pitch and voxel-centre convention: index0centres at0µm, outer edges at−pitch/2 and(count−.5)×pitch. Figure spacing was corrected after visual inspection revealed overlapping row labels. Raw and derived inputs remain unchanged. The display is a local block, not a complete secondary lobule or all septal/airway/vascular/lymphatic interfaces. Fine texture alone does not identify an anatomical tissue class, clinical diagnosis or complete native3D structure.

DataCite records returned no rightsList. The primary HOA portal's indexed FAIR statement says its datasets useCCBY4 and datasetDOIs must be cited. The current direct JavaScript-page licence text was not captured by the browser attempt. This supports the published data policy but a record-specific byte-bound raw-data licence review remains pending; it is not inferred from the software or article licence. Only provenance and numeric-review records are retained here. Raw encoded blocks, decoded arrays and the inspected display remain in ignored `.research/hipct-lung-source-review`.

Scientific parser tests7passed, exercising all-scalar/axis order, truncated header, variable mode, wrong rank, invalid/oversized dimensions and sample-length mismatch. The default environment lacks the optional scientific codec and skipped the test module; it was not reported as a pass. No reader media, model, clinical approval or structure-leaf coverage was promoted. Native labelled anatomy and all other unresolved radiology requirements remain required.
