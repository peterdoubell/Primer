# Original biliary duct and tributary 3D source candidates

Twenty original BodyParts3D version-manifest 4.3 objects were acquired under fourteen source labels. They supply gallbladder, cystic/common/right/left hepatic duct and several right/left intrahepatic tributary candidates. Every original ZIP member passes CRC and source file/representation/concept/version header checks against the actual IS-A/PART-OF mapping snapshots. [Acquisition evidence](bodyparts-biliary-source-review/acquisition.json) binds original source bytes, metadata snapshots and selected groups. Raw ZIP/OBJ files remain ignored and outside runtime.

The upstream grant is [CC BY-SA 2.1 Japan](https://lifesciencedb.jp/bp3d/info_en/license/index.html), with BodyParts3D © 2008 DBCLS attribution. It is not the separate CC BY 4.0 grant on the archived 4.0 dataset. Derived source-review views retain ShareAlike terms; no licence substitution or clinical approval is inferred.

All 16,114 triangles, original coordinates/normals and face/normal indices are retained and fingerprinted in the [complete geometry inventory](bodyparts-biliary-source-review/original-obj-geometry-review.json). Exact-position indexing is analysis only. The twenty objects contain 27 components: FJ3072 and FJ3110 each have four; FJ3090 has two; the rest have one. There are no zero-area triangles or exact-position boundary/nonmanifold edges. These numerical facts do not prove anatomical completeness, appropriate solidity, lumen identity or acquisition accuracy. Maximum declared-versus-actual source bounds difference is about 0.0483 mm; no source header or geometry was corrected or fitted.

Six source groups have two representations. [Correspondence evidence](bodyparts-biliary-source-review/source-geometry-correspondence.json) preserves all six comparisons. Cystic-duct FJ3080/FJ4526 share face indexing but have different vertex records, with a same-index whole-triangle displacement upper bound of 0.113675371 mm. The other five pairs lack that vertex/face correspondence; nearest-vertex discrepancies reach tens of millimetres in some groups and are not continuous-surface bounds. Source labels do not decide whether these are distinct branches, alternative exports or different extents. No repeated object was discarded, relabelled, fitted or fused, and their counts are not independent biological observations.

The source-labelled common hepatic duct FJ3079 returns the original PART-OF representation BP21345 rather than requested IS-A BP20587; the returned header/label still matches the source tree. It is preserved under its original label. Its longitudinal extent and relationship to the separately reportable common-bile-duct segments need anatomical review; a parent label is not permission to fill every distal/intrapancreatic/intramural requirement.

Every source object was tested separately for continuous self-contacts in native millimetres, avoiding an unverified simultaneous assembly of same-label objects. [Complete summary](bodyparts-biliary-source-review/complete-self-contact-summary.json) fingerprints all twenty lossless gzip records, with every candidate-pair accounting preserved. All 16,114 triangles were testable, across 138,718 conservative candidate pairs, with no contacts beyond ordinary adjacency found within any object. Numerical tolerance is 1e-9 mm, not acquired resolution. Cross-object biological junctions, possible overlaps and complete duct connectivity remain unverified; zero self-contacts cannot approve a whole duct tree.

Six view sheets retain every triangle in separate panels: central ducts/gallbladder, right tributaries and left tributaries, each at azimuth 35 and 215. Native source coordinates, differing panel scales, complete component counts and review lighting remain explicit. [Rendering provenance](bodyparts-biliary-source-review/source-figure-review.json) binds every object and figure hash. The azimuth-35 central/left/right sheets were directly inspected; a title overlap was corrected by increasing display spacing, without changing source geometry. This is presentation inspection, not independent specialist validation.

The packet does not separately provide every segmental/subsegmental branch, patient-specific variant, full bile-duct wall/lumen layers, ampullary region, positive stone/stricture/tumour, clinical drainage function, calibrated MRI or registered patient-specific anatomy. Original acquisition resolution, reduction history, anatomical identity/extent and clinical appropriateness still require evidence. No original source cap or fragment was repaired, pruned or smoothed; no model was promoted into the reader. All 918 biliary representation obligations remain required and missing/unverified.

Tests bind the fourteen groups, all twenty original objects, all triangles/components, separate complete contacts, correspondence limits and rendered faces.
