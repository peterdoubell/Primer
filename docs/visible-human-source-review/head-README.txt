Data is the head of a 72 year old male donor. The donor was
preserved in formalin and the blood vessels were filled with
araldite-F.  Before freezing, MRI and CAT imaging was preformed.
After freezing the specimen was cryo sectioned at 0.147mm intervals
and digital photographs were taken with a resolution of
1056 x 1528 pixels.

For a complete description of the procedure see:

P. Ratiu, B. Hillen, J. Glase and D.P. Jenkins. 
'Visible Human 2.0 - The Next Generation'
"Medicine Meets Virtual Reality 11 - NextMed: Health Horizon" 
edited by J.D. Westwood et al. IOS Press, 2003. pp 275-281.

This work was performed under contract LM-1-3535 
"The Visible Human Project Anatomical Methods"
