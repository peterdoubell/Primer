Neutron beta-decay kinematics

Gold: electron; blue: antineutrino; purple: proton. Vectors are arranged head-to-tail in momentum space so their sum closes. This is not a spatial path or a picture of the particles. All axes have equal momentum scale. Rest energies use the central 2022 CODATA values; uncertainty correlations are not propagated. Binding effects, neutrino mass, weak-interaction amplitudes, spin correlations and rates are omitted.
