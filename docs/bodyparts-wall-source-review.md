# Original abdominal-wall source geometry review

Five original BodyParts3D version-manifest 4.3 pieces were acquired and retained: linea alba (`FJ1448`), right and left external oblique (`FJ1452`, `FJ1452M`), and right and left inguinal ligament (`FJ7307`, `FJ7211`). This is an offline source candidate review, not a complete wall atlas, clinical approval or runtime model promotion.

The acquisition receipt preserves requested IS-A identities and returned archive/OBJ identities. Both muscle returns match alternative representations in the original PART-OF map; the other three match their requested IS-A representations. All five source file IDs, representation IDs, concept IDs, OBJ headers and ZIP CRCs were verified. The parser permits an upstream `M` suffix only through explicit opt-in; default source acquisition still rejects it. No local mirror is exported.

All 237,506 original triangles, position records, normals and indices were inspected without fitting, fusion, pruning, smoothing, capping or repair. Exact-position welding is an analysis index only. It finds 89 components: 43 in each external oblique and one in each remaining piece. Closed topology does not identify muscle bundles, complete tissue boundaries or reportable fine structures. Source millimetre coordinates are preserved; patient axes and acquired resolution remain unverified.

The two original muscle objects have equal triangle counts but different vertex counts (63,907 and 63,893), position sets and face/normal indices. Analytical reflection of the right object’s source X coordinate produces a maximum symmetric nearest-vertex distance of approximately 0.286737 mm from the left object, with directional means approximately 0.002204 and 0.002186 mm. This comparison changes no source geometry and is not continuous surface distance, exact reflection, proof of independent patient sides, or anatomical registration.

Every original object has lossless self-contact evidence. There are 48 nonordinary numerical contacts, 24 in each external oblique. The combined audit tests all five original objects and retains 2,116 contacts across 2,357,374 conservative candidate pairs: 48 within objects and 2,068 between differently labelled objects. All ten between-object pairs have nearest-vertex and continuous triangle-contact accounting. Positive nearest-vertex distance does not exclude triangle intersection. Contact findings are not approved muscle/fascial attachments, pathology or grounds to remove source tissue.

Ten original-source views retain every triangle in two azimuths per labelled group. Two self-contact sheets and a combined source-pair sheet locate all numerical contacts against original geometry. Review lighting, colours and projection are derived display choices; panel scales differ. Short-axis ticks are suppressed where they overlap, while full numerical bounds remain in the geometry evidence. All 13 final sheets were visually inspected.

The two original IS-A/PART-OF label and synonym maps contain no matches for the ten recorded queries covering rectus abdominis, internal oblique, transversus, rectus sheath, transversalis fascia and linea semilunaris. This is a source-specific selection hold, not proof that no tissue is present in a parent object or that no other dataset exists. The outer muscle, linea alba and ligament labels cannot supply missing wall layers, muscle/aponeurosis distinctions, mesh/fixation, dynamic hernia anatomy or pathological defects. Those reporting obligations remain unfulfilled.
