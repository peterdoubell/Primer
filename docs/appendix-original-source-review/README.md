# Original appendix and postoperative source figures

Nineteen complete original published figures are now local in the appendix reader, alongside the three earlier Mostbeck ultrasound figures. They are selected case references, not approved complete structure coverage. All five reporting steps have local sources. Five normal-result presets were removed, and the bowel model explicitly states its incomplete schematic scope.

The original NLM/PMC dataset XML, metadata, PDF and HTML figure files were acquired on 8 October 2026. All acquired HTML masters match their publisher MD5. Complete numbered PDF pages and extracted masters were visually reviewed. The selected PDF masters are larger than the acquired HTML images; every decoded pixel and ICC profile was preserved in lossless PNG without crop, resize, enhancement or new labels. `original-source-review.json` binds source PDFs, XML, pages, objects, dimensions and pixel hashes. Source PDFs and QA renders remain in the ignored acquisition cache; runtime files are the original decoded figure masters.

- PMC8531161, DOI 10.1186/s13244-021-01086-3: eight clinical figures (2–9), with Figure 1's cohort flowchart excluded. Figure 6 contains four different patients, Figure 9 two. Figure 7 is ultrasound A and subsequent CT B. Hyperenhancement in Figure 5 coexists with source gangrenous histology and is not proof of uncomplicated disease.
- PMC6497212, DOI 10.5811/cpcem.2019.2.41682: one abdominal radiograph and two ultrasound images in a single 10-year-old boy with high ascending retrocaecal appendicitis. Radiographic opacity, ultrasound morphology and operative perforation confirmation remain separate evidence. The published 14.4 mm caliper is preserved as source output.
- PMC4330234, DOI 10.1007/s13244-014-0369-6: seven CT figures and one postoperative MRI figure. Preoperative and postoperative acquisitions remain distinct. Figures 4 and 5 each contain two different patients. Object iteration order differs from figure numbering for Figures 2/3 and 7/8; complete page captions determine the explicit binding.

These three source grants explicitly permit CC BY 4.0 reuse and no figure-specific exception was found in the reviewed originals. PMC3205519 and PMC3846936 remain held: their explicit non-commercial XML licence links conflict with broader attribution prose. They supply no runtime media or commercial permission.

`reader-browser-review.json` records fake-profile browser checks, source image decoding, mobile layout and unchanged report prompts. `test_appendix_original_source_figures.py` verifies pixel binding, source grants, patient/modality separation and unapproved coverage; when original PDFs are available it independently decodes every selected source object. Earlier source images remain unchanged.

Remaining requirements include complete native base-to-tip geometry and actual variation, all wall layers and interfaces, mesoappendiceal vessels/nerves, complete acquired mimics and postoperative/complication geometry, matching native volumes, faithful schematics and models, and independent anatomical/clinical review. Selected figures do not supply dynamic compression, calibrated Doppler, complete source registration, current patient histology or treatment recommendations. Every new asset's anatomical review remains pending and structure/requirement coverage remains empty. Whole-radiology readiness remains false.
