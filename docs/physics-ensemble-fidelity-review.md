## Physical and mathematical scope

**Canonical site.** Two distinct labelled states have energies 0 and ΔE in joules. There is one state at each energy for ΔE>0; at ΔE=0 their energies coincide. A large reservoir fixes positive absolute temperature T. With x=ΔE/(k_B T), Z₁=1+exp(−x), P₁=exp(−x)/Z₁, P₀=1/Z₁ and P₁/P₀=exp(−x). At the lesson's x=1, the population ratio is 0.3678794412 while the normalized upper probability is 0.2689414214. These are separate displayed quantities. Mean energy is ΔE P₁ and Gibbs entropy is S_G/k_B=ln Z₁+xP₁. These expressions follow the [primary MIT canonical two-level derivation](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/8-333-statistical-mechanics-i-statistical-mechanics-of-particles-fall-2013/89259a8fd183acfaec06922c88a6024b_MIT8_333F13_Lec14.pdf).

The exact SI k_B=1.380649×10⁻²³ J/K is used. The energy input uses zJ=10⁻²¹ J and readouts use joules. Controls expose T=1…1200 K and ΔE=0…min(100 zJ,40k_B T), with the current joint maximum shown. Changing T can clamp ΔE. Zero/infinite temperature limits are explained analytically and are not claimed as selectable finite-temperature results. Small positive populations and entropies are retained even when rounded graphics cannot resolve them.

**Distinguishable binary objects.** For N independent labelled coins or noninteracting two-level sites, selected upper/head count j has exact multiplicity Ω_j=C(N,j). The model calculates all N+1 count probabilities and retains exact `BigInt` counts through N=1024. Fair coins assign 2⁻ᴺ to every binary string; canonical sites assign exp(−jx)/Z₁ᴺ, so P(j)=Ω_j exp(−jx)/Z₁ᴺ. Independence and distinguishability are explicit; Bose/Fermi exchange, interactions and negative-temperature populations are outside the model. The [primary two-level state-counting notes](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/8-333-statistical-mechanics-i-statistical-mechanics-of-particles-fall-2013/0cb99318c2ed889316cdcff839759e8d_MIT8_333F13_Lec12.pdf) provide the exact binomial construction.

For N≤8, every labelled binary string is enumerated. Three coins give counts [1,3,3,1], eight strings and exactly three one-head strings; their full fair ensemble entropy is ln8. Four coins give [1,4,6,4,1]. At j=2, lnΩ_j=ln6, while full fair microstate entropy is ln16. For larger N, the diagram plots every integer j on a logarithmic probability scale, and the table explicitly lists selected/nearby counts, counts near the mean, and endpoints. Joining segments aid viewing and do not introduce fractional-count macrostates.

The conditional multiplicity entropy lnΩ_j, the whole microstate Gibbs entropy N h(P₁), and entropy H(j) of only the count distribution are named separately. The calculated identity N h(P₁)=H(j)+Σ_j P(j)lnΩ_j exposes information discarded by recording j alone. Ensemble likelihoods do not imply fractions of observation time without further dynamical/ergodic assumptions. Coin entropy is a dimensionless counting analogy, not assigned coin heat or temperature.

**Free expansion.** The boundary is an insulated rigid container with a dilute classical ideal gas and evacuated extra volume. Initial/final equilibrium states have Q=W_by=ΔU=ΔT=0, ΔS=Nk_B ln r and P_f/P_i=1/r, with r=V_f/V_i. Entropy follows the [primary ideal-gas endpoint relation](https://web.mit.edu/16.unified/www/FALL/thermodynamics/notes/node40.html); actual irreversible Q=0 is not substituted into a reversible ΔS=Q/T formula. Uniform independent final-equilibrium positions imply probability r⁻ᴺ for **all particles in one specified original region at one instant**. For r=2 this is 2⁻ᴺ, not the probability for either half. N=10^q, q=0…25, is retained as an exact finite integer. Underflowed decimals become unavailable (`null`), while positive probability, its symbolic expression and finite ln/log₁₀ values remain available and displayed. No finite probability is called impossible, and no waiting/recurrence time or expansion trajectory is invented. Ratio one is the unchanged-state endpoint.

**Two thermal blocks.** Rigid blocks in an isolated container have constant positive heat capacities C_A,C_B and positive internally uniform temperatures. Energy conservation gives T_f=(C_A T_A+C_B T_B)/(C_A+C_B). The selected fraction describes transferred energy toward equilibrium, not elapsed time. Q_A=−Q_B, and each entropy change is C_i ln(T_i/T_i,initial). The entropy integrals are reversible reference paths for the blocks; their actual contact at unequal temperatures is irreversible. Combined entropy is nonnegative, and is exactly zero for equal initial temperatures or zero transfer. Equal 100 J/K capacities at 600/300 K give T_f=450 K, transfers ±15000 J and ΔS_total≈11.77830357 J/K. Unequal capacities are solved by the same conserved-energy equation. Rates, gradients, phase changes and real material-dependent heat capacities are omitted.
