# Full head/neck tumour representation requirements

The general head/neck tumour reader is expanded to 88 source-qualified groups and 528 components, each requiring image, schematic and 3D evidence. The full scope includes actual mucosal/skin/sinonasal/thyroid or salivary primary sites and components; major/accessory/minor glands, ducts and interfaces; bone/cartilage/muscle/fat boundaries; acquired neural and vascular routes; airway/visceral connections; every actual node/group and other lesion; and treated or repaired tissue. Listed groups are a known floor and every actual site/side/variant must be instantiated. There is no invented stand-alone salivary module; the salivary requirements remain included in the actual tumour and neck-space readers.

Three unsupported negative presets are removed. Tissue signal, secretions, bone destruction/remodelling, contact, enhancement and displacement are source-dependent observations with a differential. They do not independently diagnose benignity, malignancy, histology, microscopic invasion, organism, flow or function. Perineural imaging suspicion and imaging ENE remain separate from supplied pathology. Formal stage needs the actual confirmed primary/subsite/tissue/assay context and an applicable named version; no generic category is assigned by this expansion.

The [existing RA tumour source](https://radiologyassistant.nl/head-neck/neck-masses/head-neck-tumors), salivary reviews [Part 1](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6251248/) and [Part 2](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6251244/), [source high-field parotid MRI study](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11719534/), and the existing cranial-nerve imaging overview provide reference context. Original XML publisher MD5 values and grants were checked. Both salivary reviews are CC BY-NC-SA 4.0; their graphics and full captions are not commercially repackaged. The parotid MRI study is CC BY 4.0, but its classifier does not become universal histology or a current-patient diagnostic guarantee. Source figure IDs/caption hashes are intake only.

The neck companion is explicitly partial orientation. It does not supply all native internal gland/nodule/node tissues, fascicles, walls or invasion interfaces. Static geometry cannot provide secretion, duct patency, vocal/swallowing/airway or neurological function. Every unacquired or microscopic boundary remains missing, rather than negative. No source model, photograph or selected case is automatically registered to a current patient.

Ninety-five targeted checks passed. The full all-radiology gate remains incomplete: 62 investigations require expansion; the known floor is 81,966 representation obligations and the full total remains unknown. No clinical/anatomical acceptance or source coverage is granted by a scope enumeration or software check.
