Source coordinate forms and preprocessing

The authors reoriented and bias-corrected MRI, registered/resampled T2 and face-cropped images. Masks were smoothed, eroded and cleaned by the authors. Primer retains the original files and all supplied sample values. These views are original source-index planes with dominant source-coded RAS directions; they are not anatomical reslices.
