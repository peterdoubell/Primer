## Scope and source review

- TNM tables provide imaging-relevant category anchors rather than an automatic prognostic stage-group calculator.
- Histology, grade, biomarkers, examination, cytology and operative/pathological findings are explicitly identified where required.
- Head/neck tables identify site-specific frameworks and include HPV-associated oropharyngeal T descriptors. Complete clinical N and stage-group protocols remain the linked current site-specific reference; pathological N criteria are not substituted.
- PI-RADS, BI-RADS, LI-RADS, VI-RADS, Bosniak and TI-RADS diagnostic assessment are distinguished from cancer stage.
- RECIST remains a response framework; CNS integrated diagnosis/grade is not converted into conventional TNM.
- Schematics are original simplified vector drawings, generated by tools/build_cancer_staging_illustrations.py. Publisher artwork has not been copied into those diagrams.
