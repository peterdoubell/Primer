# AIIS scope and the retained hip modality boundary

The hip FAI inventory now explicitly includes the inferior AIIS prominence and subspine bone surface already named by the reporting walkthrough. This is an additive bone-scope correction. The original labrum, cartilage and periarticular soft-tissue requirements, checklist entries, worksheet fields and conditions remain intact.

The new bone condition limits radiography to the projected extent actually visible. Full three-dimensional morphology requires suitable cross-sectional acquisition; MRI morphology is assessed only where its sequences/resolution support it. The new parts do not establish that CT or MRI was acquired, do not permit MRI soft-tissue claims from radiographs, and do not diagnose impingement or symptom causation from morphology alone.

The effective checklist still conditions labral/cartilage lesion assessment and periarticular soft-tissue evaluation on acquired MRI, preserving not-assessed states. The existing `modality_condition_required` record remains a documented protocol boundary. Its reviewed scope fingerprint is refreshed for the added bone targets after checking these conditions; no anatomy, representation, site-instantiation or clinical-approval gate is waived.

Source-scope evidence: `msk-hip-aiis-ankle-accessory-scope-audit-2026-09-30.md` and its exact original-study references. The full clinical/commercial goal remains incomplete.
