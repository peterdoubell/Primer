# Original BodyParts3D peripheral source collection

Seventy complete original OBJ objects were retained from the first-party version4.3 selector, representing 55 source ontology concepts in its lower-limb systemic-circulation collection. This grouping also includes adjacent epigastric/genital branches; they are retained as source-labelled objects, not reinterpreted as foot arteries. Original OBJ bytes are stored in lossless gzip containers with separate compressed-file and original-byte hashes; the complete source text, including whitespace, is recoverable exactly. Exact native coordinates, normals, indices, source headers, is-a/part-of mapping rows and licence evidence are preserved. ## Complete triangle contacts and requested-representation follow-up

All **70 original objects / 278,094 triangles** were audited with conservative AABB candidates and explicit triangle contacts. Exact-coincident-position indexing is analysis only; every original face and invalid-face identity remains source evidence, not welded or repaired output. The audit considered **2,947,017** conservative candidates and found **19,755** contacts beyond ordinary shared vertex/edge adjacency: **19,745 between objects / 10 within objects**, across 103 source-ID pairs. This is a numerical contact result, not vascular invasion, a correct anatomical junction, lumen continuity or clinical approval.

The ten within-object findings occur in **FJ2095** (four) and **FJ2204** (six), both source sets of plantar digital arteries. Every pair is an exact coincident triangle with opposite winding, forming an isolated two-face component by original indices. These closed-edge components do not enclose 3D vessel/tissue volume. They are retained, not pruned as presumed noise or replaced with new geometry. The complete source-object and magnified-original-face sheet was inspected; it illustrates why closed/manifold edge counts alone cannot certify source anatomy.

The largest between-object contact groups involve posterior tibial/peroneal source objects: **2,634 pairs left / 2,475 right**, concentrated at source-declared Z approximately 277.47–289.77. No independent tibioperoneal-trunk object, duplicated-common-stem explanation or actual biological lumen connection is approved from this observation. Twenty explicit major transition review targets, including dorsal/deep-plantar/lateral-plantar/arch, have overlapping exact bounding boxes but no exact shared source positions at their nearest vertices. Vertex distances of about 0.0002–0.3805 declared mm are **upper bounds**, not continuous surface gaps or patency measurements. Requested “trunk”/“deep femoral” groups overlap in their source members; the same native ID is not an extra independent artery or patient sample.

A direct first-party request for **FJ2127 / BP29028 / FMA20796**, version4.3, with `all_downloads=0`, returned the requested is-a representation named **Right deep femoral artery**. The earlier batch export had supplied BP23446/FMA20731 in a part-of context named right femoral trunk. Positions, normals, face indices and normal indices are **exactly equal** between responses; the original geometry text lines also agree. Both original headers/files are retained byte-for-byte in lossless gzip containers. This grounds the requested source leaf identity without rewriting the batch header or claiming independent patient laterality/anatomical validation. The catalogue's opposite-side filename remains a separate source inconsistency.

`all-original-source-contacts.json.gz` and its summary retain complete face-owner/contact evidence and hashes. `source-junction-proximity-review.json`, `source-contact-findings-review.json` and `FJ2127-requested-representation-review.json` keep numerical bounds, component findings and source-context identity separately. These new stages supersede the initial unperformed contact audit, not its pending clinical/MRI registration status. No native vascular tree is repaired, fused, credited as complete or promoted to runtime.
