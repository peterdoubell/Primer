but its commercial redistribution conclusion was incomplete. It checked the Zenodo

agreement of the ISLES'22 team.” No such agreement is evidenced.

source snapshot and derived mask geometry must stay offline and unbound from public

an explicit source-publication NO-GO, not a new anatomical or clinical approval.
