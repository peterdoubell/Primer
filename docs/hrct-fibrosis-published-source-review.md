# Original HRCT fibrosis source figures

Six complete original CT figures1/3/4/5/6/7 from Larici et al., *ESR Essentials: imaging in fibrotic lung diseases—practice recommendations by the European Society of Thoracic Imaging*, DOI10.1007/s00330-024-11054-2 (online2024, volume2025), are preserved unchanged. Original metadata/XML/media MD5 and file/pixel SHA bind the figures to their original grant. The full article license is CC BY4.0. Every complete image and caption was inspected; no separate figure credit was found. Figure2 is a diagnostic flowchart rather than tissue anatomy and is not adopted as a reader classifier.

Actual lowercase original letters and source case groups are preserved. Figure1 provides different honeycomb/traction/reticulation examples. Figure3 a/bUIP,c/dprobableUIP,e/fNSIP represent distinct examples; UIP pattern is not an independently established idiopathic diagnosis. Figure4 a-csarcoidosis,d-fHP remain distinct. Mosaic/three-density/source air-trapping labels do not supply an acquired expiration or measured perfusion. Figure5 a-cbaseline,d-f13months and figure6 a-cbaseline,d-f15months preserve actual source exposure/systemic-sclerosis history and multidisciplinary/PPF/treatment labels. These intervals and selected stills alone do not prove the named2022within-one-year PPF definition, causal diagnosis, physiological trend or a new treatment recommendation. Figure7 baselinea-c,15monthacute studyd-f,40dayslaterg-i remains one source sequence; newopacity is not uniquely exacerbation and microscopic alveolar rupture is not directly imaged. No whole-series registration or quantitative progression is invented.

The reporting guide removes four normal assertions, including an unperformed expiratory-negative statement and an unsupported stable-prior statement. Reports now require actual source acquisition, full covered pattern/distribution/tissue interfaces, fibrosis versus mimics, acquired respiratory phases and dated comparison/clinical/PFT sources. The named2022PPF definition is separated from radiological change. Primary2025ERS/ATS terminology recognises BIP/AMP and secondary aetiologies; source pattern confidence and multidisciplinary diagnosis remain distinct. Whole parent thorax geometry is explicitly partial, with no lobular/microscopic/lesion/functional coverage granted.

The original Breathe2024 review PMC11091715 is CC BY-NC4 and its pictures are not commercially reused. The named2022guideline and2025statement are definition/terminology sources; their figures are not copied under this change. The newly packaged figures do not establish complete native series, actual microscopic walls, all disease/branch/secondary-lobule geometry, calibrated burden or independent clinical approval. Every source-structure binding remains pending.
