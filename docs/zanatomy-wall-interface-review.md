A conservative AABB sweep avoids the large-cell expansion of coarse fascia polygons. An independent brute-force overlap comparison checks exact boundary touches and long thin boxes. The full source analysis has 3,566,005 candidate pairs: 3,179,614 explicitly tested and 386,391 shared-edge pairs resolved analytically. Comparison tolerance is 10⁻⁹ mm, which is a numerical parameter, not source acquisition resolution or biological accuracy. Candidate-array identity and all retained contact points/source face identities are recorded in losslessly compressed evidence.
