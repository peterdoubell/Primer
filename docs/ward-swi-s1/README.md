# Ward S1 vein mask and susceptibility MRI intake

The [Monash dataset](https://doi.org/10.4225/03/57C1698020D1D) releases four volumes under CC0. The acquisition receipt records their original Figshare lengths and hashes. Ward and colleagues describe tracing veins in native GRE space and editing against both SWI and QSM in all three planes. [Published methods](https://users.monash.edu.au/~dld/Publications/2018/WardFerrisRanigaDoweNgBarnesEgan2018CombiningImagesAndAnatomicalKnowledgeToImproveAutomatedVeinSegmentationInMRI_NeuroImage165_2018_pp294-305.pdf)

The four source arrays have identical 232 × 256 × 160 dimensions. SWI, QSM and the dilated brain mask share the image sform. The vein mask declares a 1 mm diagonal sform with a 1 mm translation along each axis. Its sform disagrees with the approximately 0.9 mm image grid. All four qform codes are zero.

At matching voxel indices, all 187,819 vein voxels lie inside the released brain mask. Applying the sforms with nearest-neighbour sampling places zero of the 27,054 remaining positive samples inside the brain mask. Matching-index veins have a median SWI intensity of 164. The one-voxel in-brain rim with nonzero SWI has a median of 255. The three-plane contact sheet shows corresponding dark structures. Taken together with the paper, this supports same-index display as a documented interpretation. The original mask header is preserved. The audit does not establish an author-confirmed correction, named vessel segmentation, or patient-specific thrombosis.

The 3D viewer surface represents every internal interface between positive and zero voxels with two outward-facing triangles. It preserves voxel boundaries without resampling or smoothing. It leaves acquired grid boundaries open. Exact voxel edge and corner contacts can produce non-manifold topology. The viewer builds these faces directly from the verified original mask and applies the companion scan spacing for display under the documented same-index interpretation. It can section the surface at the linked sagittal, coronal or axial cursor while keeping either side. Sectioning affects visibility without editing the source geometry.

The reader serves the original SWI, QSM and tracing files byte for byte. It parses their NIfTI storage without resampling. Windowing and the tracing overlay affect display only. The cursor reports image-header coordinates and the source sample value. The fourth volume, the dilated brain mask, is used for the alignment comparison. Its source identity is recorded in the acquisition receipt.

The reader opens from the dural atlas and preserves the originating reporting reference on return. The alignment choice applies only to the companion Ward volumes. Named atlas meshes have independent provenance.
