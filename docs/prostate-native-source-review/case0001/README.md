# Original prostate MRI, source surfaces and derived masks

This is an offline source intake for Prostate-MRI-US-Biopsy-0001, a source 64-year-old male case. The acquired 106 original DICOM objects total 70,123,440 bytes. Public IDC whole-object MD5/ETag and independent SHA256 readbacks are retained; immutable cloud versions are not claimed. Source collection permissions explicitly allow CC BY 4.0 reuse. All 71,479,680 scalar samples independently decode exactly against pydicom, with no voxel resampling, high-bit alteration or invented geometry.

The source includes 60 original T2 images at 0.6640625 × 0.6640625 × 1.5 mm sampling, 20 producer-derived ADC and 20 calculated-DWI images on separate 1.625 × 1.625 × approximately 3.6 mm grids, two source STL encapsulations, two producer-derived binary segmentations and two ultrasound volumes. Native MRI positions, direction cosines, complete frame order and original DICOM display windows are retained. Sampling does not establish effective fine-tissue resolution. No physical ADC units, rescale calibration, DCE, injection clock or native acquired high-b source series is supplied by this selected case package. Source private diffusion values are preserved as metadata, not automatically applied as physical ADC units or current scoring evidence.

The prostate outline has all 1,198 source faces; the suspicious target ROI has all 4,280. Both are single closed components with no boundary/nonmanifold edges or zero-area faces in the exact coordinate-adjacency analysis. Original STL headers, every ordered float32 face corner, normals and attributes remain unchanged. The original STL coordinate header does not specify SPACE; matching DICOM frame-of-reference identifiers and the source conversion pipeline are evidence of association, not independent clinical/fine-anatomical registration approval. No axis flip, fitting, cap, smoothing or component deletion was performed.

The source prostate outline was made semi-automatically with Profuse, with user-adjustable contours/vertices. Its accuracy is user-dependent. The MRI target was drawn by a radiologist as a suspicious biopsy ROI, which is not a proven complete tumour or histological boundary. Source Likert-like scores must not become current PI-RADS v2.1 assignments. The derived masks were rasterized from source STL by Slicer; they are not independent native manual voxel segmentations. Both retain all 60 exact T2 SOP references, with a maximum source plane-position difference of about 0.00000145 mm. That numeric correspondence is not anatomical accuracy.

The two US volumes each have 227 × 360 × 360 byte samples. Their private Eigen voxel-size values differ (0.356 and 0.178), while their standard in-plane pixel-spacing values are both 0.356. Standard patient orientation/position are absent. The publisher applies a US axis permutation and describes nonrigid MRI-to-US ROI registration with average accuracy of 3–4 mm. No standard axes, isotropic calibration, new biopsy correspondence or native MRI/US registration is invented here.

The four QA plots show every source face in six views, selected complete original T2 planes and derived source-mask correspondence, and selected US source planes. MRI windows follow original DICOM metadata; colours/contour overlays are illustrative. All source arrays remain unchanged. Highest fine-anatomical boundary quality, zonal and fascial layers, ducts, neurovascular routes, seminal vesicles, sphincter, nodes, extension and every required reporting structure still need explicit source coverage and independent review.

The original source gland/ROI surfaces and complete three-grid MRI are now packaged as partial reader references. The coordinate-correspondence review checks every source voxel centre without fitting;480 gland and5 ROI centres differ from the producer masks, with maximum source-coordinate distances0.1275 and0.02791 from original faces. Original masks and faces remain. The source model unit-code defect is retained; inferred display scale follows the MRI/SEG grid, and no full DICOM conformance or independent clinical registration is claimed. The107-group reporting inventory and all41 idealized localization regions remain a draft with unapproved fine anatomy. The automatic AIMI segmentation is not substituted.
