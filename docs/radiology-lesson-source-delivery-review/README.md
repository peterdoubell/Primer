# Direct original-source figures in radiology lessons

Forty-nine radiology lessons already had matching reporting readers with locally preserved original atlas figures. Previously only the rectal lesson exposed its atlas through the lesson source-gallery control. This change attaches the existing source readers directly to all 49 eligible lessons: 48 new lesson attachments and 729 scoped source references.

The attachment uses the existing investigation-to-module mapping and preserves the authored rectal gallery. Original illustrations, 3D companions, assessment content and reporting text remain intact. Each investigation retains its separate source group, figure order, modality and ancillary evidence roles, full captions, attribution and source case context. No source image or mesh byte changes. The gallery remains on demand; pictures are not fetched into the gallery before the reader requests its sources.

The runtime rights audit now records the same source uses on every matching lesson surface. Historical prostate teaching review remains bound to its original contract, with the additive source-gallery change recorded separately by before/after hashes. This does not refresh old anatomical or clinical approval by assumption.

An isolated fake-profile browser check visited all 49 lessons, loaded the complete matching source groups and checked each at a 390-pixel viewport. The largest collection decoded all 107 images and retained its mixed CT/MRI/ultrasound and schematic labels. No browser errors were observed. Authenticated production lesson interaction was not tested.

Fifty-seven radiology lessons have no registered local source atlas and receive no substituted case or filler gallery. Those delivery gaps, incomplete reported anatomy and native 3D coverage remain outstanding. Figure availability and rights verification do not establish full clinical/commercial grade or whole-module anatomical approval.
