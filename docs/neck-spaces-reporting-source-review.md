# Supra/infrahyoid neck representation scope

The combined neck-space MRI investigation is expanded to 107 groups and 642 components, each requiring image, schematic and 3D evidence. It retains actual supra/infrahyoid compartments, source contents and connections, fascial conventions, glands/ducts, muscles, vascular/neural routes, skull-base/visceral/airway interfaces, every acquired node and lesion/complication relation. Every actual site/side/variant must be independently instantiated; listed groups are a known floor. A potential-space definition or generic model line does not prove a directly resolved thin fascial boundary.

Primary context includes the existing [RA infrahyoid reporting source](https://radiologyassistant.nl/head-neck/infrahyoid-neck/MR-anatomy-and-pathology), [source index](https://radiologyassistant.nl/head-neck/infrahyoid-neck), [retropharyngeal/prevertebral imaging review](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3994542/), [carotid-space review](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6377693/) and [cervical-fascia terminology study](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7309289/). Original XML checksums and source permissions were checked. The latter source is CC BY-NC, the retropharyngeal source has a text/data-mining grant, and the carotid-space source is CC BY; no grant is borrowed across papers. Graphics/full captions are not repackaged by this scope change. Two direct source endpoints returned unavailable/browser-check pages; those responses are not represented as inspected PDFs or full articles.

Two unsupported normal presets are removed. Source acquisition/evaluability, compartment convention, retained landmarks and uncertainty are required. Vascular displacement and bifurcation splaying support a source-localised differential rather than a unique tumour diagnosis. Contact, loss of fat plane, signal and enhancement do not independently prove invasion, histology, organism, drainability, thrombosis, vessel patency or airway/swallowing function. Appropriate actual imaging/clinical evidence remains separate.

The neck companion is explicitly partial compartment orientation. It does not supply each native fascia, node cortex/hilum, small nerve/vessel, wall or pathological interface. Photography and segmented source labels remain distinct from current-patient MRI. All source branches, thin or microscopic unresolved components, variants and uncovered extent remain required, without clinical coverage being granted.

Ninety-five scope/reporting/role/hosted checks passed. The full fidelity gate still fails: 63 investigations are unexpanded; the known floor is 80,382 image/schematic/model obligations and the total remains unknown. This is a reporting/scope correction, not clinical certification or anatomical approval.
