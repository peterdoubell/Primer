# Original LumASe schema follow-up — 30 September 2026

The original values **1–7 remain anatomically unnamed**. A newly located public author paper strengthens source provenance and annotation-process evidence, but does not establish a numeric dictionary.

[The official publisher PDF](https://xk.sia.cn/en/article/pdf/preview/10.13976/j.cnki.xk.2024.0643.pdf), DOI `10.13976/j.cnki.xk.2024.0643`, is a 2025 *Information and Control* paper by Yingdi Zhang, Zelin Shi and colleagues. Section 2.1 on page 395 explicitly links [the original Zenodo record 7181338](https://zenodo.org/records/7181338). It describes 663 L1–L5 crops, voxel annotation by three physicians, subsequent senior review and training/validation/test counts of 545/58/60. This establishes a direct dataset connection, rather than relying only on matching titles or author names.

Figure 5 on page 395 names the seven anatomical regions and associates them with display colors. It gives no NIfTI mask values. The prose enumerates SAP, VB, TP, L, P, SP and IAP; the figure legend enumerates IAP, L, P, SAP, SP, TP and VB. Neither order is identified as the numeric schema. Figure 6 on page 397 provides color-based qualitative comparisons, also without numeric values. The full PDF text and these relevant rendered pages were inspected. The publisher metadata lists no supplements or appendices.

**Decision:** retain numeric region names, original voxels and open crop boundaries. An original label configuration or explicit numeric dictionary is still required before anatomical renaming. Senior review reported by the authors is not a new clinical approval of our case, extraction or display. After the schema is established, native CT correspondence, annotation accuracy, bilateral scope and crop endpoints still require review.
