# Paediatric elbow radiography

Investigation: `ra.paediatric-elbow-fractures`

## Reporting obligations

- **Elbow alignment:** Assess radiocapitellar and ulnohumeral congruity and the anterior humeral line, accounting for age, ossification and projection.
- **Ossification and growth plates:** Identify visible capitellar, radial-head, medial-epicondylar, trochlear, olecranon and lateral-epicondylar centres and their physes; distinguish normal developmental appearance from injury.
- **Fracture map and displacement:** Inspect the supracondylar region, condyles/epicondyles, proximal radius and ulna. Describe displacement, angulation, rotation and articular or physeal extension.
- **Effusion and soft tissues:** Assess anterior/posterior fat pads, periarticular swelling and visible soft-tissue abnormality; a fracture may remain occult.
- **Associated injury and reduction:** Search for dislocation or an entrapped fragment, review included forearm injury, and describe reduction/fixation when present. Radiographs do not establish normal clinical neurovascular status.
