# MRI ankle

Investigation: `ra.mri-ankle`

## Reporting obligations

- **Bones And Osteochondral Surfaces:** Inspect talar dome, tibial plafond and hindfoot for contusion, fracture and osteochondral injury.
- **Lateral Ligaments And Syndesmosis:** Trace ATFL, CFL, PTFL and anterior/posterior syndesmotic structures.
- **Medial Ligaments And Sinus Tarsi:** Assess superficial/deep deltoid, spring ligament and sinus tarsi structures.
- **Tendons:** Follow flexor, extensor, peroneal and Achilles compartments through their visible attachments.
- **Joints And Soft Tissues:** Assess effusions, capsule, impingement sites, bursae and any mass.
