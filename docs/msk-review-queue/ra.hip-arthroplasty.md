# Hip arthroplasty radiography

Investigation: `ra.hip-arthroplasty`

## Reporting obligations

- **Component Position:** Describe acetabular and femoral component position and change.
- **Interfaces:** Assess lucencies and osteolysis with named zones and serial change.
- **Bearing And Hardware:** Look for eccentric head position, liner wear and hardware breakage.
- **Bone And Alignment:** Assess periprosthetic fracture, bone stock and dislocation.
- **Soft Tissues And Comparison:** Describe soft-tissue findings and whether the pattern raises concern for infection or another complication.
