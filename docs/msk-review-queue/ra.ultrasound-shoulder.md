# Ultrasound shoulder

Investigation: `ra.ultrasound-shoulder`

## Reporting obligations

- **Long Head Of Biceps:** Follow the tendon in the groove and rotator interval; assess stability during rotation.
- **Rotator Cuff:** Assess subscapularis, supraspinatus and infraspinatus; describe tear surface, thickness and dimensions.
- **Bursa And Dynamic Assessment:** Assess subacromial-subdeltoid bursal distension and tendon/bursal movement.
- **Joint And Acromioclavicular Joint:** Review accessible posterior joint recess and AC joint with focal symptom correlation.
- **Muscles And Limitations:** Assess visible cuff bulk and any paralabral cyst; state the deep structures not evaluated reliably.
