## Reporting obligations

- **Distribution:** Map involved muscles and whether change follows a nerve, compartment or systemic pattern.
- **Oedema:** Describe fluid-sensitive signal and focal versus diffuse muscle involvement.
- **Fatty Change And Atrophy:** Use T1 to distinguish chronic fatty replacement from active oedema.
- **Collection Or Mass:** Identify focal lesions, blood products and enhancement if acquired.
- **Nerves And Associated Structures:** Inspect visible nerves, entrapment sites, tendons and bone for a cause.
