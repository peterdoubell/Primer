# Wrist radiography — carpal instability

Investigation: `ra.wrist-instability`

## Reporting obligations

- **Carpal Arcs:** Trace all three Gilula arcs and assess carpal joint spaces.
- **Lateral Alignment:** Assess radius-lunate-capitate relationship on a true lateral view.
- **Carpal Angles:** Measure axes using stated landmarks and describe DISI/VISI pattern where supported.
- **Bones:** Inspect for associated carpal or distal forearm fracture and chronic collapse.
- **Degenerative And Dynamic Findings:** Map arthrosis and compare neutral with stress views.
