# MRI shoulder

Investigation: `ra.mri-shoulder`

## Reporting obligations

- **Cuff:** Name involved tendon, tear surface/depth, AP extent and retraction.
- **Muscle:** Describe atrophy and fatty infiltration for each affected cuff muscle.
- **Biceps:** Review anchor, pulley, intra-articular segment and groove position.
- **Labrum/instability:** Map labral injury, capsuloligamentous findings and glenoid/humeral defects.
- **Joint/outlet:** Assess cartilage, bursae, AC joint and associated osseous abnormality.
