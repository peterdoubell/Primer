# Ultrasound-guided joint injections

Investigation: `ra.ultrasound-joint-injection`

## Reporting obligations

- **Target And Preprocedure Survey:** Confirm the named joint or bursa and assess fluid, anatomy and access.
- **Technique:** Document patient position, sterile preparation and needle approach.
- **Needle Tip And Delivery:** Confirm tip location and observed spread in the intended compartment.
- **Aspiration If Performed:** Record aspirated amount and appearance and specimens actually submitted.
- **Outcome And Aftercare:** Describe tolerance, immediate complication and documented aftercare.
