# Ankle fracture radiography

Investigation: `ra.ankle-fractures`

## Reporting obligations

- **Fractures and mortise:** Inspect the medial, lateral and posterior malleoli, distal tibia and talus; describe fibular level, fracture morphology/displacement and tibiotalar alignment.
- **Syndesmosis and proximal fibula:** Assess tibiofibular alignment, clear spaces/overlap and syndesmotic avulsion fragments as radiographic injury clues. State proximal-fibular coverage and concern for a higher fibular injury.
- **Osseous articular surfaces:** Describe visible talar-dome/plafond defects, impaction, articular step/gap and posterior-malleolar extent. Separate osseous findings from direct cartilage assessment on supplemental imaging.
- **Foot and hindfoot when included:** For the clinically indicated region actually imaged, assess hindfoot and tarsometatarsal alignment, navicular, cuneiforms/cuboid and metatarsals. State foot or hindfoot coverage not obtained.
- **Soft tissues and modality limits:** Describe swelling and radiographic avulsion/alignment clues. Do not grade tendon or ligament discontinuity on radiographs; name direct integrity findings only from an appropriate acquired MRI or ultrasound examination.
