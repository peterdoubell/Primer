# Stress fracture imaging

Investigation: `ra.stress-fractures`

## Reporting obligations

- **Site And Side:** Identify the exact bone and surface, including tension versus compression side where applicable.
- **Fracture Line:** Look for a cortical or trabecular fracture line and displacement.
- **Marrow And Periosteum:** Separate fluid-sensitive oedema from T1 marrow abnormality and periosteal reaction.
- **Articular And Soft Tissues:** Assess joint surface, associated collapse and adjacent tissue.
- **Comparison And Differential:** Compare evolution and consider insufficiency, infection or another lesion when atypical.
