# MRI elbow

Investigation: `ra.mri-elbow`

## Reporting obligations

- **Tendons:** Assess common flexor/extensor origins at the epicondyles and the distal biceps, brachialis and triceps through their covered course and bony attachments.
- **Ligaments:** Name UCL and lateral collateral complex components and tear sites.
- **Osteochondral surface:** Inspect capitellum/trochlea, marrow and loose bodies.
- **Nerves:** Trace ulnar, median and radial branches with secondary denervation.
- **Joint mechanics:** Review alignment, impingement features, effusion and postoperative structures.
