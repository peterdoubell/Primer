# MRI knee

Investigation: `ra.mri-knee`

## Reporting obligations

- **Menisci:** Inspect both menisci, roots and meniscocapsular attachments for tear and displaced fragments.
- **Cruciates/collaterals:** Assess continuity, tear location and associated avulsion.
- **Cartilage and bone:** Map chondral loss, osteochondral lesions, contusions and subchondral fracture.
- **Extensor mechanism:** Review quadriceps/patellar tendons and patellofemoral stabilisers.
- **Joint and corners:** Assess effusion, loose bodies, posteromedial/lateral structures and cysts.
