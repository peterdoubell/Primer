# MRI diabetic foot

Investigation: `ra.mri-diabetic-foot`

## Reporting obligations

- **Ulcer And Sinus Tract:** Localise the skin defect and trace any contiguous route to bone or joint.
- **Bone Marrow:** Separate confluent T1 marrow replacement from isolated fluid-sensitive oedema.
- **Joints And Neuroarthropathy:** Describe joint-centred distribution, fragmentation, subluxation and deformity.
- **Collections And Viability:** Assess fluid collections, rim enhancement when available, and nonenhancing tissue.
- **Tendons And Other Soft Tissues:** Review tendon sheaths, muscles and spread across fascial spaces.
