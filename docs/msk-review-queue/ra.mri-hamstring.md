# MRI hamstring injury

Investigation: `ra.mri-hamstring`

## Reporting obligations

- **Tendon Identity:** Name semimembranosus, semitendinosus and biceps femoris components separately.
- **Tear Type And Location:** Assess partial-thickness origin injury, full-thickness avulsion and musculotendinous injury.
- **Retraction:** Measure each avulsed tendon from its footprint and describe the distal stump.
- **Muscle And Haematoma:** Map muscle oedema, structural fibre loss and haematoma.
- **Sciatic Nerve And Associated Findings:** Assess the sciatic nerve relationship and adjacent bone or soft-tissue changes.
