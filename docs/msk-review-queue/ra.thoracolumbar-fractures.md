# CT and MRI thoracolumbar fractures

Investigation: `ra.thoracolumbar-fractures`

## Reporting obligations

- **Numbering and traumatic alignment:** Name the vertebra or motion segment and assess translation, rotation, distraction and focal angulation; document numbering and coverage limits.
- **Vertebral fracture morphology:** Map vertebral-body walls, one or both endplates and each posterior element. Distinguish compression/burst morphology, posterior-wall retropulsion and a transosseous distraction pattern. Note relevant bone or marrow abnormality and fracture-age evidence using the acquired modality and comparison.
- **Posterior tension band:** Assess spinous/interspinous and facet relationships on CT. On acquired MRI, assess supraspinous and interspinous ligaments, ligamenta flava and facet capsules separately; distinguish definite disruption from indeterminate oedema.
- **Discs and longitudinal ligaments:** Assess traumatic disc-space widening or disruption, endplate interfaces and anterior/posterior longitudinal ligaments with the available modality, including anterior distraction in a rigid spine.
- **Canal, epidural and neural structures:** Localise canal, lateral-recess or foraminal encroachment and epidural abnormality. On acquired MRI, describe cord, conus, cauda equina and named root compression or signal abnormality within coverage.
- **Paraspinal and associated injury:** Describe paraspinal or prevertebral soft-tissue injury, additional included fractures, postoperative limitations and other relevant visible abnormalities.
- **Classification inputs and clinical neurology:** Keep imaging morphology and posterior ligamentous complex assessment separate from supplied clinical neurological status. Name the applicable AO/TLICS assessment and document missing clinical information.
