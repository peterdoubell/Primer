# MRI muscle injury

Investigation: `ra.mri-muscle-injury`

## Reporting obligations

- **Injured Unit:** Name muscle, side and compartment, including adjacent involved muscles.
- **Fibre Disruption:** Separate oedema without definite discontinuity from partial or complete structural disruption.
- **Tendon And Aponeurosis:** Assess central tendon, aponeurosis and origin/insertion.
- **Oedema And Haematoma:** Describe extent and compartment of oedema and blood products.
- **Associated Findings:** Look for neurovascular proximity, contusion, scar and alternative explanations.
