# Coronary CT fidelity scope and reader corrections

The effective `ra.ct-coronary` reporting contract is expanded into 181 draft anatomy/finding requirements and 543 image/schematic/model obligations. Exact reporting checklist fields, templates, walkthroughs and linked reading remain hashed against the current application. All five checklist fields and all seven walkthrough steps remain present. This is a draft scope, not clinical approval or a complete denominator for every patient variant and acquired finding.

The inventory distinguishes coronary trunks/segments, visible lumen and wall boundaries, variable branches, root/ostial context, dominance relationships, anomaly/bridge/fistula sites, plaque and obstruction, stents/grafts/anastomoses, cardiac structures, myocardial localisation regions and the acquired extracardiac field. Known named findings include valve calcification, pericardial effusion, pulmonary nodule/embolus and intracardiac thrombus. Every branch, lesion, intervention, variant and acquired organ/finding still needs case-specific instantiation. Generic parent labels do not close that scope.

The linked [coronary anatomy](https://radiologyassistant.nl/cardiovascular/anatomy/coronary-anatomy-and-anomalies), [CAD-RADS reading](https://radiologyassistant.nl/cardiovascular/cad-rads/coronary-artery-disease-reporting-and-data-system) and [CAD-RADS 2.0 consensus](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9627235/) were consulted for scope. This review grants no image reuse rights. Embedded labels, segment conventions, conditional pathology and complete source/guide boundaries remain unresolved. No fixed 16/18-segment denominator is inferred from the draft groups.

Calcium scoring, calibrated lumen/lesion measurements, plaque assessment, CT-FFR/perfusion and dynamic/chamber findings have separate evidence obligations. Contrast/phase, artefact and diagnostic quality cannot be inferred from a static model. Routine CT-visible morphology does not automatically establish histological fine layers or cap dimensions. Normal anatomy cannot supply positive lesion-specific evidence.

The current BodyParts3D coronary region has nine named meshes: gross ventricular and right/left atrial walls, ascending aorta, left/right coronary trunks, LAD trunk, circumflex and an RCA posterior interventricular branch. It does not independently define every reportable branch, lumen, plaque, intervention, valve, pericardial layer or extracardiac structure. Seven reader anatomy notes now state those limits at the relevant steps, without dropping any reporting target.

The normal prefill previously assigned right dominance. It now retains the case choices and PDA origin as prompts; the example circulation cannot establish patient dominance. All other reporting fields and classification requirements remain preserved.

Reader figure assignments were also corrected to match the existing source captions. The stent example (`ccta-img-5`) no longer illustrates the anatomy/anomaly step and is shown with stent/physiology review. The napkin-ring plaque-detail figure (`ccta-img-7`) is shown in plaque review rather than being presented as quantitative stenosis evidence. Their labels now describe the actual subjects. Required anomalous-course and orthogonal lumen-measurement images remain required in the patient key-image checklist; they are not waived because an appropriate source figure is missing.

Seventy-five relevant tests passed, including canonical data formatting, complete walkthrough/catalogue checks, scope hashes, no generic-parent coverage credit, CT/pathology context discrimination and figure/field preservation. Browser checks confirmed the case-specific dominance prompt, visible model-scope note and the updated plaque figure/label; runtime errors were empty. Screenshots and `browser-checks.json` are in `coronary-fidelity-scope-review/`. Owned QA processes were stopped.

The complete total remains unknown, and all 543 new coronary obligations remain missing. No clinical/commercial readiness or source representation approval is claimed. These changes remain local and undeployed.
